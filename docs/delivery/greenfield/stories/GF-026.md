# GF-026 — Qualified local file artifact provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Qualify one local-file realization for immutable artifacts under GF-013's protected/disposable
routing contract. It provides provider evidence for GF-023/GF-024, never a new artifact semantic,
effect authority, Run, or dispatch path.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`RT-EVIDENCE` reaches the provider through `PORT-ARTIFACT`. `CB-STORE` binds each Operation to the
admitted artifact-provider manifest/resource scope, existing protected-or-disposable holder
context, exact artifact subject/digest, and mode. `CP-MEDIATOR` validates the complete binding and
`CP-TRANSITION` alone adopts or retires the returned fact and advances the witnessed lookup head.
The binding cannot cross artifact/resource scope, substitute a holder class, alias protected and
disposable contexts, turn release into deletion, or access ledger/registry resources directly.
`OPC-ART-PUT` creates/verifies bytes and the required pin set, `OPC-ART-GET` reads verified bytes, and `OPC-ART-DISPOSE` is mode-bound;
all results are `EV-ARTIFACT-FACT`. `LG-WITNESS` and `RT-WITNESS` provide flush-before-witness-
before-ack currency. `CF-MECH-ARTIFACT` and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` prove the realization; `DR-5`/
`DR-6` constrain selection. `PC-JIG-15`, `PC-JIG-16`, `PC-CONCEPTS-5`, and `PC-USE-5` map exact
replaceability/proof routes. `STACK-1`, `STACK-2`, `STACK-3`, `STACK-4`, `STACK-5`, `STACK-6`,
and `STACK-7` require portable, swappable, attested, authority-contained compatible provision.
`FC-MECHANISM`, `FC-EFFECT`, and `FC-TRUST` distinguish a bounded mechanism fault, uncertain
artifact mutation, and broken witnessed continuity. `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, `BND-RETRY`,
`BND-WAIT-LEDGER`, and `BND-RECOVERY` bound response, attempts, witness acknowledgement, and
uncertain-effect reconciliation respectively.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

GF-013 is the `semantic-to-provider` implementation edge and GF-022 is the manifest/admission
evidence edge. Both must be merged/current in the externally recorded execution base/tuple with exact provider/build/
resource/witness basis. A successful file write or unbound manifest does not qualify the provider.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is local-file protected/disposable routing, holder/pin behavior, witness ordering, restore,
and disposal uncertainty proof. New evidence semantics, acceptance, source/ledger behavior,
configuration policy, byte disposal authority, and runtime outcome changes are excluded.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The provider serves `RT-EVIDENCE` through `PORT-ARTIFACT` and returns only validated
`EV-ARTIFACT-FACT` facts after `CP-MEDIATOR` validates the exact `CB-STORE`. The five protected pre-Run holder classes use the protected context: no
move, alias, or disposal. The six disposable evidence holder classes use the disposable context and
the exact two-pin routing/index contract. Only the owning mediator/controller may authorize an
Operation; provider cannot approve, transition, decide, publish, finalize, create a Run, dispatch,
or use one subject/resource binding for another. Witness advancement belongs to the fixed
ledger-mediated ordering, not the artifact port.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a current exact provider proof that protected addresses stay protected and disposable
put/get/release routes preserve the required pin/witness behavior. Mismatch, uncertain disposal,
restore/currency failure, stale proof, or bad pin leaves the provider unavailable; it is not a
Story outcome and creates no runtime authority, acceptance, or landing.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

`FC-INPUT` rejects malformed/noncanonical bytes, digest, mode, holder, and pin tuples;
`FC-SUBJECT` rejects the wrong artifact, operation, provider/resource scope, or holder basis;
`FC-FENCE` rejects stale operation, manifest, generation, or witnessed head; `FC-AUTHORITY` rejects
unauthorized disposal and every protected move/alias/dispose request; and `FC-EVIDENCE` rejects
missing or integrity-invalid gate, receipt, or artifact proof. A confirmed port fault or timeout
before mutation uncertainty is `FC-MECHANISM`; a lost or unverifiable result after dispatch is
`FC-EFFECT`; and a behind, forked, mismatched, missing, or unverifiable lookup/witness chain is
`FC-TRUST` and halts adoption, release, disposal, and autonomous recovery.

`CP-MEDIATOR` owns `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`: it starts with the durable Operation request/deadline,
uses the 15-minute default within 5 seconds–2 hours, and wakes only on a validated
`EV-ARTIFACT-FACT`, failure, or certainty observation. It never renews; a proved-absent attempt may
retry only under `BND-RETRY`, whose three-attempt default and 1–5 range never reset and whose every
attempt is recorded. Exhaustion blocks the owning Story/Operation only when no effect uncertainty
remains. The transition commit protocol owns `BND-WAIT-LEDGER`: it starts with the exact disposable
lookup/witness head, uses the 30-second default within 1 second–5 minutes, wakes only on verified
acknowledgement/readback, never renews, and on exhaustion halts dispatch and enters Recovery.

Intent precedes any `OPC-ART-PUT` or `OPC-ART-DISPOSE`. An `FC-EFFECT` result starts
`BND-RECOVERY` under `CP-RECOVERY` with the retained exact operation/fence; its three-attempt
default and 1–5 range never renew and never permit a semantic-effect retry. It wakes only on
`EV-RECOVERY-OBSERVATION` or `EV-EFFECT-CERTAINTY`; exhaustion escalates/parks while retaining the
fence and conservative pin. Reconcile by exact operation identity, mode, digest, and complete pin
set. Uncertain release preserves the pin; uncertain byte disposal permits neither another disposal
nor adoption. Restore verifies chain/head against independent witness before disposable adoption,
release, disposal, or resume. Protected context remains non-disposable, and no cleanup can weaken
those guarantees.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical digest, context/resource scope, holder class, full pin tuples, operation mode,
and witnessed currency. Resolve local credentials in memory only; redact path/bytes/secrets from
logs/evidence. A protected address may never alias disposable storage. Provider facts, fallback, or
local configuration cannot grant authority; construction guard makes the provider unreachable until
its exact gate and manifest are current.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver one local-file artifact realization, scoped configuration guard, protected/disposable/pin
fault corpus, restore/disposal reconciliation evidence, and gate record. `DR-5` owner
**Configuration** selects it after proof; `DR-6` owner **Engineering** realizes physical storage
under immutable routing/pin/witness contracts. Unavailable is fallback; Arye owns any widening.
No package path or new storage/cleanup rule is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run protected five-class no-move/no-alias/no-dispose probes; disposable six-class routing and
two-pin set probes; put/get/release/dispose mode, witness ordering, lost ack/reconcile, crash,
rollback/restore, uncertain disposal/preservation, manifest gate, and negative-authority tests for
cross-resource, cross-subject, cross-holder-context, protected/disposable alias, release-as-delete,
and direct ledger/registry access.
Record provider/build/resource/witness, artifact/pin/operation digests, suite/probe/corpus/candidate
metadata for both named suites and routing/pin-witness oracle. Product E2E is excluded.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires exact provider/manifest/resource/witness package, all pin/routing/
restore evidence, target/candidate and delivery metadata, and no unresolved finding. Acceptance
does not grant byte-disposal authority or land anything; merge/CI proof remains separate.

## DR choices

`DR-5`: **Configuration**, local provider after current gate, unavailable fallback. `DR-6`:
**Engineering**, physical realization under immutable protected/disposable/witness constraints,
conformance evidence, unavailable fallback. Arye decides any widening.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`L`: one artifact mechanism oracle because protected and disposable routing share the same provider
and witness/pin proof. Split only if reviewability fails into disabled `GF-026a` contract/guard then
`GF-026b` realization; never split a pin mutation from its required witness ordering.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-013/GF-022 exact contract, admission, and execution-base evidence are current.
- Mapped IDs/routes/imports, contexts, authority, facts, recovery, and oracle are named.
- Provider remains unreachable before full proof.

## Definition of Done

- Local realization proves protected/disposable and pin/witness semantics without widening authority.
- Required hostile, reconciliation, crash/restore, gate, and negative-authority evidence passes.
- Exact review, required checks/CI, and `git diff --check` pass before configuration.
