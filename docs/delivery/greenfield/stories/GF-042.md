# GF-042 — Effect-free verification semantic contract

## Outcome, value, and why now

Define exact-subject, enforced effect-free PORT-VERIFY semantics and a scripted Accepted/`Finalizing` fixture for frozen deterministic checks, so every timed-out or lost observation is replaced under a new bounded operation identity. This story is independently schedulable before GF-043: it proves the semantic contract without production lifecycle authority, and GF-043 later integrates it into the finalization-entry Transition. It cannot choose check classes/sufficiency/posture, accept, mutate, land, or configure a verifier; GF-047 separately qualifies local command execution.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

Mechanism/provider contracts owns `CB-VERIFY`: one exact Candidate/basis evidence subject, frozen
configured check classes, environment, clean receipt, read-only checkout, discarded scratch, and
zero external effects or network egress. `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes the exact Operation and
`CP-MEDIATOR` validates that complete subject/resource/capability binding before dispatch and
observation adoption. It cannot cross Candidate, basis, class, environment, receipt, or Operation,
and no delivery, workspace, or session binding can substitute for it.

Review execution owns declarative `RP-CHECKCLASS`, `RP-VERIFY`, clean checkout, and the
read-only/scratch/no-egress effect-free contract. `PORT-VERIFY`, `OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE`, and
`EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION` identify the local contract; `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes it;
`FC-SUBJECT` rejects a wrong candidate/basis; `FC-MECHANISM`, `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, and
`BND-RETRY` type and bound mechanism timeout/replacement; `CF-MECH-VERIFY` proves effect-free
realization; and `CF-POLICY` proves configuration cannot weaken required classes. `RP-FINDING`,
`RP-INDEPENDENCE`, `RP-PACKAGE`, `RP-PACKAGE-DIGEST`, and `RP-VERDICT` are input bindings from the
accepted review package, not verification outputs. `PC-JIG-8`, `PC-CONCEPTS-3`, and `PC-JIG-10`
carry independent review and frozen-policy commitments; `EARN-1`/`EARN-2`, `GUARD-1`/`GUARD-2`,
`MERGE-1` requires independent policy-aligned evidence; `MERGE-2` reserves push, PR creation, and
merge to the runner; `MERGE-3` makes done conditions policy-bound; `MERGE-4` separates done from
merged; and `MERGE-5` requires safe Blocked-work surfacing.

`ID-OP` is the immutable identity of one authorized verification attempt, including a replacement
after a lost result; `ID-CAND` names the exact Candidate whose content and basis the check observes.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-004 harness, GF-015 operation semantics, GF-021 policy binding, GF-022 manifest admission, and GF-035 Candidate; prove those predecessors in the separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment, with approved delivery package `P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS` and the authoritative landing-equivalence record retained as distinct evidence. Freeze Candidate/basis, scripted Accepted/`Finalizing` fixture, explicit final-verification posture, policy required-class declaration, configuration binding, clean receipt, manifest, `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`/`BND-RETRY` values, and fixture versions. Omitted/unknown/unbound required class or posture fails preflight; fixture is non-configurable until GF-047 gate and any real verifier realization additionally requires the external activation record in `decisions.md`.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is the semantic request/observation, timeout/replacement, and scripted Accepted/`Finalizing` fixture contract for a named frozen local check class against the exact Accepted Candidate; no GF-043 implementation is a start prerequisite. Exclude remote-gate observation or execution, policy authoring/sufficiency, production lifecycle authorization, verdict/rework judgment, mutation, workspace provisioning, external effects, target-changing delivery, landing, and provider configuration. Remote gates are delivery facts on PORT-DELIVERY in GF-044. Effect-needing checks are separately authorized workspace/delivery work or rejected.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The scripted fixture supplies a recorded mock of the authorizing `Waiting` or retained-authority `Accepted` to `Finalizing` Transition and its OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE intent; it grants no production lifecycle authority. The mediator contract dispatches through PORT-VERIFY and the ledger fixture records intent/observation. GF-043 later owns the production controller/finalizer integration. A request binds ID-OP, Candidate/basis, class/configuration, environment, clean receipt, retry ordinal, deadline, and fence. EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION binds pass/fail, bounded redacted artifacts, provider/build/manifest/environment/time. Verifier observes only; it cannot choose policy, accept, rework, mutate, dispatch itself, or authorize a target-changing operation. Capability fences read-only checkout, discarded scratch, zero egress.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Under posture `deterministic`, success is a durable subject-matching passing observation for every frozen required class, recorded and consumed inside `Finalizing` before any target-changing delivery intent. A required negative observation takes the cataloged `Finalizing` to `Reworking` edge, returns through GF-035 bounded rework, and releases held finalization authority. Posture `none` is an explicit no-op: dispatch nothing, fabricate no observation, and leave GF-043 to record the mode-bound finalization entry. Wrong/effectful/unbound input is FC-SUBJECT or FC-AUTHORITY, invalid evidence FC-EVIDENCE, and mechanism fault/timeout FC-MECHANISM; exhausted mechanism retry or required-check/rework paths directly block. Only exact Story-bound owner reject-story yields Rejected, and only a direct Blocked/Rejected dependency yields Pending NotRun; missing input/start/provider does neither. Run Stopped is overlay-only. It cannot Accept/Land/release.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Intent is durable first. Each mechanism failure, timeout, or lost observation records its `FC-MECHANISM` result, retry consumption, and failure/supersession lineage before any replacement; the effect-free replacement is a newly authorized Operation with a new ID-OP over the same exact subject and consumes the next `BND-RETRY` ordinal. `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` never converts timeout into a generic park: exhaustion of `BND-RETRY` directly blocks the owning Story/Operation. Parking is legal only for a separately cataloged cause whose recorded owner action or changed fact can advance it. A negative check observation uses rework rather than mechanism retry. Write/network/mutation assertion, stale fence, or ambiguity fails closed and preserves Candidate. Recovery validates intent/receipt/observation and every ordinal/supersession edge; scratch discards only after safe adoption/abandonment; timers wake only.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Canonicalize/limit class/basis/environment and enforce digest equality, read-only paths, discarded scratch, no egress. Reject escapes, undeclared env, effects, cross-Candidate/stale/forged result, and secret/oversized output. Credential names resolve only in provider memory; fixture uses none; redaction/quarantine applies. Results never mint policy, acceptance, target, or delivery authority.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver versioned request/observation schemas, capability fence, guards, scripted local verifier, evidence admission, and CF fixtures. DR-4 Engineering + configuration proposes typed in-process port/edge transport with exact-subject/effect-free constraint, CER proof, and unavailable fallback; any realization requires the external activation record in `decisions.md`. No policy or command-selection algorithm is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

The negative binding matrix rejects every cross-Candidate, cross-basis, cross-class,
cross-environment, cross-receipt, and cross-Operation request plus every delivery/workspace/session
capability substitution.

Unit/schema canonical values; contract exact echo/read-only, `deterministic` pass/fail, effect-free replacement, and `none` no-op. Negative authority rejects pre-`Finalizing` dispatch, effect/network/write/accept/land, reuse of an ID-OP, and target-changing delivery before all required passes. Adversarial rejects unbound class, changed subject, stale proof, forged gate, hostile output, omitted posture, and fabricated observation for `none`. Replay/crash/timeout covers durable intent, every timeout/lost-result ordinal, recorded supersession, restart, direct block on `BND-RETRY` exhaustion, and dependent `NotRun` only after that direct blocker. Scripted E2E proves pass advances only inside `Finalizing`, fail enters rework, and `none` dispatches nothing; none can create acceptance/landing. Record CER/build/manifest/environment/fixture/probes and run both CF suites.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review freezes implementation Candidate, schemas, fence, fixture, policy/config binding, finalization-entry vectors, pass/fail/`none` vectors, every retry ordinal, failure/supersession/direct-block evidence, and CF CERs. PASS admits semantic contract only, not configured verifier or lifecycle authority; runtime Candidate needs GF-040, GF-043 authorizes finalization entry, and GF-044 alone lands.

## Definition of Ready

- All named predecessors and exact-subject evidence are proven in the separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment; approved `P`, authoritative landing-equivalence remain distinct.
- Governing IDs, port, principal, fence, durable facts, typed failures, finite bounds, recovery route, oracle, DR record, and PR boundary have independent readiness review.
- Unqualified providers/effect paths are unreachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Versioned immutable inputs validate and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every observation dispatch; every timed-out or lost effect-free attempt records bound consumption and supersession before a new authorized ID-OP, and exhaustion directly blocks.
- Required unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash, timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence is exact-subject-bound; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
