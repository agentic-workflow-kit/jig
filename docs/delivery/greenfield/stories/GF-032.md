# GF-032 — Bounds, durable wakes, and liveness

## Outcome, value, and why now

Implement all 12 BND classes and 16 finite wait/progress surfaces. Timers only wake a re-read; durable facts decide. GF-015 supplies reconciliation, GF-030 containment, GF-031 capacity. This enables bounded execution, never provider configuration, acceptance, landing or synthetic Stopped/release.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

scheduling-and-bounds.md owns 12 bounds and surfaces 1–16: rework, retry, refresh, decision, mechanism, admission, ledger, target, idle, silence, reconciliation, retirement, capability proof, config read, finalizer queue, open obligation. failure-and-liveness.md owns FC-LIVENESS. DR-9 delegates values inside ranges only.

### Track mapping

FC-LIVENESS is the fixed stuck/dead/overdue selector; DR-9 is Configuration/policy bound choice; BND-REWORK, BND-RETRY, BND-REFRESH, BND-WAIT-DECISION, BND-WAIT-MECHANISM, BND-WAIT-CAPACITY, BND-WAIT-LEDGER, BND-WAIT-TARGET, BND-IDLE, BND-SILENCE, BND-RECOVERY and BND-RETIRE bound rework, attempts, refresh, human, mechanism, starvation, ledger, target, progress, heartbeat, reconciliation and retirement; EV-WAKE-DEPENDENCY, EV-WAKE-CAPACITY, EV-WAKE-TIMER, EV-WAKE-AUTHORITY, EV-WAKE-FINALIZATION and EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT are the six distinct durable wake selectors; RT-CONTROLLER records them; EV-LIVENESS-OBSERVED and SCH-LIVENESS carry validated liveness; EV-BOUND-EXHAUSTED and FC-BOUND use fixed exhaustion disposition; CF-CONTAINMENT, CF-BOUNDS, CF-LIVENESS prove those rules; idle_progress is the track token for qualifying progress. Product routes: PC-JIG-23 control/bounds/observability; PC-JIG-25 containment/control/release/observability; PC-USE-1 bounds/capacity/workspace/observability. ISO-1 requires dependency-aware eligibility/isolation; ISO-2 policy-determined resolution; ISO-3 attributable first-class blocks; ISO-4 collision-free workspace; LIVE-1 distinguishes thinking/stuck/dead and LIVE-2 parks rather than burns budget.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-015 certainty, GF-030 lifecycle selectors and GF-031 starvation facts observed in this story's execution base/tuple. Freeze profile/policy digest with every default/range, predecessor commit/tree/evidence, deterministic-clock fixture revision, CI and independent review. Missing value/proof is preflight failure.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: bound schemas, durable start/deadline/consumption/exhaustion, wakes/classifiers for 16 surfaces. Exclude timer authority, provider retry algorithm, capacity selection, obligation/Settlement realization, acceptance and delivery. No effect dispatches.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-CONTROLLER/CP-TRANSITION consume bound instance, subject, ID-OP where applicable, policy digest, clock observation and ID-GEN; witnessed ledger writes SCH-LIVENESS, bound facts, EV-WAKE-DEPENDENCY, EV-WAKE-CAPACITY, EV-WAKE-TIMER, EV-WAKE-AUTHORITY, EV-WAKE-FINALIZATION, EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT and EV-BOUND-EXHAUSTED. Timer requests read only. Fenced controller selects exhaustion; provider, clock, projection and owner cannot reset except catalogued progress/scoped decision.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Every surface has one durable start/deadline/reset/exhaustion. EV-WAKE-TIMER is a durable wake record, never a decision; EV-WAKE-DEPENDENCY, EV-WAKE-CAPACITY, EV-WAKE-AUTHORITY, EV-WAKE-FINALIZATION and EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT have their fixed catalogue selectors. BND-WAIT-LEDGER mechanically enters Recovery; BND-WAIT-CAPACITY and BND-WAIT-TARGET park/escalate; BND-IDLE classifies stuck and parks while preserving workspace; BND-SILENCE classifies dead and permits only the fixed attested replacement guard; implementation/review/rework/check exhaustion directly Blocks; BND-RETIRE creates one open obligation. FC-LIVENESS cannot stop, resolve, land or release. Acceptance/landing unavailable.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Invalid bound FC-INPUT; stale fence FC-FENCE; unbounded reset/missed durable deadline FC-LIVENESS; shared-truth uncertainty FC-TRUST. Record intent/bound first. Retry same effect only after confirmed absence/re-authorization; otherwise reconcile stable ID-OP under BND-RECOVERY or park. Recovery replays starts/deadlines without new epoch; cleanup preserves uncertainty for GF-038/GF-046.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate IDs, policy digest, values/ranges, monotonic lineage and witnessed clock fact. No credential is used. Persist timing/digests, redact session/provider payload. Timer/heartbeat/fallback cannot widen authority or reset wait; stale/ambiguous proof fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver bound/liveness schemas, deterministic-clock/wake contract, 16-surface fixtures, classifier/replay tests and CF evidence. Configuration/policy owns DR-9 values; frozen profile/boundary matrix is evidence. Ambient timer authority and silent/unbounded retry rejected; a widening/change is OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED for Arye Kogan; blast radius every later wait.

## Test and evidence obligations

Exercise all 12/16 at start/deadline/exhaustion, early/late/lost timer, duplicate wake, restart, permutation, stale fence, reset/non-reset and uncertain effect. Record policy/fixture/clock/ledger/candidate/tree digests and runner metadata. CF-BOUNDS, CF-LIVENESS and boundary/exhaustion/replay required; provider/acceptance/delivery E2E deferred.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer checks exact candidate/profile digest, all 12/16 records and findings. Verdict is evidence only; GF-040/GF-044 unavailable. Differing clock trace, stale evidence or self-review fails closed.

## DR choices
