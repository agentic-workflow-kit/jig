# GF-053 — Audit export, retention, and guarded disposal

## Outcome, value, and why now

Produce a redacted, write-once, digest-verifiable audit export for the exact terminal cut and
permit evidence-byte disposal only after every owner and preservation guard. This makes a closed
history reviewable without turning export into authority. It follows settlement/projection/notice
truth and enables SDK/CLI/MCP consumption; it cannot settle, land, release, or erase on uncertainty.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`evidence-handling.md` owns evidence lifecycle, retention, holders, pins, and secret absence;
`operations-and-observability.md` owns read-only export and cleanup runbooks. `SCH-AUDIT-EXPORT`
binds exact range/cut/redaction manifest, `ID-EXPORT` makes creation once-only, and
`OPC-ART-DISPOSE` is the sole disposal Operation family. `DR-6` limits storage/ledger realization;
`DR-12` limits the terminal/file presentation route.

Local mapping: `SCH-AUDIT-EXPORT`/`ID-EXPORT` bind create-once exact-cut output; `RT-EVIDENCE` and
`PORT-ARTIFACT` own bytes; `EV-ARTIFACT-FACT` records receipt/failure and `EV-OWNER-DECISION` carries
the only destructive authorization; `OPC-ART-DISPOSE` is the sole disposal effect.
`CF-NOTICE-EXPORT`, `CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE`, and `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE` prove export, retention, and
redaction. `PC-JIG-1` and `PC-USE-6` consume this auditable visibility path. `SEE-1`, `SEE-2`,
`SEE-3`, `SEE-4`, `SEE-5`, and `SEE-6` close through complete redacted evidence, not export authority.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start after exact evidence/artifact/operation, obligation, retirement, settlement,
projection, and notice stories listed above are observed in this story's execution base/tuple. Record the observed terminal cut and witnessed head,
artifact manifests/holders/pins, retention/audit holds, live obligations, owner principal, and all
dependency candidate/suite digests. Missing seal, incomplete range, untrusted head, or stale/unbound
provider/storage qualification blocks start.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope is create-once redacted export, receipt/failure facts, retention holds, release-pin intent,
and guarded byte disposal. Excluded are mutable analytics, arbitrary data export, policy changes,
automatic deletion, disposal authorization delegation, provider access expansion, and changes to
terminal/Story/Run/obligation truth.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-EVIDENCE`/artifact port creates immutable bytes after `CP-TRANSITION` records intent; the
operator/export reader observes verified artifacts. Inputs bind `ID-EXPORT`, terminal cut/head,
ordered range/digests, redaction profile, artifact holder/pin/retention/obligation state, and owner
decision where destructive. Durable ledger/artifact facts are truth; output is a write-once package
and receipt/failure. Arye alone authorizes `dispose-bytes`; settlement only authorizes export; a
provider/store/consumer may report facts but cannot authorize deletion or alter coverage.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is exactly one export whose content/range/digest/redaction manifest matches the sealed
terminal cut, with receipt bound to `ID-EXPORT`. Post-terminal receipt/failure is an allowed
administrative append and cannot revise cut, outcome, authority, or dependency facts. Disposal
succeeds only after routing, preservation, retention, open-obligation, audit-hold, live-pin, and
witnessed-head guards all pass and an exact owner decision exists. Missing/unsealed/incomplete data
causes no export success; uncertain disposal preserves bytes/pins and may surface an obligation.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Classify malformed, unknown, noncanonical, oversized, or version-skew range input as `FC-INPUT`;
wrong export/range/holder subject as `FC-SUBJECT`; stale owner or holder fence as `FC-FENCE`; invalid
digest/retention proof as `FC-EVIDENCE`; bad witness as `FC-TRUST`; storage timeout/fault as
`FC-MECHANISM`; uncertain create/dispose as `FC-EFFECT`; and guard/deadline exhaustion as `FC-BOUND`
at `BND-RETIRE`. Record stable `ID-EXPORT` or disposal Operation intent before effect. Reconcile
by immutable-object lookup/receipt and witnessed head; retry only confirmed absence plus recorded
reauthorization. Recover indexes from ledger/artifact facts, fence stale owner/holder results, and
preserve bytes, holds, pins, and obligations on crash or uncertain cleanup. Export/disposal cannot
resume a stopped Run or rewrite any terminal state.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical IDs/ranges, verifier digests, holder lineage, owner authentication, policy limits,
and protected-context exclusion. Storage/export credential names resolve only in memory with
write-once or narrowly destructive scope; secrets and raw protected evidence never appear in export,
receipt, logs, fixtures, or review package. Quarantine hostile bytes and redact diagnostics.
Unqualified storage/export routes are unreachable, and no provider receipt or fallback substitutes
for owner authorization or weakens a guard.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver versioned export/receipt schema, exact-cut selector, redactor/verifier, write-once adapter,
guard evaluator, owner-decision validator, retention/disposal runbook, fixtures, and evidence.
`DR-6` implementation owner is Engineering for witnessed immutable storage; `DR-12` implementation
owner is Engineering + configuration for proposed terminal/file presentation. The exact external
owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` must bind that realization; Arye alone
authorizes widening. Fallback is retain or export unavailable with durable obligation.
Constraints are the guards above; rejected options are mutable export and auto-delete; any new
redaction/disposal policy needs `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test schema/range/digest vectors, full and missing terminal cuts, write-once/replay, redaction and
secret scanning, hostile inputs, owner/grant negatives, every disposal guard permutation, protected
context, crash before/after intent/receipt, uncertain create/dispose reconciliation, retention/pin
recovery, and export verifier E2E. Update `CF-NOTICE-EXPORT`, `CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE`, and
`CF-SECRET-ABSENCE` with exact subject/build/storage-posture/fixture/output digests. Provider tests
are required only for an adapter admitted by GF-022 and bound by the exact external activation record in `decisions.md`.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer receives exact candidate and terminal-cut digest, export/redaction manifest,
guard matrix, immutable lookup/receipt, hostile corpus, and suite outputs. They require no secret,
mutable range, owner bypass, or uncertain deletion. The verdict is exact-candidate acceptance;
separate CI/merge proves landing.

## DR choices

`DR-6` implementation owner is Engineering and `DR-12` is Engineering + configuration. The exact
external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` must bind proposed bounded
storage/presentation choices; Arye alone authorizes widening. Governing constraints,
evidence, retain/unavailable fallback, and lifecycle/evidence blast radius remain fixed. No disposal
algorithm or policy is delegated; a gap stops for owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L, one evidence-lifecycle oracle. Expected artifact/export/guard/test/runbook paths; exclude SDK
and notification semantics. Internal export/verification and destructive-realization slices may be
staged only within GF-053's single worktree, branch, continuous pair, candidate review, and
integration result if they use independently qualifying mechanisms; the first slice cannot enable
disposal. They do not create suffixed story IDs, tracker edges, separate delivery subjects, or PRs.
