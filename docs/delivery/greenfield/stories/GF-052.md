# GF-052 — Actionable notice projection and delivery

## Outcome, value, and why now

Expose durable parked, blocked, obligation, settlement, and delivery conditions as actionable,
redacted notices and, only after external activation, deliver them through the proposed terminal/append-only-file channel. Acknowledging
or snoozing changes presentation only, so operators can manage attention without altering authority
or urgency truth. It follows durable projections and enables export/consumer surfaces; it does not
resolve a decision, effect, or lifecycle state.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`operations-and-observability.md` owns notice urgency, non-authoritative channels, and decision
boundary. `SCH-NOTICE` is the durable presentation carrier, `ID-NOTICE` its exact identity, and
`EV-NOTICE-ACKNOWLEDGED`/`EV-NOTICE-SNOOZED` are cataloged administrative events. `DR-12` is the
proposed terminal/file channel only; it remains inactive until the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` binds it and does not delegate urgency, content authority, or a
new transport.

Local mapping: `SCH-NOTICE`/`ID-NOTICE` bind the durable presentation object;
`EV-NOTICE-ACKNOWLEDGED`, `EV-NOTICE-SNOOZED`, and `EV-WAKE-TIMER` only alter presentation;
`PORT-PUBLISH` is the read-only source, `DR-12` bounds the channel, and `CF-NOTICE-EXPORT`/
`CF-BLOCK-SURFACING` prove redacted delivery and retained blocked visibility. `PC-JIG-1` and
`PC-USE-6` receive notice/export observability only. `SEE-1`, `SEE-2`, `SEE-3`, `SEE-4`, `SEE-5`, and
`SEE-6` close through durable status, receipt, acknowledgement/snooze, and re-exposure, not channel authority.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-036 decision/grant validation, GF-038 obligation surfacing, GF-045 delivery block
facts, GF-050 settlement, and GF-051 exact-head projections, each observed with exact commit and suite
evidence in this story's execution base/tuple. Confirm immutable urgency policy/default range, bound principal/grant, ledger witness,
and a terminal/file channel configuration admitted by the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md`, with exact notice-suite evidence; otherwise
the channel is unavailable. `baseline_commit` remains immutable planning/authority provenance, not the execution base. This DR-12 presentation channel is not a DR-5 port provider.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: derive, persist/deduplicate, deliver, acknowledge, snooze, and wake notices. Exclude
webhooks, scheduler authority, lifecycle/obligation resolution, arbitrary messaging, mutable channel
content, public notification API, and any channel retry that changes underlying facts.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`CP-PROJECTION` derives a notice from verified ledger facts; `RT-OPERATOR` renders/delivers through
the private consumer/channel boundary. Durable facts bind `ID-NOTICE`, source condition/head,
urgency, redacted payload digest, delivery attempts/receipts, acknowledgement/snooze scope, and
wake condition. `CP-ESCALATION` alone validates the responder/current `ID-GRANT` for ack/snooze and
commits events; neither channel nor consumer can decide, resolve, dispatch, or alter urgency. Timers
only submit the exact durable wake basis.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is one deduplicated notice per causal basis, durable before delivery, with redacted terminal/
file output and exact acknowledgement or snooze presentation record. Delivery failure leaves the
notice and condition undelivered/surfaced; it cannot hide `Blocked`, `Parked`, `Stopped`, or an open
obligation. Ack/snooze do not alter Run, Story, settlement, owner, grant, deadline, outcome, or
underlying condition. Invalid/no-grant input records no presentation mutation.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Use `FC-INPUT` for malformed, unknown, noncanonical, oversized, or version-skew notice input;
`FC-SUBJECT` for wrong notice/source condition; `FC-FENCE` for stale wake/generation;
`FC-AUTHORITY` for responder/grant mismatch; `FC-EVIDENCE` for invalid delivery receipt;
and `FC-MECHANISM` for a channel timeout/fault. The configured delivery target and snooze wake are
deterministic finite deadlines, not a new product `BND-*` class: a missed or failed presentation
attempt records the notice as unsent and keeps it visible, with no automatic renewal. Any underlying
condition retains its own named `BND-*` start and exhaustion; notice delivery never resets it.
Record delivery intent by stable notice/attempt identity first and reconcile an uncertain write
against the append-only receipt. Same delivery retry needs confirmed absence and recorded
reauthorization; otherwise retain undelivered evidence and surface it. Rebuild notices from ledger
facts after crash, fence stale wake/ack inputs, preserve channel records on cleanup, and never resume
a lifecycle from a notification.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Canonicalize notice identity, source head, responder/grant, bounded snooze condition and payload
limits. Resolve channel credential name in memory with append-only least privilege; redact secrets,
credentials, protected evidence, and untrusted provider text from payload/receipts. Unknown channel
configuration or missing/invalid channel evidence fails closed. Channel success, fallback, or a
provider claim cannot create a grant or widen its scope.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver versioned notice schema/reducer, channel adapter contract, append-only writer/receipt
reconciler, CLI-safe renderer, fixtures, and conformance evidence. `DR-12` is implemented by
Engineering + configuration; the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` must bind the recorded terminal/append-only channel, while Arye alone owns any widening.
Constraints are durable fact first, no delivery authority, retained
undelivered notice, redaction, and exact receipt; rejected alternatives are webhook/scheduler and
ephemeral-only message; fallback is durable unsent notice and `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` for a new
channel.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run unit/schema/dedup tests; source-condition permutations; negative grant and cross-principal
tests; hostile payload/redaction tests; receipt loss/replay/crash tests; snooze/wake bound tests;
channel absent/failure/uncertain reconciliation; recovery rebuild; and terminal `Stopped`/blocked
surface E2E. Update `CF-NOTICE-EXPORT` and `CF-BLOCK-SURFACING` with exact manifest, fixture,
channel posture, input/output, and result digests. The activation-bound terminal/file route is DR-12-only:
no `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` evidence applies. A future external port provider requires a separately
authorized, qualified, and reviewed provider story.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review verifies exact candidate/target/package digests, redacted output fixtures,
grant-negative corpus, replay/fault evidence, and both suite results. A self-authored/stale channel
receipt or notice not bound to its source condition fails; acceptance is not the landing proof.

## DR choices

`DR-12` implementation owner is Engineering + configuration; the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` must bind the proposed terminal/file
option, and Arye alone owns any widening. It records the immutable constraints above, evidence channel corpus, failure
fallback durable unsent notice, and no widened alternative. `DR-5` does not apply to this presentation
channel; new transport/policy requires owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

M, one durable notice semantic plus activation-bound terminal/file presentation oracle. Expected notice/
projection/channel/test paths; exclude export, public consumers, and external port providers. Do
not split this story to invent a qualified provider; a future external provider needs a separately
authorized semantic/provider contract and gate.
