# GF-046 — Preservation-safe retirement

## Outcome, value, and why now

Retire sessions, workspaces, review venues, authority, artifacts, and controller resources only after preservation, without changing business outcome or release. It needs durable artifact/evidence/operation/workspace/session/obligation/publication/delivery/block facts. Terminal Stopped settlement remains GF-050.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

I18 separates outcome from Retirement and I19 requires preservation before destruction. Failure/liveness defines finite Retirement; lifecycle catalog owns retiring transitions; operations/observability owns residual duties. Listed operations/events implement preserved session/workspace/review resources; CF-SEPARATION and CF-PRESERVATION are proof.

`RT-CONTROLLER` is the only retirement transition writer; `EV-WORKSPACE-PRESERVED` is the required
preservation receipt. `OPC-SESSION-CLOSE`, `OPC-WS-PRESERVE`, `OPC-WS-RETIRE`,
`OPC-REV-RETIRE-REF`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-REQUEST`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-STATUS`, and
`OPC-REV-RETIRE-COMMENT` retire only their named session/workspace/review resources after proof.
`OPC-ART-DISPOSE` is available here only in its cataloged post-retirement `release-pin` mode after
the exact holder-retirement Transition commits; `dispose-bytes` is never authorized by this story.
`BND-RETIRE` and `FC-BOUND` govern the finite duty; `retirement_or_stop` is the progress surface,
not a new Story outcome. `CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE` supplements `CF-SEPARATION` and
`CF-PRESERVATION` for retained evidence. `PC-USE-6` requires reconstructible/redacted evidence.
`MERGE-1` requires independent policy-aligned evidence; `MERGE-2` reserves push, PR creation, and
merge to the runner; `MERGE-3` makes done conditions policy-bound; `MERGE-4` separates done from
merged; and `MERGE-5` requires safe Blocked-work surfacing. None authorizes cleanup to alter the
recorded outcome.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged exact evidence for all dependencies, separately recording explicitly authorized phase/story scope, current product/design/track provenance, and the observed execution-base ref/commit/tree with predecessor containment. Freeze preserved resource inventory, owner, outcome, authority/fence, evidence/retention/pin state, review/request identity, destruction eligibility, and BND-RETIRE. Missing or unverifiable preservation/authority never permits cleanup.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is duty planning, preserve/readback, safe close/retire, reconciliation, residual obligation, recovery, and post-commit holder-pin release through `OPC-ART-DISPOSE release-pin`. Exclude decision of business outcome, acceptance, landing/release, terminal settlement, evidence disposal policy, every `dispose-bytes` action, provider admission, and destructive cleanup outside authorized retirement.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

Controller/transition engine alone records retirement intents and dispatches their operations through `PORT-SESSION`, `PORT-WORKSPACE`, `PORT-ARTIFACT`, or `PORT-DELIVERY`. `PORT-LEDGER` is not a dispatch port: it records only the witnessed Transitions, intents, and validated results, including preservation receipt, duty, outcome-separation, and obligation facts. `PORT-ARTIFACT` may receive only a mode-bound `OPC-ART-DISPOSE release-pin` intent after the exact holder-retirement Transition commits; this story cannot form or dispatch `dispose-bytes`. Inputs bind resource, holder, exact outcome, fence, retention/pin, and deadline. Providers attest actions only; worker/reviewer/projection cannot retire authority or rewrite outcome. Preserved facts are prerequisite to holder retirement or pin release.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is durable preservation evidence and completed retirement duties while Story outcome and dependency-release history are byte-for-byte unchanged. Failed/uncertain duty retains resource and opens obligation; no cleanup can create Landed, clear Blocked, accept, or release. Rejected/NotRun/Blocked/Landed retain their recorded outcomes; suspension/resume retains duty state.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

An ordinary absent, unreadable, malformed, or integrity-failing preservation receipt/proof selects story-scoped `FC-EVIDENCE` and never authorizes cleanup. Before a business-terminal/Retiring position, apply the named Story containment: park only when recorded owner action can supply or reauthorize the evidence, otherwise directly block. During an automatic duty in `Retiring` or the terminal `Stopped` overlay, retain the resource and current position and mint or preserve the exact live `open` Residual Obligation; if its original bound later exhausts while blocking settlement, record the named no-settlement disposition without changing that status or position. Never invent a `Retiring` → `Blocked` edge. A witness-chain fork/rollback, witness-currency failure, or trust-root compromise is shared `FC-TRUST`: immediately fence/halt dispatch and adoption, preserve the resource, surface the lost guarantee, and require externally governed recovery. `FC-EFFECT` covers uncertain retirement or `release-pin`, FC-AUTHORITY/FENCE/SUBJECT cover scope errors, and FC-BOUND applies at BND-RETIRE. Intent precedes effect; reconcile by stable resource/operation identity and exact mode; never retry an uncertain effect without confirmed absence plus reauthorization. An uncertain pin release preserves or restores the conservative pin. No failure, reconciliation, recovery, or cleanup route may select `dispose-bytes`. Recovery rebuilds duties/pins/receipts; timeout creates a residual obligation, preserves the resource, and wakes only.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate resource identity, holder, retention/pin, evidence integrity, witness lineage/currency, fence, destructive eligibility and bounded redacted facts. Credentials remain named/in-memory. Never log secret artifacts or provider URLs; quarantine hostile receipts. Ordinary receipt absence/integrity failure remains `FC-EVIDENCE`; only witness-chain/currency/trust-root compromise selects `FC-TRUST`. Unqualified adapter cannot clean; provider result never widens cleanup/outcome authority.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver retirement duty planner/state machine, preservation/readback receipts, dispatch-versus-ledger port guards, mode-locked `OPC-ART-DISPOSE release-pin` handling, scripted cleanup fixtures, obligation integration, recovery matrix, and CF catalog. DR-3 is immutable transition tables with replay proof. DR-6 is witnessed storage/protected-disposable separation with `FC-EVIDENCE` for ordinary missing/invalid preservation proof and `FC-TRUST` stop only for witness-chain/currency/trust-root compromise; no `dispose-bytes` route or disposal algorithm is implemented.

Operation-mode tests accept `OPC-ART-DISPOSE release-pin` only after the exact holder-retirement commit and reject every attempted `dispose-bytes` payload, authorization, dispatch, result adoption, retry, or recovery. Failure-selection fixtures prove absent/unreadable/malformed/integrity-failing receipts or proofs produce story-scoped `FC-EVIDENCE`, with preterminal park/block containment versus `Retiring`/terminal-overlay resource retention and an exact `open` Residual Obligation; separate exhaustion fixtures prove the later no-settlement disposition leaves status/position unchanged and reject an invented `Retiring` → `Blocked` edge. Witness fork/rollback, stale witness currency, or trust-root compromise alone produce shared `FC-TRUST` with immediate fence/halt and no cleanup. Run CF-SEPARATION/CF-PRESERVATION and applicable evidence lifecycle.
