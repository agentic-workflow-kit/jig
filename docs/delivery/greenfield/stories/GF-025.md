# GF-025 — Qualified local file ledger, registry, and witness provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Qualify one local-file realization for ledger, target registry, and independent witness under the
already fixed semantic contracts. It closes the persistence provider gate required by GF-023/GF-024;
it does not redefine storage semantics, create a Run, or authorize any effect.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`RT-LEDGER`, `RT-REGISTRY`, and `RT-WITNESS` are separate trust responsibilities reached only via
`PORT-LEDGER`. `LG-APPEND`, `LG-READ`, `LG-RECORD`, `LG-POSITION`, `LG-ACK`, and `LG-CHAIN` fix
conditional ordered records, verified readback, flush-before-ack, and tamper detection.
`LG-WITNESS` is independently trusted; `LG-INTAKE` owns the pre-Run commit point;
`LG-PREFLIGHT-ATTEMPT` is durable pre-Run evidence, not authority; `LG-SNAPSHOT` is only verified
replay aid. `FC-TRUST` owns fork, rollback, contradictory witness, and indeterminate trusted-read
failure; `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` owns ledger/registry/intake acknowledgement and verified-read waits.
`CF-MECH-LEDGER` and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` prove realization. `DR-5`/`DR-6` retain choice bounds.
`PC-JIG-15`, `PC-JIG-16`, `PC-CONCEPTS-5`, and `PC-USE-5` map provider replaceability,
proof-before-trust, and swap discipline. `STACK-1`, `STACK-2`, `STACK-3`, `STACK-4`, `STACK-5`,
`STACK-6`, and `STACK-7` require vendor-independent, seam-swappable, profile-compatible,
attested, authority-contained, no-shortcut, compatible provision.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

GF-010 is the `semantic-to-provider` ledger edge, GF-012 supplies registry semantics, and GF-022
supplies current manifest/admission evidence. All must be merged on the selected baseline; verify
the exact local provider/build/resource/witness-trust basis and current gate. A working local file,
snapshot, or provider assertion cannot substitute for the full proof.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is local-file realization and qualification for the single `PORT-LEDGER` mechanism family:
Run ledgers, registry, witness, intake records, preflight records, and snapshots. Package layout,
new protocol, new authority, artifact storage, source/provider semantics, preview, intake policy,
and controller lifecycle behavior are excluded.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The provider implements `PORT-LEDGER` for the three runtime units but is passive: callers retain
authority. It conditionally appends/reads exact records and heads, atomically creates accepted
successor intake acknowledgement-plus-cut-claim pairs, stores preflight variants, and exposes
verified witness readback. Registry rebind uses the fixed registry contract, not provider choice.
The witness has a trust path independent of every witnessed store and backups. The provider cannot
decide, approve, dispatch, publish, finalize, create a Run, or manufacture an intake result.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is current exact local-provider evidence proving conditional append, chain/readback,
registry rebind, atomic successor pair, preflight record behavior, and witness-before-ack. It
releases provider configurability only. Faulty, stale, incomplete, rollback, or unverified
observations leave the provider unavailable; no Story outcome, Run, transition, or effect is
created by qualification.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Every write records intent before mutation. `FC-INPUT` rejects malformed or noncanonical records,
identities, digests, and pair members; `FC-SUBJECT` rejects the wrong Run, registry, intake, provider,
resource, or witness basis; `FC-FENCE` rejects stale generation, manifest, expected head, or
position; `FC-EVIDENCE` rejects missing or integrity-invalid qualification/readback proof;
`FC-AUTHORITY` rejects a caller or provider attempting an unauthorized write or decision; and
`FC-MECHANISM` classifies a confirmed file/witness access fault before commit uncertainty.

`CP-INTAKE` owns an intake wait and the transition engine's commit protocol owns every other
ledger/registry wait. `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` starts when the durable expected head/request identity is
submitted, uses the 30-second default within the approved 1-second–5-minute range, and wakes only
on a verified acknowledgement or readback. It never renews. Exhaustion halts dispatch and enters
Recovery; pre-Run intake remains unaccepted and no shared-authority uncertainty becomes a
Story-level block or a new Run.

Lost acknowledgement uses verified five-way readback: same proposal committed, expected position
absent, competing committed, same-writer digest integrity failure, or indeterminate trusted-read
failure. Only confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization may retry the same identity without
resetting the original wait; otherwise adopt, fence/recompute, or stop/preserve. Fork, rollback,
witness-ahead/contradiction, missing pair companion, wrong winner, or unverifiable trusted read is
`FC-TRUST`: fence and halt autonomous adoption for externally governed recovery, and never claim
`Stopped` without a trustworthy witnessed append basis. Crash/restore probes cover before flush,
after flush/before witness, after witness/lost response, snapshot recovery, and each trust failure.
The successor pair is never repaired one side at a time; recovery resumes only after exact
chain/head/witness verification. No provider cleanup may delete authoritative facts.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate scope, canonical bytes/digests, expected position, chain, registry realization, pair
members, and witness head. Local credentials/paths are manifest-bound and resolved in memory only;
logs retain opaque digests and redact paths/secrets. The witness cannot depend on ledger, registry,
intake, or their backups. Provider output/config/fallback cannot grant caller authority; guard
construction keeps the local provider unreachable until exact conformance and admission evidence.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver one local-file mechanism realization, manifest-scoped configuration guard, fault/crash/
restore corpus, exact proof records, and provider-gate evaluator integration. `DR-5` owner
**Configuration** selects this local provider only after gate; `DR-6` owner **Engineering** selects
physical realization only beneath all fixed semantics. Both fall back to unavailable; Arye owns any
widening. No package path, alternate protocol, pair relaxation, or witness trust choice is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run conditional append, chain/read, all five readback results, witness independence/order,
rollback/restore, snapshot, registry rebind/concurrency, preflight variant replay, atomic accepted
successor pair and second-winner/crash corpus, and negative-authority tests. Record provider/build/
scope/witness basis, records/heads/digests, suite/probe/corpus/candidate metadata for
`CF-MECH-LEDGER`, `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, and the stated five-way probes. No product E2E outcome is
claimed because this is a passive provider.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires exact provider/manifest/build/resource/witness basis, complete
conformance and crash/restore evidence, candidate/target and delivery metadata, and resolved
findings. Acceptance is not runtime authority or landing; merged exact-candidate CI proof remains
separate.

## DR choices

`DR-5`: **Configuration**, initial local provider only after exact gate, unavailable fallback.
`DR-6`: **Engineering**, physical file realization under invariant append/pair/witness constraints,
conformance evidence, unavailable fallback. Arye must decide any widening; no other choice opens.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`L` remains one cohesive `PORT-LEDGER` mechanism-family oracle: ledger, registry, witness, intake,
and preflight records share conditional-create/readback and one independent-trust proof. Split only
if reviewability fails: `GF-025a` may land the contract/config guard with the provider disabled,
then `GF-025b` realizes the family; never split atomic pair or witness from its acknowledgements.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-010/GF-012/GF-022 exact merge, manifest, and current provider basis are verified.
- All mapped IDs/routes/imports, port authority, durable records, trust, recovery, and gate are named.
- The local provider is unconfigurable before complete proof.

## Definition of Done

- One qualified mechanism preserves all fixed ledger/registry/witness semantics and atomicity.
- Five-way, crash/restore/rollback, concurrency, authority, and gate evidence passes.
- Exact review, scoped checks/CI, and `git diff --check` pass before configuration.
