# GF-022 — Provider manifest and capability-proof admission

## Outcome, value, and why now

Establish exact Arye-approved provider authority manifests and bounded capability-proof admission.
It releases eligibility evidence only for later provider stories; unsupported modes remain
unconfigurable and no source/provider semantics, Run, or dispatch is released.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`EP-PROVIDERS` consumes exact `SCH-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY` for `ID-MANIFEST` and authenticated
`ID-PRINCIPAL`; `SCH-CAPABILITY-PROOF` uses `LG-PREFLIGHT-ATTEMPT` with
`BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, `BND-RETRY`, and `capability_proof`. `FC-INPUT` and `FC-AUTHORITY` fail
closed; `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE`, `CF-ENVELOPE`, and `CF-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY` prove security/admission.
`DR-4`/`DR-5` stay bounded. `PC-README-3`, `PC-JIG-1`, `PC-JIG-2`, `PC-JIG-3`, `PC-JIG-7`,
`PC-JIG-8`, `PC-JIG-12`, `PC-JIG-15`, `PC-JIG-19`, `PC-JIG-21`, `PC-JIG-23`,
`PC-CONCEPTS-1`, `PC-CONCEPTS-3`, `PC-CONCEPTS-5`, `PC-USE-3`, and `PC-USE-5` map envelope,
permission, provider, and replaceability routes. `SEC-1`, `SEC-2`, `SEC-3` map safe evidence,
authority containment, and no secret leakage; `CFG-1`, `CFG-2`, `CFG-3`, `CFG-4`, `CFG-5`,
`CFG-6`, `CFG-7`, `CFG-8`, `CFG-9`, `CFG-10` keep configuration owner-bound; `STACK-1`,
`STACK-2`, `STACK-3`, `STACK-4`, `STACK-5`, `STACK-6`, `STACK-7` require portable, attested,
authority-bounded providers with no bundled shortcut.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

GF-004 evidence, GF-013 protected preflight storage, and GF-021 immutable composition must be
merged in the externally recorded execution base/tuple. Require Arye's configured principal and exact manifest candidate,
then current build/environment/scope evidence. Proposal approval, provider self-attestation, and a
manifest without Arye's exact binding are not start evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

Manifest declaration/approval, proof attempt variants, admission evaluation, and structural
unreachability are in scope. Provider protocols, concrete adapters, source semantics, preview,
proposal approval, setup, intake, and runtime dispatch are excluded.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The Builder receives one authenticated manifest-approval action; Arye alone may bind exact bytes
and scope. It records immutable start/result proof variants before/after exchange and consumes only
positive exact proof. Provider returns facts only. Manifest, proof, or preflight record never
creates a Run, event, transition, grant, dispatch, or proposal approval; guard construction keeps
every unqualified adapter unreachable.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a current exact Arye manifest binding plus positive subject-matching proof and
qualification evidence. Missing, stale, negative, mismatched, timed-out, exhausted, or invalid
facts make the provider unavailable; they are not a Story outcome or an intake acknowledgement.
No provider operation or runtime authority results.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Use `FC-INPUT`/`FC-AUTHORITY`; immutable attempt records hold deadline, ordinal, predecessor, and
the two bounds. Loss/crash re-reads exact variant bytes; a next attempt requires a proved terminal
predecessor. Unknown completion is reconciled, never blindly retried; exhaustion supplies no
positive proof. Protected facts are preserved; recovery cannot reset consumption.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical manifest/scope/posture/build/environment/capability/age and principal binding.
Credentials are names resolved in memory only; forbidden authority classes and secret material are
rejected/redacted/quarantined. Changed scope is a new unapproved subject. Evidence/config/fallback
cannot widen authority, and admission guard prevents configuration before current proof.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver manifest/approval/proof schemas, gate evaluator, immutable attempt protocol, reachability
guard, fixtures, and evidence. `DR-4` owner **Engineering + configuration** may select only
transport under fixed semantics; `DR-5` owner **Configuration** selects concrete providers only
after exact gate. Both fall back to unavailable; Arye alone may widen or approve a plan.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test canonical principal/scope binding, manifest/proposal non-substitution, stale/negative/timeout/
exhausted variants, replay/crash/readback, secret redaction, gate unreachability, and no Run/event/
grant/dispatch. Record exact manifest/approval/proof/build/environment/bounds/candidate/suite/probe
metadata for all named suites. Concrete adapter proof is deferred to its provider story.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review receives candidate/target, principal/manifest scope, proof-chain handles,
gate/suite metadata, security corpus, findings, and delivery metadata. Exact-current no-finding
review is required; acceptance is neither provider configuration nor landing absent separate proof.

## DR choices

`DR-4`: **Engineering + configuration**, fixed semantic transport, conformance evidence,
unavailable fallback. `DR-5`: **Configuration**, selected provider only after admission and gate,
unavailable fallback. Arye owns widening and plan selection.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`L`: one manifest/proof admission oracle. Split manifest guard from proof machinery only if the
first stays green with every provider disabled; no concrete provider shares this boundary.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-004/GF-013/GF-021 and exact owner/manifest inputs are merged/current.
- All mapped IDs/routes/imports, authority, durable facts, bounds, and evidence are named.
- No provider is reachable before its proof/gate.

## Definition of Done

- Exact owner approval and current positive proof are required for eligibility only.
- Replay/recovery preserves bounded facts and never grants runtime authority.
- Required authority/secret/replay/crash evidence and exact review/checks/CI pass.
