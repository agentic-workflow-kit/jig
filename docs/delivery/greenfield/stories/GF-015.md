# GF-015 — Operation mediation and uncertainty reconciliation

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver the generic operation state machine: a validated intent is durably recorded before dispatch; `CP-MEDIATOR` narrows the authorized binding and validates returned facts; `CP-RECOVERY` reconciles uncertainty conservatively. This is the prerequisite for later mechanism stories without permitting a real mechanism. No effect whose outcome is uncertain may be blindly retried, and no adapter becomes configurable through this story.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`guarantees.md` maps this to `FENCE-3` (worker has no privileged credentials), `RESUME-3` (no double effect), and `RESUME-4` (named safe stop). `components/control-plane.md` makes `CP-MEDIATOR` sole inbound mediated-port gate, `CP-TRANSITION` sole intent writer, and `CP-RECOVERY` sole reconciler; `state-and-recovery.md` fixes identity/retry/fence/reconstruction; `data-and-identity.md` fixes `ID-OP` and `SCH-OPERATION`; scheduling fixes `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, `BND-RETRY`, `BND-RECOVERY`; failure containment fixes scope. `CF-DOUBLE-EFFECT`, `CF-CONTAINMENT`, and `CF-FENCE` are exact oracles.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only after GF-005's closed operation catalog and record-before-dispatch kernel, GF-010 witnessed ledger, GF-011 fenced recovery, and GF-014 exact evidence/admission bases are merged with current exact-candidate proof. Verify canonical operation/result schemas, current controller generation/witness chain, and a scripted fake for each mediated-port shape. A raw provider response, unrecorded intent, or provider manifest/proof not yet qualified is not start evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: generic `SCH-OPERATION` intent/attempt/result/certainty model, capability narrowing, mediated validation, timeout/retry accounting, effect-free replacement, reconciliation observations, and crash boundary recovery. Exclude concrete adapters/providers, session/workspace/verify/delivery/artifact business semantics, intake preflight primitive, target authority, policy choice of non-gating operations, and all acceptance/landing outcomes.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`CP-TRANSITION` records one exact subject/payload/fence/capability Operation intent inside a committed Transition; `CP-MEDIATOR` dispatches only that binding through mediated `PORT-SESSION`, `PORT-WORKSPACE`, `PORT-VERIFY`, `PORT-DELIVERY`, or `PORT-ARTIFACT`, validates identity/role/subject/lifecycle/fence/capability/result, and emits a fact; `CP-RECOVERY` owns reconciliation. `ID-OP` is `<txn>/op/<ordinal>` and binds payload basis; effectful reauthorization retains it only after confirmed absence, while an observation replacement receives a new identity. Persist intent, request/attempt, deadline/bound consumption, dispatch generation/current authority where relevant, response/failure/certainty/reconciliation observations and supersession. A provider may neither create events/operations nor advance state; mediator cannot decide/adopt; only transition commits facts. No external port is configurable in this story.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a committed exact intent followed by a valid exactly bound result/certainty fact, or a recorded reconciliation proving confirmed effect/absence; it provides a safe input for later transition work. Confirmed effect adopts once; confirmed absence can receive recorded reauthorization; indeterminate becomes a parked/escalated owning scope with retained fence. This generic story produces no delivery, `Landed`, acceptance, registry grant, or autonomous `Stopped`; `FC-TRUST` shared stop and Story-level `Blocked`/`Parked` are later cataloged transitions, not provider decisions.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Reject malformed/unknown operation/result/bound/capability as `FC-INPUT`; wrong subject/identity/lifecycle as `FC-SUBJECT`; stale/duplicate/late/wrong generation or authority fence as `FC-FENCE`; invalid provider attestation/mechanism response as `FC-MECHANISM`; certainty contradiction or attempted second semantic effect as `FC-EFFECT`; corrupted ledger/witness as `FC-TRUST`. Every request starts `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` (default 15m/range 5s–2h); timeout/failed/proved-absent attempt consumes `BND-RETRY` (default 3/range 1–5) with no renewal. Each uncertain effect starts `BND-RECOVERY` (default 3/range 1–5), has a stable identity and reconciliation observation: confirmed effect adopts; confirmed absence plus fresh recorded reauthorization/current fences permits same identity/payload retry; indeterminate or exhausted recovery parks/escalates, preserves resources/fences, and authorizes no second semantic attempt. Crash at intent, dispatch, response, fact, and adoption resumes from durable facts after GF-011 fencing; cleanup is deferred, and uncertain cleanup is preserved/reconciled rather than retried blindly.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate bounded canonical inputs before provider contact and bind every request/result to exact subject, ID, role, lifecycle position, generation/finalization fence, manifest capability and policy. Credential names come only from the approved manifest and resolve in memory; redact credentials, provider payload secrets, and opaque external handles from durable records/evidence/logs. Manifest narrows authority per Operation; provider outputs, fallback, retry, and reconciliation cannot widen it. Any provider whose exact build/manifest/environment/proof/`CF-MECH-*` evidence is missing or stale remains unreachable in configuration; fakes are test-only.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Add `packages/operation-contract/`, `packages/operation-mediator/`, `packages/operation-recovery/`, `packages/testkit/operations/`, and exact evidence operands under `evidence/conformance/{CF-DOUBLE-EFFECT,CF-CONTAINMENT,CF-FENCE}/`. **DR-3:** engineering may choose deterministic state/index algorithms only inside fixed IDs, catalogue, retry and certainty outcomes; evidence is crash/permutation vectors; fallback is park/fence. **DR-6:** durable evidence reads/writes use only GF-010/013 contracts; fallback is Recovery/preservation, never an alternate unverified store. Any new operation protocol, retry policy, capability widening, or non-gating classification requires owner decision.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test every mediated port with scripted fakes; intent-before-dispatch ordering; malformed/oversize/cross-subject/stale-fence/capability rejection; duplicate/late/wrong-result suppression; effect-free replacement new ID; confirmed absence reauthorization/same ID; confirmed effect adoption once; indeterminate timeout/lost response; crash at each boundary; replay/restart/reconciliation exhaustion; provider cannot mint a trigger; no unqualified path configurable. `CF-DOUBLE-EFFECT` oracle proves no duplicate semantic effect; `CF-CONTAINMENT` proves smallest safe scope/preservation; `CF-FENCE` proves refreshed reauthorization fences. Evidence records candidate digest, operation/subject/payload basis, generation/authority/manifest IDs, fake identity, exact fault/certainty observation, suite/probe versions, environment, redacted result and CI checks. Real-provider and product E2E tests are inapplicable because effects stay scripted/unreachable.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer evaluates one candidate with complete source/content digest, operation state/validation contract, all scripted-port and crash/recovery corpus, three exact CF packages, unreachability/configuration proof, and CI/check metadata. Partial/self-authored/stale/differently bound results or unresolved findings fail closed. Approval is not a provider qualification, dispatch authorization, or landing; those require later exact evidence and authoritative merge proof.

## DR choices

DR-3 and DR-6 are fully bounded above. Their failure fallback is fence/reconcile/park/preserve; neither permits a blind repeat or an alternate durable truth. No other delegated choice applies.
