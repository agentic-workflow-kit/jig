# GF-031 — Deterministic scheduler and reservations

## Outcome, value, and why now

Provide deterministic admission, durable reservations, progress reserve and immutable comparator (approved priority, plan ordinal, ID-STORY) for all seven RC classes. It follows GF-012 witnessed registry and GF-030 lifecycle eligibility/release. It enables bounded later work only: no provider invocation, acceptance or landing. An admitted Story/finalizer holder is never preempted.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

scheduling-and-bounds.md owns CP-SCHEDULER, all seven RC classes and BND-WAIT-CAPACITY; concurrency-and-finalization.md owns advance-or-retire-before-admit, reserve and single RC-FINALIZER. DR-3 bounds the reducer and DR-9 bounds owner values/ranges, never a new scheduling policy.

### Track mapping

CP-SCHEDULER selects deterministically; BND-WAIT-CAPACITY preserves continuous starvation; DR-3 is Engineering reducer realization and DR-9 Configuration/policy values; RC-ISOLATION, RC-SESSION, RC-IMPL-TURN, RC-REVIEW-TURN, RC-VERIFY, RC-DELIVERY and RC-FINALIZER respectively cap workspace, retained session, implementation turn, reviewer turn, verification, delivery in flight and the structural single finalizer; RT-CONTROLLER writes selector facts; EV-WAKE-CAPACITY wakes after recorded reserve change; FC-CAPACITY selects preflight rejection or exhausted-wait Parked; CF-CAPACITY and CF-ORDER prove reserve and comparator; capacity is the track coverage token for this seven-class admission. Product routes: PC-CONCEPTS-2 requires blockers/capacity/release; PC-CONCEPTS-9 envelope and independent controller/scheduler governance; PC-USE-1 bounds/capacity/workspace/observability. ISO-1 requires dependency-aware eligibility/isolation; ISO-2 policy-determined resolution; ISO-3 attributable first-class blocks; ISO-4 collision-free isolated work.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-012 grant/rebind/release concurrency/crash proof and GF-030 eligibility, terminal-root and release proof observed in this story's execution base/tuple. Freeze capacity-policy/configuration digest, seven maxima and progress reserve; record predecessor commit/tree, witness result, CI and independent review. Mutable host observation or unbound provider proof is insufficient.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: selection, ordering, capacity feasibility, reserve/release, finalizer queue and starvation. Exclude provider pools, workspace/session mechanisms, timer dispatcher, candidate/review/finalization/delivery and policy changes. Scheduler emits only durable admission/wait, never a port call.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

CP-SCHEDULER in RT-CONTROLLER consumes Story position, immutable priority/ordinal, capacity snapshot, registry facts and ID-GEN; witnessed ledger records reservation and EV-WAKE-CAPACITY. Reservation binds Run, Story, class, comparator, generation and authorizing transition. Fenced controller alone reserves/releases; registry witnesses; providers observe only. Duplicate/stale/over-capacity is FC-CAPACITY/FC-FENCE.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Same ledger prefix/configuration replays same selection, reservation and finalizer waiter. Each RC class stays within maximum/reserve; preflight infeasibility rejects, and BND-WAIT-CAPACITY exhaustion mechanically selects FC-CAPACITY with the catalogued Parked escalation naming admission/finalizer starvation, never generic discretion. Only durable release frees capacity. No acceptance, Landed, Stopped or provider completion is reachable.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad comparator/config is FC-INPUT; double/infeasible reserve FC-CAPACITY; registry uncertainty FC-TRUST/FC-FENCE and Recovery. BND-WAIT-CAPACITY preserves continuous starvation start and exhaustion parks/escalates without release. Record reserve before later dispatch; reconcile exact reservation before retry. Recovery rebuilds witnessed reserves; retirement release is deferred.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical IDs, policy digest, bounded capacities, class membership and comparator. Witness/readback is trust basis; no credential is used; durable records contain IDs/digests only. Provider capacity/fallback cannot mint admission, and unqualified providers remain unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver comparator/reservation schemas, pure selectors, registry contract integration, property fixtures and CF-CAPACITY/CF-ORDER evidence. Engineering owns DR-3; Configuration/policy owns DR-9 frozen values; active-Story-only capacity and mutable fairness are rejected. A widening/change is OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED for Arye Kogan; blast radius is every execution lane.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test seven classes, reserve-minimum preflight, ordering permutations, concurrent reserve/release, double-use, crash/readback, starvation non-reset and finalizer queue. Record policy/fixture/candidate/tree digests, witness position and runner metadata. CF-CAPACITY, CF-ORDER and starvation corpus are required; provider/acceptance/delivery tests are deferred because no port is enabled.

## Exact acceptance

Independent non-author verifies exact candidate/baseline/policy digest, reservation evidence and findings. This is phase-3 evidence only; GF-040 acceptance and GF-044 landing are unavailable. Partial/self-authored proof fails closed.

## DR choices

DR-3 is Engineering-owned and DR-9 Configuration/policy-owned within fixed ranges. New class, comparator/range change or reserve waiver is a widening/change requiring Arye Kogan's owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

Size L; one scheduler replay oracle. Paths: scheduler, registry contract, schemas/fixtures/evidence; provider code excluded. Split selector/reserve core from finalizer queue only under same durable contract and unconfigurable ports.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-012/GF-030 evidence is observed and bound in this story's execution base/tuple, with capacity policy evidence frozen.
- Classes, principal, writer, failures, bound and CF oracles are named.
- DR-3/DR-9 are recorded; no mechanism is exposed.

- Reservations are deterministic, fenced, durable and capacity-safe.
- Exhaustion parks with attribution; recovery precedes admission.
- Required concurrency/property/crash evidence, diff check, checks, CI and independent review pass.
