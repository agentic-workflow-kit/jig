# GF-050 — Terminal Stopped Settlement overlay

## Outcome, value, and why now

Consume and validate the one `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened` carrier that GF-037 already atomically recorded
with a catalogued terminal `Stopped` Transition, then progress its finite duty set without inventing a
business outcome. This story never enters `Stopped` and never opens or replaces the overlay. It is the
first safe point after durable recovery, obligations, retirement, and cleanup exist; it releases
reconstruction and operator-surface stories. It does not make `Stopped` resumable, release
dependencies, or make a real provider reachable.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`D16` selects the Run-scoped overlay. `lifecycle-catalogs.md` owns the cataloged terminal entry,
post-terminal append allow-list, residual-obligation lifecycle, and `FC-TRUST` rule. `SCH-SETTLEMENT`
is the durable carrier, `ID-SETTLEMENT` its exact Run identity, and `EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT` the
deduplicated duty wake. `DR-3` confines lifecycle realization, `DR-6` ledger/witness realization,
and `DR-9` preserves the failure selectors; none delegates a new transition or authority.

Local mapping: `SCH-SETTLEMENT`/`ID-SETTLEMENT` are the durable Run overlay and exact identity;
`EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT`, `EV-OWNER-DECISION`, `EV-RUN-TERMINAL-STOP-DECISION`,
`EV-BOUND-EXHAUSTED`, and `EV-OBLIGATION-RESOLVED` are the carried wake/decision/exhaustion/
resolution triggers; `RT-CONTROLLER` and `PORT-ARTIFACT` locate control and artifact duties;
`FC-TRUST` and `BND-RETIRE` define no-false-terminal containment; `CF-PRESERVATION`,
`CF-TRUST-STOP`, and `CF-RUN-CONTROL` are settlement proof; `retirement_or_stop` and
`residual_obligation` are the tracked semantic obligations, not new IDs. `PC-JIG-1`, `PC-JIG-6`,
`PC-JIG-23`, `PC-JIG-25`, `PC-CONCEPTS-7`, `PC-CONCEPTS-8`, and `PC-USE-4` are discharged only by
preserved stop/control/observability evidence. `RESUME-1`, `RESUME-2`, `RESUME-3`, `RESUME-4`, and
`RESUME-5` close through no-resume-after-Stopped, durable fence, recovery, and exact owner disposition.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only when exact external activation separately binds approved delivery package
`P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS`, the authoritative landing-equivalence record for `P`/`Q`, and the observed
execution-base ref/commit/tree proving predecessor containment. Those records must cover merged,
independently reviewed GF-011 fencing/replay, GF-013--015 artifact/evidence/operation reconciliation,
GF-030 lifecycle control, GF-037 stop entry plus atomic complete-overlay opening, GF-038 obligations,
and GF-046 preservation/retirement proofs. Record their merged
commit IDs, exact candidate digests, `CF-FENCE`, `CF-TRUST-STOP`, `CF-PRESERVATION`, and
`CF-RUN-CONTROL` evidence. Confirm a witnessed ledger head, controller generation, and no unresolved
design decision; a missing or untrustworthy append basis is not a start condition.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope is validation of GF-037's already-open phase-preserving carrier and its provenance, progress
of one cataloged duty, seal, or record of an exact owner-accepted handoff or evidence-backed
resolution. GF-037 alone enters `Stopped` and atomically opens the carrier in the same terminal-stop
Transition; this story never enters `Stopped`, opens an overlay, or creates a second carrier. Excluded
are a Story-state change, ordinary Retirement redesign, new Run phase/port/provider power, provider
implementation, post-terminal export/disposal realization, and any automatic authority transfer.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-CONTROLLER` validates GF-037 terminal-entry provenance through the witnessed ledger, then
`CP-TRANSITION` consumes the `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened` carrier atomically recorded by GF-037 and
records only a catalogued progress, handoff, resolution, or seal variant. Inputs bind Run, current
generation, the existing `ID-SETTLEMENT`, exact owner principal for handoff, responder/current exact
in-scope grant for resolution, completion criteria, digest-verified evidence, and ledger head;
stale/replayed input resolves to the existing record or fails closed.
The sole durable truth is ledger-backed settlement duty status, preserved Story snapshot, duty basis,
fence, and resulting `SCH-OBLIGATION`; read models only observe it. Arye's authenticated configured
principal alone may accept the exact live `open` obligation and completion criteria for
`open` → `accepted-handoff`. A responder/current exact in-scope grant may provide digest-verified
completion evidence for `open` → `resolved` or `accepted-handoff` → `resolved`, and may handle
only other duties specifically covered by that grant. Providers, stores, projections, and adapters
may return bounded facts but cannot decide disposition, append an authoritative record, enter
`Stopped`, open or seal the overlay, dispose artifacts, or widen duty authority.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a witnessed, replayable `Stopped` Run with one `ID-SETTLEMENT`, all business dispatch
fenced, every finite preservation/session/review/workspace/authority/artifact/obligation duty either
complete or exact `accepted-handoff`, and a sealed settlement record. `Stopped` is terminal and
non-delivery; it preserves `Pending` through `Finalizing` Story facts and releases no dependency.
`Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, `NotRun`, and `Landed` remain only their already-recorded Story/Run
facts; this story cannot manufacture or revise any of them. No terminal append is legal when the
witnessed append/trust basis is absent: fence and halt outside the ledger, preserve resources, surface
the trust-root loss for externally governed recovery, and append no event, `Stopped`, or overlay.
An already-durable prior `FC-TRUST` fact may be retained, but absence of append authority cannot
create one. GF-050 also rejects a carrier that claims a direct `Active`, `Parked`, or `Settling`
terminal entry, a `Suspended` entry while any resumable Transition remained, or any second terminal
entry/opening.

`NotRun` is legal only from a dependency root reaching `Blocked` or `Rejected`; `Rejected` is legal
only on the cataloged owner event. `Stopped` is solely this phase-preserving Run overlay, never a
Story outcome or a substitute for either disposition.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Classify malformed, unknown, noncanonical, or oversized decision fields as `FC-INPUT`; wrong Run,
Settlement, obligation, or evidence subject as `FC-SUBJECT`; stale generation as `FC-FENCE`;
unauthorized actor as `FC-AUTHORITY`; invalid proof as `FC-EVIDENCE`; witness fork/rollback as
`FC-TRUST`; operation uncertainty as `FC-EFFECT`; and durable timeout/fault as `FC-MECHANISM`.
Each duty uses `BND-RETIRE`; unresolved owner action uses the original `BND-WAIT-DECISION`, and
recovery uses `BND-RECOVERY`. Record settlement
Transition intent before a duty Operation. Reconcile an uncertain duty by its stable Operation identity
and witnessed observation; retry only confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization. Otherwise the
Run stays terminal `Stopped` at its current overlay position, mints or retains the exact live `open`
obligation, and on original-bound exhaustion records the named no-settlement disposition without
changing obligation status or overlay position. No unresolved terminal-Stopped duty may transition
to `Parked`. Rebuild the overlay from ledger facts under a new generation; resume is forbidden after
`Stopped`. Cleanup only preserves/closes/retires/release-pins by cataloged duty and never deletes on
uncertainty; open residuals remain durable and re-escalatable.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Bound and canonicalize all decision, recovery, duty, and evidence references; authenticate the owner
against the configured principal and verify witness/head lineage before any append. Credential names
resolve only in memory and no secret, provider correlation, or raw evidence is written to settlement
facts or logs. Quarantine malformed facts, redact diagnostics, and retain proof digests. A provider
fact cannot create authority; missing/stale/ambiguous proof prevents transition and makes every
unqualified adapter unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver the controller settlement reducer/validator, versioned `SCH-SETTLEMENT` codec, ledger
records, deterministic projection fixture, and test/evidence fixtures in the owning private runtime,
ledger, and conformance paths selected by GF-001--004. `DR-3` implementation owner is Engineering
for only cataloged overlay transitions; `DR-6` implementation owner is Engineering for witnessed
atomic records; `DR-9` implementation owner is Configuration/policy for fixed selectors. Arye
approves the recorded plan and alone authorizes widening. Evidence is replay/five-way readback;
fallback is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` or fail closed. No algorithm or new duty may be invented.

For every unresolved terminal-Stopped duty, prove the Run remains `Stopped` at the current overlay
position with the exact `open` obligation and, after original-bound exhaustion, the no-settlement
disposition; reject any transition to `Parked`.
Update `CF-RUN-CONTROL`,
`CF-PRESERVATION`, and `CF-TRUST-STOP` with exact build, manifest, fixture, ledger-head, probe, and
output digests. Provider and E2E delivery tests are excluded because this story exposes no provider
or delivery effect.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer receives the exact implementation-candidate commit/tree, approved delivery
package `P`, any authoritative landing-equivalence record, the observed execution base/target,
settlement/replay/crash evidence metadata, suite outputs, and delivery manifest as separately bound
evidence. They require no blocking findings and verify no terminal-entry/opening authority in GF-050,
no second carrier, owner-only handoff acceptance, current-grant evidence-backed resolution, and
preserved state on the same candidate. They also reject any unresolved-duty path from terminal
`Stopped` to `Parked` and require the exact open-obligation/no-settlement disposition at the unchanged
overlay position. The story verdict binds that implementation candidate and does not create or
redefine `Q`, `R`, or `P`; authoritative landing remains a separate record.

`OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` for any representation or atomicity gap. No new DR applies.
