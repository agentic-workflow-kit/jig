# GF-037 — Rule invalidation and Run control

## Outcome, value, and why now

Implement frozen-input/rule-surface revalidation and authenticated suspend, resume, and D16's two catalogued terminal-stop entries: an authenticated owner decision from `Suspended`, or an `FC-TRUST` recovery observation while a trustworthy witnessed append basis remains. Either terminal-stop Transition atomically opens D16's durable `SCH-SETTLEMENT` overlay; this story provides no Settlement duty completion, acceptance, delivery or landing, and GF-050 later consumes and advances that already-open overlay.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

lifecycle-catalogs.md owns EV-RULE-SURFACE-TOUCHED and three Run decision events; lifecycle flow and D16 own Active/Parked→Suspended, Suspended→Active/Parked/Stopped, and the exceptional trustworthy-witnessed-basis `FC-TRUST` recovery entry to the same terminal overlay. Both catalogued entries preserve Story states, fence dispatch/retained finalizer authority, and atomically create the durable `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened` carrier under `ID-SETTLEMENT`. DR-3/DR-9 do not permit a new run state, timeout policy, or generic automatic stop.

### Track mapping

EV-RULE-SURFACE-TOUCHED invalidates frozen safety basis; EV-RUN-SUSPEND-DECISION, EV-RUN-RESUME-DECISION and EV-RUN-TERMINAL-STOP-DECISION are authenticated exact control events. `RT-CONTROLLER`/`CP-TRANSITION` is writer and atomically records the terminal event or the catalogued `FC-TRUST` recovery observation, `Stopped` transition, dispatch fence, preserved Story snapshot, and `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened` under `ID-SETTLEMENT`; the trust route is legal only while its trustworthy witnessed append basis remains. PORT-DECIDE supplies but cannot directly transition; EV-OWNER-DECISION binds the owner route; SCH-RULE-SURFACE freezes the basis; FC-RULES parks/requires reapproval; CF-RULE-SURFACE, CF-RUN-CONTROL and CF-FENCE prove invalidation/control/fencing. Product routes PC-JIG-1 control, PC-JIG-2 envelope/rule/fence, PC-JIG-6 control, PC-JIG-12 policy/provider authority, PC-JIG-23 control/bounds/observability, PC-JIG-25 containment/control/release, PC-CONCEPTS-7 canonical control projection, PC-CONCEPTS-8 canonical control projection, PC-USE-4 fence/control and PC-USE-7 rule/operator all apply. ## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-030 legal Run phases, GF-035 frozen candidate/input basis and GF-036 authenticated owner/current-delegate decision records. Start evidence includes exact rule-surface/config/setup/candidate digests, current ID-GEN, decision/grant proofs, commits/tree/evidence/CI/review. Ambient changed file, provider request or unsigned operator intent is insufficient.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: touched-rule detection, invalidation, frozen revalidation, dispatch suspension, resumed new generation, and either catalogued atomic terminal-stop Transition that opens the phase-preserving `SCH-SETTLEMENT` overlay. Exclude actual Settlement duty progress/completion, policy authoring, acceptance, delivery, retirement, and every automatic stop other than D16's witnessed-basis `FC-TRUST` recovery entry.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-CONTROLLER/CP-TRANSITION validates rule-surface digest, affected subject and ID-GEN, writes touched/invalidation and decision events to witnessed ledger, and for terminal stop atomically commits the catalogued `Stopped` Transition plus `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened`/`ID-SETTLEMENT`, complete deterministic duty inventory, preserved Story snapshot, and dispatch/finalizer fences. `PORT-DECIDE` supplies the authenticated owner decision from GF-036; it never directly changes state. The controller alone suspends/resumes/stops and opens the overlay, either from that owner/current exact grant while `Suspended`, or from a verified `FC-TRUST` recovery observation with the required trustworthy witnessed append basis; provider/projection cannot. New generation and accepted-successor fence bind resume.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Touched frozen rule invalidates dependent evidence/authority and parks before unsafe dispatch. Suspend immediately fences dispatch, preserves Story state and retains finalization grant fenced until target-changing operations reconcile. Resume passes RC-RESUME-INTEGRITY under new generation or parks for exact reapproval. The explicit terminal owner decision or D16's qualified `FC-TRUST` recovery observation atomically creates one durable preserved-state Settlement overlay, not a Story outcome or settlement completion; GF-050 may only consume/advance/seal that carrier. No acceptance/Landed/release occurs.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Invalid rule/input FC-RULES/FC-INPUT; stale generation FC-FENCE; untrusted decision FC-AUTHORITY; shared-truth `FC-TRUST` enters Recovery. Only the D16-selected recovery observation with a trustworthy witnessed append basis may atomically stop/open the overlay; absent or untrustworthy append basis records/retains `FC-TRUST`, preserves resources, and cannot append a terminal claim. Uncertain target effect retains fence and reconciles before authority release; no dispatch while suspended and no blind retry. BND-WAIT-DECISION/RECOVERY behavior is GF-032. Recovery reconstructs touched basis and decisions; cleanup/obligations deferred.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical rule surface/version/digest, affected bindings, principal/grant and fence. No credential resolves; redact sensitive policy values. Provider/config fallback cannot widen authority or append terminal state. Missing/stale/ambiguous evidence fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver rule-touch schema/selectors, Run-control transitions/fences, revalidation fixtures and CF evidence. DR-3 closed transition reducer and DR-9 existing bounds are immutable; automatic stop or mutable ambient basis rejected. Gap OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED; blast radius GF-040/GF-050.

Record rule/basis/decision/fence/settlement/candidate/tree digests, witness and runner. CF-RULE-SURFACE, CF-RUN-CONTROL, CF-FENCE required.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer checks exact rule/decision package and findings. Verdict remains semantic evidence only: acceptance/landing unavailable. Partial, self-authored or stale decision/basis fails closed.
