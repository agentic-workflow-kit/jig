# GF-057 — Qualified GitHub review-publication provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Qualify GitHub only for D15's pre-acceptance review-publication capability: exact Candidate review
ref publication, draft non-mergeable request, stable status/comment, bounded Blocked surfacing, and
the catalogued review-venue retirement operations. The qualified provider remains incapable of
target change, finalization, merge, landing, or `ID-AUTH` use. GF-061 is a distinct later final-
delivery qualification subject; neither credential nor its evidence can substitute for the other.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

D15 and forge-and-landing define the review-only `OPC-REV-PUBLISH`, `OPC-REV-REQUEST`,
`OPC-REV-STATUS`, `OPC-REV-COMMENT`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-REF`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-REQUEST`,
`OPC-REV-RETIRE-STATUS`, and `OPC-REV-RETIRE-COMMENT` set, `CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`, exact
Candidate/target-basis/generation/ref/marker binding, and the draft/non-mergeable/no-target-change
limits. The mechanism contract owns real-provider admission; scheduling owns
`BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, `BND-RETRY`, and `BND-RECOVERY`, separating confirmed pre-uncertainty
mechanism absence from a lost or unverifiable post-dispatch review effect.
`CF-REVIEW-PUBLICATION` proves the review scope, `CF-BLOCK-SURFACING` proves its bounded Blocked
marker posture, `CF-MECH-DELIVERY` proves the exact review-only delivery binding against the
reusable mechanism contract and hostile corpus, and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` proves the qualified profile
is the only reachable one. `ID-OP` and `EV-TARGET-FACT` identify and persist reconciled review
effects; they never confer final target authority. `DR-4` and `DR-5` delegate typed-edge/
configuration realization only within D15.

Track mapping: `PC-JIG-8`, `PC-JIG-11`, `PC-JIG-15`, `PC-JIG-21`, `PC-CONCEPTS-3`,
`PC-CONCEPTS-5`, `PC-CONCEPTS-6`, and `PC-USE-5` close only through this review-only provider
evidence. `MERGE-2` proves that D15 publication cannot merge, touch the anchor, acquire target
authority, or land. `MERGE-5` binds review publication, Blocked surfacing, the four catalogued
retirement classes, and preservation of a failed duty as `ID-OBLIGATION`. `EARN-1` binds the exact
build, suite/probe versions, owner-approved authority-manifest digest, environment, and fresh per-
Run compose-time capability proof; `EARN-2` makes changed, stale, missing, or failed exact-subject
proof re-gate and leave the provider unavailable or use the existing owner-decision park rather
than weaken admission. `SEC-1` and `SEC-3` require
end-to-end redaction/secret absence, a narrowed delivery binding, no worker credential, and strict
credential separation. `STACK-1`, `STACK-2`, `STACK-4`, `STACK-5`, `STACK-6`, and `STACK-7`
require a provider-independent port contract, disjoint forge bindings, exact admission, fenced
capability/credential ownership, no provider decision authority, and equal qualification of custom
providers. `DRIVE-1`, `DRIVE-2`, and `DRIVE-4` require the shared adversarial probes, exact
owner-approved authority-manifest digest, and the same versioned conformance gate for every build.
None grants final-delivery authority here.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-022's merged provider-authority evidence, GF-041's scripted D15 semantic contract,
GF-045's scripted Blocked-surfacing contract, and GF-046's preservation-safe review-retirement
contract. Before qualification begins, require the exact external activation record; exact pre-
verdict candidate identity `Q`; full external review record `R`; approved package tuple
`P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS`; authoritative landing-equivalence record; and separately
observed execution-base ref, commit, tree, and predecessor containment. Freeze the GitHub API/build,
exact provider manifest,
review credential reference and least scope, repository/target basis, review ref/request/marker
format, selected host/environment, and all predecessor suite digests. Missing, stale, substituted,
over-broad, or mode-ambiguous evidence leaves the review-publication provider unreachable.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: real GitHub qualification for only `OPC-REV-PUBLISH`, `OPC-REV-REQUEST`,
`OPC-REV-STATUS`, `OPC-REV-COMMENT`, and the four `OPC-REV-RETIRE-*` operations, including their
exact effect reconciliation and Blocked marker behavior. Exclude every `OPC-DEL-*` operation,
target lineage anchor/ref mutation, target authority, `ID-AUTH`, finalizer proposal, merge,
landing, generic automation, worker/reviewer credentials, queue/held mode support, and public API.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`CP-TRANSITION` is the sole authorizer: after it records the D15 Transition, `CP-MEDIATOR` narrows
the admitted manifest to `CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`, dispatches one `PORT-DELIVERY` operation, and
validates the result. Every operation binds Candidate content digest, target basis, controller
generation, dedicated review ref, draft request identity/markers, provider manifest, and stable
`ID-OP`; it deliberately omits `ID-AUTH`. Durable facts are operation intent, effect identity,
markers, observed ref/request state, receipt/failure, delivery metadata, and retirement obligation.
The provider may attest those facts only. It cannot acquire or retain finalization authority, touch
the target lineage anchor, mutate the target ref, request merge, declare acceptance/landing, or
release a dependency.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is an exact-Candidate dedicated review ref plus an attested draft, non-mergeable request
with one stable marker context; terminal Story outcomes or the terminal-stop Settlement overlay may
authorize only the four catalogued review-retirement classes. `Blocked` surfacing leaves the exact
business outcome and dependency graph unchanged. Publication, status, comment, and retirement can
never change target state or make a Candidate Accepted or Landed. A held, queue, unknown, ambiguous,
or out-of-scope observation fails closed under existing containment and leaves the provider
unavailable rather than falling through to final delivery.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Map malformed/noncanonical Candidate/ref/marker input to `FC-INPUT`; wrong Candidate/ref/target
subject to `FC-SUBJECT`; stale generation to `FC-FENCE`; scope breach to `FC-AUTHORITY`; invalid
review fact to `FC-EVIDENCE`; confirmed API failure/timeout before effect uncertainty to
`FC-MECHANISM`; and a lost, late, conflicting, or unverifiable result after dispatch to
`FC-EFFECT`. Record every exact Operation intent and deadline first.

`CP-MEDIATOR` starts `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` with the durable request; its 15-minute default (range 5
seconds–2 hours) never renews and wakes only on a validated result, confirmed mechanism failure, or
effect-certainty observation. A confirmed mechanism failure/timeout may consume the next
`BND-RETRY` ordinal only after exact ref/request/marker observation proves absence and the
controller records reauthorization. `BND-RETRY` has three attempts by default (range 1–5), records
every ordinal, never resets, and exhaustion with confirmed absence and no effect uncertainty
directly blocks the owning Story/Operation.

An `FC-EFFECT` result retains the exact Operation/generation fence and starts `BND-RECOVERY` under
`CP-RECOVERY`. Reconcile only by the stable marker plus exact review ref/request observation. The
recovery bound has three observations by default (range 1–5), never renews, wakes only on
`EV-RECOVERY-OBSERVATION` or `EV-EFFECT-CERTAINTY`, and permits no semantic-effect retry.
Exhaustion parks/escalates while retaining the fence and draft/resource; recovery preserves that
resource as the recorded retirement obligation. No reconnect can widen review scope or resume a
final effect.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Canonicalize repository/ref/target/marker/Candidate digest, validate draft/non-mergeable posture and
response binding, and require a review-only least-privilege credential class. Credential names
resolve only in provider memory; tokens, headers, raw URLs, and unredacted review text never enter
ledger, evidence, exports, or logs. The review credential is structurally disjoint from GF-061's
final-delivery credential. No GitHub response, draft request, review credential, or fallback can
mint `ID-AUTH`, acceptance, finalization, merge, target mutation, or landing authority.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver the private GitHub review-publication adapter, review-only profile validator, exact
ref/request/marker mapper, review-effect reconciler, review-retirement/obligation bridge, sandbox
repository fixtures, and qualification recorder. `DR-4` is Engineering plus configuration and
`DR-5` is Configuration; their work starts only under the exact external activation record, exact
pre-verdict `Q`, full external review record `R`, approved `P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS`,
landing-equivalence record, and separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree with predecessor
containment, while widening remains an owner decision. D15 exclusions are fixed; the fallback is
provider unavailable. GF-061 owns the separate final-delivery provider/credential subject and no
algorithm may bridge the two capabilities.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run real sandbox-repository tests for exact Candidate/basis/generation/marker binding, draft and
non-mergeable posture, denied target/anchor/merge/landing/`ID-AUTH` attempts, status/comment dedup, Blocked
surfaces, all retirement classes, discovery/reconciliation, timeout/API failure/unknown result,
stale generation, crash/recovery, hostile response, secret absence, and held/queue rejection.
Replay/crash/timeout tests prove durable intent, each `BND-RETRY` ordinal only after confirmed
absence, direct block on retry exhaustion without uncertainty, `FC-EFFECT` entry after every lost
post-dispatch result, no semantic retry through every `BND-RECOVERY` observation, and retained
fence/resource plus park/escalation on recovery exhaustion.
Require `CF-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`, `CF-BLOCK-SURFACING`, `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE`,
`CF-MECH-DELIVERY`, and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` against the current real GitHub provider with exact
Candidate/build/manifest/authority-manifest/repository/mode/environment/suite/probe/fixture/output/
adversarial-corpus digests plus fresh compose-time capability proof. Mutate each exact binding,
credential class, proof age, and compose-time fact to prove drift re-gates and the mechanism/gate
fail closed. No target-changing or landing E2E belongs to this story.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review receives exact Candidate/target/provider/review-credential/mode and authority-
manifest digests, redacted current real-provider evidence, fresh compose-time capability proof,
marker reconciliation traces, shared and port-specific adversarial results, and all five required
suite results. Any substituted build or digest, stale/missing compose proof, mergeable draft, target
effect, `ID-AUTH` appearance, credential overlap with final delivery, unreconciled uncertain effect,
or enabled held/queue mode fails. PASS is only exact-subject provider admission for D15's review-
publication scope and is not acceptance, finalization, or landing proof.
