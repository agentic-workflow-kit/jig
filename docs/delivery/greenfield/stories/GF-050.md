# GF-050 — Terminal Stopped Settlement overlay

## Outcome, value, and why now

Make terminal `Stopped` safe from every active Run position: preserve each Story's business state,
freeze business dispatch, and finish a finite settlement duty set without inventing a business
outcome. This is the first safe point after durable recovery, obligations, retirement, and cleanup
exist; it releases reconstruction and operator-surface stories. It does not make `Stopped` resumable,
release dependencies, or make a real provider reachable.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`D16` selects the Run-scoped overlay. `lifecycle-catalogs.md` owns the cataloged terminal entry,
post-terminal append allow-list, residual-obligation lifecycle, and `FC-TRUST` rule. `SCH-SETTLEMENT`
is the durable carrier, `ID-SETTLEMENT` its exact Run identity, and `EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT` the
deduplicated duty wake. `DR-3` confines lifecycle realization, `DR-6` ledger/witness realization,
and `DR-9` preserves the failure selectors; none delegates a new transition or authority.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only when the selected baseline contains merged, independently reviewed GF-011 fencing/replay,
GF-013--015 artifact/evidence/operation reconciliation, GF-030 lifecycle control, GF-037 stop
decision fencing, GF-038 obligations, and GF-046 preservation/retirement proofs. Record their merged
commit IDs, exact candidate digests, `CF-FENCE`, `CF-TRUST-STOP`, `CF-PRESERVATION`, and
`CF-RUN-CONTROL` evidence. Confirm a witnessed ledger head, controller generation, and no unresolved
design decision; a missing or untrustworthy append basis is not a start condition.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope is one phase-preserving overlay: open, progress one cataloged duty, seal, or record exact
owner-accepted handoff. Excluded are a Story-state change, ordinary Retirement redesign, new Run
phase/port/provider power, provider implementation, post-terminal export/disposal realization, and
any automatic authority transfer.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-CONTROLLER` validates owner stop decisions through `PORT-DECIDE` and recovery observations through
the witnessed ledger, then `CP-TRANSITION` atomically records the event, Transition, and
`SCH-SETTLEMENT`. Inputs bind Run, current generation, exact owner principal or verified `FC-TRUST`
observation, and ledger head; stale/replayed input resolves to the existing record or fails closed.
The sole durable truth is ledger-backed settlement duty status, preserved Story snapshot, duty basis,
fence, and resulting `SCH-OBLIGATION`; read models only observe it. Arye's authenticated configured
principal alone may enter from `Suspended` and accept an exact open obligation handoff. Providers,
delegates, stores, and projections may return facts but cannot stop, seal, dispose, or alter obligation
disposition.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a witnessed, replayable `Stopped` Run with one `ID-SETTLEMENT`, all business dispatch
fenced, every finite preservation/session/review/workspace/authority/artifact/obligation duty either
complete or exact `accepted-handoff`, and a sealed settlement record. `Stopped` is terminal and
non-delivery; it preserves `Pending` through `Finalizing` Story facts and releases no dependency.
`Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, `NotRun`, and `Landed` remain only their already-recorded Story/Run
facts; this story cannot manufacture or revise any of them. No terminal append is legal when the
witnessed append/trust basis is absent: record/retain `FC-TRUST`, preserve resources, and do not claim
`Stopped`.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Classify malformed/stale decision as `FC-VALIDATION`, unauthorized actor as `FC-AUTHORITY`, witness
fork/rollback as `FC-TRUST`, operation uncertainty as `FC-EFFECT`, and failed durable mechanism as
`FC-MECHANISM`; all retain the fence. Each duty uses its existing named `BND-*`; unresolved owner
action uses the original `BND-WAIT-DECISION`, and recovery uses `BND-RECOVERY`. Record settlement
Transition intent before a duty Operation. Reconcile an uncertain duty by its stable Operation identity
and witnessed observation; retry only confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization, otherwise park
or mint/retain the obligation. Rebuild the overlay from ledger facts under a new generation; resume is
forbidden after `Stopped`. Cleanup only preserves/closes/retires/release-pins by cataloged duty and
never deletes on uncertainty; open residuals remain durable and re-escalatable.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Bound and canonicalize all decision, recovery, duty, and evidence references; authenticate the owner
against the configured principal and verify witness/head lineage before any append. Credential names
resolve only in memory and no secret, provider correlation, or raw evidence is written to settlement
facts or logs. Quarantine malformed facts, redact diagnostics, and retain proof digests. A provider
fact cannot create authority; missing/stale/ambiguous proof prevents transition and makes every
unqualified adapter unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver the controller settlement reducer/validator, versioned `SCH-SETTLEMENT` codec, ledger
records, deterministic projection fixture, and test/evidence fixtures in the owning private runtime,
ledger, and conformance paths selected by GF-001--004. `DR-3`: Engineering realizes only cataloged
overlay transitions, owner Arye, constraints D16/lifecycle catalogs, evidence replay corpus, fallback
`OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`, blast radius lifecycle. `DR-6`: Engineering realizes witnessed atomic
records only, evidence five-way readback, fallback fail closed. `DR-9`: fixed selectors, no policy
choice; unknown failure parks/preserves. No algorithm or new duty may be invented.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run schema/unit and transition-table tests; every-position stop permutations; negative-owner and
untrusted-stop tests; replay/dedup/order tests; crash points before/after open, each duty, and seal;
witness fork/rollback; stale-generation; duty timeout; uncertain-effect reconciliation; recovery under
new generation; and preservation/obligation cleanup tests. Update `CF-RUN-CONTROL`,
`CF-PRESERVATION`, and `CF-TRUST-STOP` with exact build, manifest, fixture, ledger-head, probe, and
output digests. Provider and E2E delivery tests are excluded because this story exposes no provider
or delivery effect.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer receives the exact candidate content digest, selected target/baseline,
settlement/replay/crash evidence metadata, suite outputs, and delivery manifest. They require no
blocking findings and verify no false terminal append, owner-only handoff, and preserved state on the
same candidate. Acceptance records a review verdict only; authoritative landing remains separate
merged-commit/CI proof.

## DR choices

`DR-3`, `DR-6`, and `DR-9` are bounded above: question, lifecycle/ledger/failure realization;
owner, Arye; rejected alternatives, synthetic Story outcome, unwitnessed pre-read, and retry by
timer; fallback `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` for any representation or atomicity gap. No new DR applies.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L, one Run-scoped settlement authority and its deterministic oracle. Expected private lifecycle,
ledger codec, and conformance paths; exclude export/disposal and operator transports. Split
GF-050a contract/reducer from GF-050b durable realization only if the selected witnessed atomic
mechanism cannot share this oracle; GF-050a remains unconfigurable.

## Definition of Ready

- All listed predecessors are merged with exact evidence and the baseline is current.
- Cataloged IDs, principals, duty facts, bounds, failure selectors, and no-false-terminal rule are named.
- DR-3/6/9 records above are independently reviewable; no unqualified provider is reachable.

## Definition of Done

- Versioned, fail-closed records preserve state, fence dispatch, reconcile uncertainty, and never resume.
- Required replay, fault, bound, recovery, preservation, and negative-authority proof passes.
- Exact-candidate review, `git diff --check`, repository checks, CI, and separate landing proof pass.
