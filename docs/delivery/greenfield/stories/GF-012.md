# GF-012 — Witnessed target-authority registry

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver a registry-first, independently witnessed cross-Run arbiter for canonical targets: deterministic waiter, withdrawal, grant, release, and atomic-rebind records. It lets later finalization safely serialize work but does not itself touch a target, accept a Candidate, or dispatch an effect.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`guarantees.md` maps this to `ISO-4` (parallel work cannot collide) and `RESUME-4` (named safe stop). `concurrency-and-finalization.md` fixes comparator `(priority, plan ordinal, Story ID)` and one target holder; `persistence-and-projections.md` fixes registry conditional append, registry-first recovery and record variants; `data-and-identity.md` fixes realization-bound `ID-REGISTRY`, canonical `ID-TARGET`, and ordinal `ID-AUTH`; `runtime.md` assigns passive `RT-REGISTRY` through `PORT-LEDGER`. `CF-ORDER` is total-order oracle and `CF-MECH-LEDGER` applies the shared witness contract.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only after GF-005 exact comparator/authority-kernel evidence and GF-010 exact ledger/witness conformance pass are merged. Record baseline, canonical target/comparator vectors, provider-attested registry realization descriptor, expected `ID-REGISTRY`, and independent registry witness topology. A copied label, Run-ledger mirror, mutable queue, or unreviewed registry branch is not evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: host-scoped registry records and witnessed conditional serialization. Exclude finalizer scheduling, Candidate acceptance/review, target observation/effects, capacity implementation, ordinary Run ledger authority, registry provider configurability, and policy changes. Registry has no global scheduler and is unreachable from target dispatch.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-REGISTRY` is the one passive cross-Run authority record; `CP-FINALIZER` is its future proposer, while this story exposes no finalizer effect. `PORT-LEDGER` conditionally appends `SCH-REGISTRY-RECORD` with registry/target IDs, expected head/digest, position, predecessor digest, source Run/Story, and variant content digest. Persist waiter (exact Candidate/basis/eligibility/comparator and continuous `BND-WAIT-CAPACITY` start), withdrawal, grant/fence, release reconciliation proof, and atomic-rebind old/new bindings. Only registry commits allocate current `ID-AUTH`; Run ledgers mirror audit facts only. Registry/witness/adapters cannot decide acceptance, mutate targets, or mint a grant absent comparator-least current eligible waiter.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a witness-covered registry head whose granted authority is exactly the least eligible unwithdrawn waiter and whose rebind has no observable unowned interval. Outputs are durable registry facts and audit mirror candidates; no `Landed`, `Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, `NotRun`, or target effect is produced. Conflict/stale eligibility remains ungranted/re-read; trust failure is `FC-TRUST` shared stop, not a winner; capacity exhaustion is future finalizer-owned `Parked` behavior.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Reject malformed/noncanonical IDs/descriptors/records as `FC-INPUT`/`FC-SUBJECT`, stale head/fence as `FC-FENCE`, invalid comparator/eligibility or duplicate authority as `FC-AUTHORITY`, and fork/rollback/missing/mismatched witness or registry/Run-mirror disagreement as `FC-TRUST`. `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` bounds append/read then enters Recovery; `BND-WAIT-CAPACITY` starts continuously at recorded waiter and is never reset by recomputation (future exhaustion parks, no grant). Conditional contention re-reads head and recomputes bytes, never blindly repeats uncertain target semantics. Recovery verifies registry/witness before a mirror; registry wins disagreements and mirror repairs only as audit. Preserve all registry/witness records; no release, target cleanup, or retry of a target effect is permitted. Resume/actual authority use is GF-043.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate exact target canonicalization, realization descriptor attestation, immutable comparator/basis, record limits, and witness proof. Registry credentials are named/in-memory; redact locators/tokens. The registry identity is provider-attested and frozen in preflight so labels cannot alias stores. An unqualified adapter cannot be configured, and a provider output or Run mirror cannot widen grant authority. Missing/stale/ambiguous proof rejects/fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Add `packages/target-registry/`, `packages/target-registry-contract/`, `packages/testkit/registry/`, and `evidence/conformance/{CF-ORDER,CF-MECH-LEDGER}/` registry operands. **DR-3:** engineering may choose conditional queue data structures only if immutable comparator, records, and observable serial order remain fixed; evidence is race/permutation vectors; fallback is no grant. **DR-6:** physical registry/witness technique must meet `LG-*`; exact build/manifest/environment evidence gates it; fallback is unreachable/stop. A new comparator, store identity derivation, or release rule requires owner decision.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test canonical descriptor/target vectors; concurrent conditional waiter/grant/withdrawal races; stale eligibility; same-target ordering permutations; duplicate/replayed grant; release structural/reconciliation proof; atomic rebind; crashes before/after flush/witness/ack; lost ack/readback; rollback/fork/witness mismatch; registry-first mirror repair. `CF-ORDER` oracle checks comparator leastness/non-preemption; `CF-MECH-LEDGER` checks append/witness rules. Record exact candidate, registry/target/basis IDs/digests, build/manifest/environment, probes, fault points and results. Provider and E2E delivery tests are inapplicable/unreachable.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires the complete candidate digest, registry contract/realization binding, race/crash corpus, exact two-suite evidence and CI/check metadata. Findings, self-review, partial candidates, stale registry identity, or evidence bound to another descriptor fail closed. Acceptance is not a target landing; merge/CI on the exact landing commit is separate proof.

## DR choices

DR-3 and DR-6 are instantiated above; their fallback is no authority/recovery stop. No unrecorded registry algorithm or provider choice is authorized.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

**M.** One coherent semantic oracle: registry-first deterministic authority order. Paths are registry contract/adapter, fixtures, and evidence. Do not split unless a pure contract/fake remains unconfigurable before independently failing realization qualification; finalizer/target mechanisms are excluded.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-005/GF-010 plus comparator and registry descriptor/witness evidence are merged/current.
- IDs, variant records, principals, exact authority, bounds, typed failures, recovery, and oracles are named.
- DR-3/DR-6 bounds are recorded and no registry adapter is configurable.

## Definition of Done

- Witnessed registry-first append/recovery and deterministic waiter/grant/withdraw/release/rebind facts pass race/crash proof.
- No mirror/provider/adapter can mint target authority; all uncertainty preserves/fences it.
- Exact evidence, checks, CI, `git diff --check`, and independent review pass.
