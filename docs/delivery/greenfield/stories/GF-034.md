# GF-034 — Role-session contract and scripted provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver logical role-session lifecycle and scripted provider: open, bind, assign, acknowledge, collect, respond and close with stable principal/assignment/posture. It needs GF-004 evidence, GF-015 certainty, GF-022 proof shape, GF-031 capacity. It enables GF-035/GF-036 only; no real Agent provider, credential, acceptance or landing configures.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

mechanism-and-provider-contracts.md owns PORT-SESSION/provider facts; lifecycle catalogue owns five OPC-SESSION operations/SCH-SESSION. D14 says internal provider allow/reject does not cross Jig; only human-needed request does. ID-SESSION is logical, not process identity; DR-4 limits contract selection.

### Track mapping

PORT-SESSION is the mediated seam; ID-SESSION is logical identity; SCH-SESSION is its durable lifecycle; DR-4 is Engineering-plus-configuration contract choice; OPC-SESSION-OPEN, OPC-SESSION-RESPOND, OPC-SESSION-ASSIGN, OPC-SESSION-COLLECT and OPC-SESSION-CLOSE respectively open, answer, bind, collect and close; RT-CONTROLLER authorizes; EV-SESSION-FAULT and EV-SESSION-FACT are validated facts; SCH-LIVENESS, FC-FENCE, FC-LIVENESS and BND-SILENCE govern attribution/fencing/silence; CF-PROVIDER-PERMISSION and CF-MECH-SESSION prove boundary and mechanism; session_silence is the track token. Product routes: PC-JIG-8 and PC-CONCEPTS-3 require binding/independence/provider/secret/mechanism proof; PC-JIG-9 and PC-USE-2 require provider permission/session mechanism. FENCE-1 exact posture, FENCE-2 no widening, FENCE-3 no privileged worker credential, LIVE-1 classification and LIVE-2 parking all apply.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-004 recorder, GF-015 reconciliation, GF-022 admitted scripted manifest/proof and GF-031 RC-SESSION/turn reserve. Freeze principal/posture/assignment schema and fixture build digest; live Codex/provider approval/branch assertion is not evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: lifecycle, assignment lineage, reconnect versus attested loss replacement, result attribution and scripted request fixture. Exclude real provider, workspace, candidate acceptance, automatic middleman, rework policy and delivery. Fixture is test-only, no credential and unconfigurable.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

CP-MEDIATOR sends recorded SCH-OPERATION over PORT-SESSION; facts bind ID-SESSION, ID-PRINCIPAL, Story/role, assignment/rework ordinal, posture/build/manifest, transition, generation and digest. RT-CONTROLLER writes SCH-SESSION to witnessed ledger. Controller alone opens/binds/assigns/collects/closes. Provider enforces native posture but cannot transition, reject Story, accept evidence, authorize delivery or forge credential. Principal/assignment fences stale/prebind/reused ordinal.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Valid assignment replays open→bound→active→collected/closed. Reconnect retains identity; attested loss permits lineage-linked same-principal replacement, while unrestorable request later closes/reissues lineage. Internal allow/reject is not Jig outcome. Invalid result fails closed/parks; no Landed, acceptance, delivery or real route exists.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad shape/posture FC-INPUT; wrong Story/assignment FC-SUBJECT; stale generation/ordinal FC-FENCE; idle/silence FC-LIVENESS; uncertain session effect FC-EFFECT. Intent first; retry only confirmed absence/re-authorization, else reconcile GF-015/GF-032, fence/park and preserve lineage. Recovery reconstructs logical session. Uncertain close remains preserved for retirement.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate authenticated principal, selected posture/build/manifest, limits, assignment/basis/fence/request scope. Credential name resolves only in provider memory; secrets/prompts/internal approvals redacted. Manifest/proof trusts; output/fallback cannot widen authority; unqualified real adapter unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver session schemas/state machine, port contract, scripted adapter, reconnect/loss fixtures and CF evidence. Engineering plus configuration own DR-4; D14's approved provider-boundary is immutable. Jig interception/automatic responder rejected; widening/change is OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED for Arye Kogan; blast radius GF-035/GF-036/GF-060.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test five operations, bind-before-assign, active-before-collect, duplicate/stale/reused ordinal, reconnect/loss/replacement, internal non-leakage, human request, faults/crash/replay and hostile identity. Record fixture/build/manifest/operation/session/candidate/tree digests. CF-MECH-SESSION/CF-PROVIDER-PERMISSION required; real provider deferred.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer checks exact contract, fixture unconfigurability, D14 negatives and bounds. Evidence only; GF-040/GF-044 unavailable. Self-authored/stale/differently bound/real-provider claim fails closed.

## DR choices

Engineering plus configuration own DR-4. Posture-authority change, middleman responder or real-provider configuration is a widening/change requiring Arye Kogan's owner decision and Configuration-owned DR-5 gate.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

Size L, oracle is session lineage plus permission non-leakage. Paths: port/schema/scripted adapter/tests/evidence; real provider excluded. Split only if core remains unconfigurable and exact contract remains shared.

- DR-4/D14 recorded; scripted provider only.
