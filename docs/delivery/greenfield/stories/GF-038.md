# GF-038 — Residual Obligation lifecycle

## Outcome, value, and why now

Implement durable absent→open→accepted-handoff/resolved obligation lifecycle with finite owner re-escalation. It needs GF-014 evidence, GF-030 lifecycle, GF-032 bounds and GF-036 authenticated owner actions. It enables later surfacing, retirement and settlement, but creates neither terminal Settlement nor acceptance/landing/release.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

Lifecycle catalogue owns SCH-OBLIGATION/ID-OBLIGATION and obligation events; failure/liveness owns BND-WAIT-DECISION and exhaustion; flow says EV-BOUND-EXHAUSTED leaves open obligation, owner-only handoff and current phase unchanged. EV-OBLIGATION-RESOLVED and EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT are exact facts, not timer results. DR-3/DR-9 are bounded.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-014 immutable evidence, GF-030 outcomes/retiring positions, GF-032 16th live-open-obligation surface and GF-036 owner/delegate authentication. Freeze duty/origin/completion-criteria schema and policy digest; record predecessor commit/tree/evidence, witness, CI/review. A notice, retry, provider result or promised cleanup is not completion evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: identity/schema/state transitions, owner accountability, accepted handoff, evidence-bound resolution, deadline and re-escalation. Exclude notice channel, physical cleanup adapters, terminal Settlement overlay, export/disposal, acceptance/delivery. Creation is not handoff/resolution and cannot satisfy a duty.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

CP-TRANSITION records SCH-OBLIGATION with ID-OBLIGATION, exact resource/duty/reason/origin, accountable owner, evidence criteria, original start/deadline and bound digest. CP-ESCALATION routes owner decision through PORT-DECIDE; witnessed ledger writes open/handoff/resolved/exhaustion/wake facts. Controller creates/advances; owner alone accepts handoff; delegated resolution requires exact scoped grant and evidence. Provider, notice and projection observe only.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Absent becomes open only on named automatic-duty failure; open becomes accepted-handoff only by exact owner decision, or resolved only by criteria-bound evidence. First timeout appends EV-BOUND-EXHAUSTED re-escalation while preserving same identity/start/owner; it cannot auto-accept, resolve, close, settle, export or dispose. EV-WAKE-SETTLEMENT is a wake, not settlement itself. No Landed/release/acceptance occurs.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad schema/criteria FC-INPUT; wrong obligation/owner FC-SUBJECT; stale grant FC-FENCE; unavailable proof FC-EVIDENCE; timeout FC-BOUND; trust uncertainty FC-TRUST. Intent before any later duty effect; uncertain outcome reconciles stable identity and never blindly retries. Recovery rebuilds open state/start/deadline. Preserve resource/duty on uncertainty; later GF-046/GF-050 execute cleanup/settlement.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical identity, origin/duty/resource, owner/grant, criteria/digest, deadline lineage and evidence trust root. No credentials stored; redact resource secrets. Provider fact, notice acknowledgement or fallback cannot widen owner/handoff authority. Missing/stale/ambiguous proof fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver obligation schema/state reducer, exact owner-action contract, re-escalation fixtures and CF evidence. DR-3 governs reducer; DR-9 governs bound value; automatic handoff/notice-as-resolution rejected. Gap OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED; blast radius GF-045/GF-046/GF-050+.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test absent/open/handoff/resolved, duplicate/replay, wrong owner/grant, criteria mismatch, deadline non-reset/re-escalation, crash/recovery, hostile resource/evidence and preservation. Record duty/origin/owner/grant/evidence/bound/candidate/tree digests, witness and runner. CF-OPERATOR-ACTIONS, CF-BOUNDS, CF-PRESERVATION required.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer verifies exact obligation/evidence/owner package and findings; result is phase-3 evidence only. No acceptance, landing or terminal settlement follows. Self-authored/stale/partial proof fails closed.

## DR choices

DR-3/DR-9 apply. New handoff authority, completion criteria or reset rule needs owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

Size M; one lifecycle oracle is durable identity plus owner-only handoff/evidence resolution. Paths: schema/controller/fixtures/CF; notifications/cleanup excluded. Do not split state from re-escalation.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-014/GF-030/GF-032/GF-036 merged with evidence/bounds/principal facts.
- Identity, owner, writer, failure/recovery and CF oracles explicit.
- DR-3/DR-9 recorded; no settlement capability exists.

## Definition of Done

- Open obligation has finite durable re-escalation and never auto-handoffs/resolves.
- Resolution/handoff is exact-authority/evidence bound and preservation-safe.
- Evidence, diff check, checks, CI and independent review pass.
