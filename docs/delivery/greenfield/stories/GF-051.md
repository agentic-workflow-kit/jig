# GF-051 — Ledger-derived projections and why explanations

## Outcome, value, and why now

Provide queryable Run, Story, capacity, wait, authority, evidence, obligation, settlement, and
"why" explanations reconstructed from a stated ledger position. Operators can understand a durable
fact without controller-memory folklore. This follows lifecycle/settlement completion and enables
notices, export, and private consumers; it cannot decide, dispatch, or repair a Run.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`persistence-and-projections.md` makes ledger records authoritative and projections rebuildable;
`operations-and-observability.md` limits read models to observation/proposal. `CP-PROJECTION` owns
derivation and `PORT-PUBLISH` publishes read-only snapshots; `OBS-*` are defined observations, not
facts. `DR-4` delegates only configured mechanism realization and cannot widen projection authority.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged exact candidates for lifecycle, scheduling, escalation, authority, evidence,
finalization, retirement, and GF-050 settlement listed in `depends_on`, with witnessed ledger
readback and their suite evidence. Freeze the requested ledger position and schema versions; reject
if it predates retained records, is unwitnessed, or the selected baseline/head differs.

## In-scope and non-goals

Implement deterministic, read-only projectors and explanation bindings. Exclude command APIs,
controller caches as truth, policy/lifecycle changes, notice delivery, export encoding, public API
stability, provider qualification, and any projection-triggered wake or cleanup.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-OPERATOR`/consumer reads through `PORT-PUBLISH`; `CP-PROJECTION` consumes a verified ordered
ledger prefix, schema version, and identity filters and emits snapshots tagged with exact head/digest.
The ledger remains sole durable truth; projection cache/checkpoint is disposable derived data. Only
the ledger writer creates facts; controller retains lifecycle authority; consumers and publishers
only observe. Every why answer binds subject identity, causal event/transition/operation/decision
positions, current status, and projection head, never an unrecorded in-memory rationale.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

At any accepted head, independently rebuilt views are byte-equivalent and explain each surfaced
state from durable causal records. Cache loss yields rebuild, not a changed truth. `Stopped`,
`Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, and `NotRun` are reported as recorded; no projection can turn one
into success/non-delivery or append a terminal fact. Unknown/corrupt/truncated input yields no
authoritative output and a typed read failure, not a guessed explanation.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Malformed schema/identity is `FC-VALIDATION`; absent/forked readback is `FC-TRUST`; unavailable
store is `FC-MECHANISM`; stale request/head is rejected. Projection has no effect identity and
therefore no same-effect retry: a failed read is a new bounded observation under the applicable
read/mechanism `BND-*`. Reconcile a checkpoint against its recorded head and full replay; discard
on mismatch. On crash rebuild from the witnessed ledger prefix, fence stale publisher results, and
delete only derived cache. It cannot resume lifecycle or settle obligations.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate caller scope, subject relationship, canonical filters, size/page limits, and readback
witness. Resolve named read credentials in memory with least read scope; redact secrets, private
provider metadata, and protected evidence while retaining safe causal IDs. Missing proof, cache,
or credential fails closed; a projection, fallback, or provider result cannot mint decision or
controller authority.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver private projection schemas, deterministic reducers/checkpoint format, why-response binding,
publisher contract, rebuild fixtures, and evidence recorder. `DR-4` owner Arye selects only the
approved provider/configuration realization; constraints are read-only `PORT-PUBLISH`, exact head
binding, and unavailable-until-qualified behavior; evidence is rebuild/parity/fault corpus;
fallback is no configured publisher and `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` for any new query semantics.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test schema/input limits, unit reducers, cross-head replay/permutation, checkpoint loss/corruption,
crash/restart, stale publisher fencing, redaction and negative authority, and each why link for
Run/Story/capacity/wait/authority/evidence/obligation/settlement. Contract tests compare two
independent rebuilds and source ledger positions. Update `CF-OBSERVABILITY` with exact fixture,
head, manifest, build, and result digests. Provider/E2E effects are inapplicable: this port is
read-only.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer verifies the exact content/target digest, immutable fixture ledgers,
projection and redaction outputs, rebuild evidence, and `CF-OBSERVABILITY` result; any cache-only
truth, unbound why, or authority path fails. Acceptance is an exact-candidate verdict; merge/CI is
the separate landing proof.

## DR choices

`DR-4`: owner Arye; selected bounded option is a private qualified read mechanism; alternatives
that expose controller/store internals or infer truth from cache are rejected; fallback disables
the mechanism. New projection behavior requires `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

M, one coherent ledger-to-read-model oracle. Expected private projection/publisher/test paths;
exclude notice/export/UI. No split: deterministic derivation and its read contract are inseparable,
and no independently failing real provider is introduced.

## Definition of Ready

- Dependency commits, exact ledger fixtures, schemas, caller scope, and `CF-OBSERVABILITY` oracle are current.
- `DR-4` limitation, retained-head policy, redaction rules, and cache non-authority are explicit.

## Definition of Done

- Projections rebuild from verified records, bind explanations to positions, fail closed, and retain no truth only in memory.
- All listed deterministic, adversarial, crash, replay, and redaction evidence passes on the exact candidate.
- `git diff --check`, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review/landing proof pass.
