# GF-040 — Independent exact-candidate acceptance

## Outcome, value, and why now

This story makes one independently judged, fully identified Candidate eligible for `Accepted`. It releases the review/acceptance prerequisite used by finalization, while making acceptance auditable rather than a branch label or worker assertion. It is the earliest safe point because GF-014 supplies admissible evidence, GF-034 binds contributors to principals, GF-035 mints the Candidate, and GF-037 invalidates rule-surface-dependent approval. It does **not** publish review material, select/execute checks, acquire target authority, land, release dependents, or retire anything: Accepted never means Landed.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

[Review and verification execution](../../../redesign/design/review-and-verification-execution.md) owns `RP-PACKAGE`, its digest, findings, contributor-principal independence, and selected final-verification posture. [Acceptance and evidence](../../../redesign/design/acceptance-and-evidence.md) and D7 own reviewer-principal full-package judgment and the rule that delivery success is not landing proof. `SCH-VERDICT` and `ID-FINDING` persist verdict/finding history; `RP-VERDICT`, `RP-FINDING`, and `RP-INDEPENDENCE` constrain it. `CF-ACCEPTANCE`, `CF-BINDING`, and `CF-POLICY` prove valid acceptance, exact binding, and no policy substitution.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only with merged exact GF-014/034/035/037 evidence. Freeze selected baseline; Candidate content and target-basis digests; frozen-requirements, evidence-manifest, findings-ledger, delivery-metadata, and fixed review-publication-observation digests; policy/rule-surface facts; and contributor principal set. A changed, missing, stale, self-authored, or unmerged prerequisite is not start evidence. Reviewer sessions are scripted only; no real provider is qualified before GF-060.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is construct, assign, validate, record, supersede/reopen, and consume an exact independent verdict for one `SCH-CANDIDATE`. Exclude D15 publication (GF-041), check execution/sufficiency (GF-042), target authority (GF-043), delivery (GF-044), block surfacing, retirement, provider configuration, remote gates, policy scripting, public API, and reviewer-less acceptance. A reviewer judges requirements/severity; Jig validates binding and authority, never invents judgment.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-CONTROLLER`/`CP-EVIDENCE` assemble a package and `CP-MEDIATOR` validates inbound verdicts on `PORT-SESSION`; `RT-LEDGER` is the writer and projections observe only. Inputs are immutable Candidate, frozen plan requirements, admitted evidence, prior findings, closed delivery metadata, assignment, and attested verdict—canonical, bounded, versioned, and bound to `ID-CAND`, `ID-SESSION`, `ID-PRINCIPAL`, and `ID-FINDING`. Durable facts are package/digest, contributor set, assignment, verdict, and append-only finding transitions. Only reviewer principal judges; only controller validates/transitions. Implementers, providers, artifacts, and projections cannot approve; contributor-set matching fences self-review even across replacement sessions.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a ledger-visible authorized independent `approve` over the current complete `RP-PACKAGE-DIGEST`, with no unresolved blocking finding; Story becomes `Accepted` and records selected final-verification posture. Any Candidate/basis/evidence/requirements/findings/delivery-metadata change invalidates verdict and re-enters full review. `changes-required` records findings and enters `BND-REWORK`; exhaustion is `Blocked`; owner refusal is `Rejected`; absent start proof is `NotRun`; suspension preserves state. This story cannot make `Landed`, release a dependency, finalize a target, or make retirement an outcome.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Wrong/stale/missing package/verdict, contributor collision, or invalid rule/evidence yields typed `FC-SUBJECT`, `FC-EVIDENCE`, `FC-AUTHORITY`, or `FC-RULES` and fails closed. `BND-REWORK` is the only loop; exhaustion blocks, not accepts. Verdicts are inbound attestations: duplicate receipt reconciles by package/session; conflicting or indeterminate receipt parks. Recovery rebuilds package/findings/contributors from ledger and revalidates current digest; it never transfers verdict to a successor. No external cleanup is authorized; stale resources are preserved for GF-046. Timers wake only; durable facts decide resume.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Authenticate/bind reviewer principal at the session boundary; reject role labels, duplicate/cross-Run identities, noncanonical digests, oversized text, and untrusted evidence. Credentials are named references resolved only in a later provider’s memory and never appear in verdicts, fixtures, logs, or exports. Store bounded redacted finding text and quarantine hostile payloads. Provider facts never mint reviewer authority; scripted reviewer remains unconfigurable. Missing/stale/ambiguous proof leaves Candidate non-Accepted.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver `SCH-VERDICT`, `ID-FINDING`, immutable package/digest construction, verdict/finding reducer, contributor-principal index, transition guards, scripted reviewer fixture, projection rows, and `CF-ACCEPTANCE`/`CF-BINDING`/`CF-POLICY` evidence records. DR-3 owner Engineering selects pure immutable tables under replay/permutation proof; fallback rejects incomplete transitions. DR-8 owner Engineering selects deterministic private testkit/CER with versioned fixture/probe digests; fallback is no evidence claim. Any change to judgment, package members, or posture is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Test and evidence obligations

Unit/schema: canonical package/verdict/finding and all finding edges. Contract: every package member binds one Candidate. Negative-authority: self-review, provider verdict, replacement-session laundering. Adversarial: missing/stale/cross-candidate evidence, basis-only change, hostile finding text, invalid rule surface. Replay/crash/recovery: append-before-transition, duplicate/conflicting verdict, reconnect, rework. Scripted provider only; GF-060 owns real-provider proof. E2E proves `approve→Accepted` only, no landing/release. Record exact candidate/build/manifest/environment/suite/fixture/probe/output/CER digests and run all three CF suites.

## Exact acceptance

An authorized independent reviewer—not an author, implementer, rework author, or contributor—reviews the frozen Candidate content/target basis, requirements, evidence manifest, findings state, delivery metadata, fixed publication observation, policy/posture, rule-surface fact, and evidence/CER. These compose the exact `RP-PACKAGE-DIGEST`. Partial, stale, self-authored, or differently bound verdict fails closed. PASS/approval creates only `Accepted`; GF-044 alone can later prove `Landed` through authoritative target content.

## DR choices

DR-3: Engineering, immutable acceptance/finding tables, fixed catalog/record-before-transition/replay constraints, permutation evidence, reject gaps. DR-8: Engineering, deterministic harness/CER, exact-subject/versioned adversarial fixtures, recorded suite/probe evidence, no PASS claim on failure. No DR-4/DR-5 applies because this is scripted semantic proof, not a configurable adapter.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L; one exact reviewer-principal acceptance oracle. Expected paths: verdict/finding schema, controller/evidence reducer, scripted fixture, tests, catalog. Exclude publication, verify, target effects, provider admission. Split GF-040a package/finding representation from GF-040b acceptance transition only if the first stays green and cannot accept.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-014/034/035/037 are merged with current Candidate, principal, evidence, and rule-surface contracts.
- Package members, authority fence, durable facts, `BND-REWORK`, terminal matrix, DR-3/DR-8, CER, and scripted-only posture have independent readiness review.
- No unqualified reviewer or acceptance-to-landing path is reachable.

## Definition of Done

- Immutable package/finding/verdict values validate; invalid/self-authored input fails closed; crash recovery never transfers a verdict.
- Approval creates exact `Accepted` only; rework/exhaustion/rejection/suspension preserve non-delivery semantics and no dependency release.
- Unit, contract, adversarial, replay, crash/recovery, scripted E2E, `CF-ACCEPTANCE`/`CF-BINDING`/`CF-POLICY`, `git diff --check`, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
