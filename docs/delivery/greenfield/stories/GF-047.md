# GF-047 — Qualified local verification provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Qualify the proposed separate local-command verifier against GF-042 exact effect-free semantic contract. It becomes configurable only after an explicit current owner or named-delegate request selects it and GF-022 admits that manifest/build/environment. Remote gates remain outside this story in GF-044. It cannot accept, choose policy, run effect-needing checks, or substitute for delivery; no qualification evidence transfers across provider build, manifest, environment, posture, or suite version.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`CB-VERIFY` binds one exact Candidate/basis evidence subject, frozen configured check classes,
admitted provider manifest/build/environment, clean receipt, read-only checkout, discarded scratch,
and zero external effects or network egress. `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes each Operation and
`CP-MEDIATOR` validates that complete subject/resource/capability binding before dispatch and
observation adoption. It cannot cross Candidate, basis, class, environment, receipt, provider
tuple, or Operation, and no delivery, workspace, or session binding can substitute for it.

Mechanism/provider contracts owns capability manifest, qualification and CF-GATE-PROVIDER; review execution owns exact subject/effect-free verification. PORT-VERIFY is the sole local boundary, and OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE is the controller-authorized, effect-free local check operation it carries. EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION, CF-MECH-VERIFY, CF-GATE-PROVIDER, and DR-5 map the result, qualification, and configuration boundary. `ID-OP` identifies one authorized local command observation and `ID-CAND` names its exact Candidate content/basis. FC-SUBJECT and FC-MECHANISM reject wrong subject and malformed mechanism fact; BND-WAIT-MECHANISM bounds each local observation wait and every failed, timed-out, or lost replacement consumes BND-RETRY. PC-JIG-8 requires independent reviewer authority, PC-JIG-15 provider-gated supported seams, PC-CONCEPTS-3 the same separation at concepts altitude, and PC-USE-5 a swap-safe qualified provider.

EARN-1 requires fresh positive capability proof and EARN-2 makes it specific to driver and run;
GUARD-1 freezes policy and GUARD-2 pauses rule-surface change. STACK-1 preserves guarantees across
vendors; STACK-2 fixes the four swappable seams; STACK-3 makes agent choice a profile choice;
STACK-4 requires attestation; STACK-5 keeps credentials/effects at authority seams; STACK-6 denies
first-party shortcuts; STACK-7 applies the same bar to custom providers. DRIVE-1 requires
conformance before trust; DRIVE-2 requires an approved manifest; DRIVE-3 reports the native posture
honestly; DRIVE-4 makes conformance reusable. These imports make a failed verifier unavailable,
not an outcome authority.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-022 provider approval/proof machinery and GF-042 semantic contract/CER, proven in the separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment; record explicitly authorized phase/story scope and current product/design/track provenance as distinct bound evidence. Freeze the proposed local-verifier manifest, explicit owner selection and GF-022 admission, provider build, command allowlist and native posture, environment fingerprint, credential names, clean checkout/scratch/no-egress contract, `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`/`BND-RETRY` values, suite/adversarial versions, and exact candidate vectors. Any drift makes provider unavailable.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is local command verification on `PORT-VERIFY` through `OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE` only. Exclude every `PORT-DELIVERY`, `OPC-REV-*`, and `OPC-DEL-*` path; remote-gate observation or execution; semantic policy/check selection; acceptance; workspace/write/network effects; final delivery; review publication; real forge; and any alternate verifier/host. Remote gates remain in GF-044. Unsupported effect-needing checks are rejected, never routed silently.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

Local verifier is PORT-VERIFY mechanism behind admitted capability binding; controller records intent then authorizes each effect-free OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE under its ID-OP, frozen class/basis/fence, and ID-CAND; ledger persists the EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION. Provider receives read-only exact checkout, discarded scratch, permitted command/environment, no egress; it returns attestation only. It cannot mint authority, mutate subject, select class, or access delivery credentials.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is current exact CF-MECH-VERIFY pass plus CF-GATE-PROVIDER admission for this provider tuple; only then configuration may expose it. Observation pass/fail retains GF-042 semantics. Drift or failure leaves the provider unconfigurable. A timed-out or lost observation follows BND-WAIT-MECHANISM plus BND-RETRY; exhaustion directly blocks the owning Story/Operation. Parking is legal only for a separately cataloged cause with a recorded owner action or changed fact that can advance it. The provider attests facts and has no Story-outcome power.

Pending NotRun requires a direct Blocked/Rejected dependency, and Rejected requires the exact Story-bound owner reject-story action. It cannot accept/Land/release or make effects legal.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Command timeout/result loss is bounded by BND-WAIT-MECHANISM. Each failed, timed-out, or lost attempt records FC-MECHANISM, its consumed BND-RETRY ordinal, and failure/supersession lineage before the controller may authorize an effect-free replacement under a new ID-OP over the same exact subject. BND-RETRY exhaustion directly blocks the owning scope; it never becomes a generic park. Effect assertion, subject/fence/env mismatch, stale manifest, or malformed output is FC-SUBJECT/AUTHORITY/MECHANISM/EVIDENCE and fails closed. Recovery validates durable intent/receipt/result plus every ordinal and supersession edge; no blind retry of an external effect is possible because none is allowed; scratch is discarded safely.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate executable digest/allowlist, args/class, environment, checkout/basis/fence, capability proof age, provider identity, no egress/write and result limits. Credential names resolve only in provider memory and no forge credential is permitted. Redact/quarantine output; hostile command/output cannot widen manifest/authority. Remote-gate handling is excluded from this provider.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver local verifier adapter, manifest binding/admission, command/environment sandbox enforcement, qualification runner/CER, drift invalidation, and configuration reachability guard. DR-5 Configuration selects the local verifier only after external owner activation of the proposed profile and GF-022 admission; exact CF evidence is mandatory and fallback unavailable. Any widening requires separate external owner authorization. No algorithm beyond GF-042 contract is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

The negative binding matrix rejects every cross-Candidate, cross-basis, cross-class,
cross-environment, cross-receipt, cross-provider, and cross-Operation request plus every delivery/
workspace/session capability substitution.

Contract tests exact command/class/basis/fence, no effect, and new-ID-OP replacement. Negative-authority tests reject every `PORT-DELIVERY`, `OPC-REV-*`, `OPC-DEL-*`, acceptance, and outcome transition. Adversarial tests cover write/network/escape, undeclared env/credential, wrong digest, stale proof, hostile output, reused ID-OP, skipped retry ordinal, and generic parking on mechanism exhaustion. Replay/crash/timeout covers every BND-RETRY ordinal, failure/supersession durability, replacement, direct block on exhaustion, and dependent `NotRun` only after that direct blocker. Provider qualification tests bind the exact build/manifest/environment/posture; E2E admits deterministic pass/fail observations only. Run complete CF-MECH-VERIFY and CF-GATE-PROVIDER with versioned CER.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer freezes provider build, proposed manifest, explicit owner selection, GF-022 admission, environment/posture, command and authority limits, suite/adversarial/CER, drift and configuration guards, and exact GF-042 vectors. PASS admits only that tuple; it is not acceptance, landing, or proof for a changed provider.

## DR choices

DR-5 Configuration: proposed local-verifier profile after explicit owner selection, GF-022 admission, exact manifest/build/environment, and CF gate; alternatives need own provider story; fallback unavailable. DR-4 remains fixed by GF-042 and has no new choice.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

M; provider realization only, deliberately split from GF-042 semantics. Expected adapter/manifest gate/qualification fixture/tests/config guard. Exclude policy, workspace, delivery, and other providers; no further split because one provider gate is the oracle.

## Definition of Ready

- Named predecessors and exact-subject evidence are proven in the separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment; explicitly authorized scope, current product/design/track provenance, and planning provenance remain distinct.
- IDs, port, principal/fence, durable facts, FC failures, finite BND bounds, recovery, oracle, DR, and PR boundary have independent readiness review.
- No unqualified provider/effect path is reachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Immutable inputs validate and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every observation dispatch; every timed-out or lost effect-free attempt records bound consumption and supersession before a new authorized ID-OP, and exhaustion directly blocks.
- Exact unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash/timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence passes; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
