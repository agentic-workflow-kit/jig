# GF-023 — Effect-free preview and distinct owner approvals

## Outcome, value, and why now

Provide an effect-free preview and two non-substitutable authenticated owner actions: proposal
approval and exact provider-manifest approval. The owner can inspect/approve a frozen candidate
without creating a Run or authorizing dispatch. This unblocks GF-024 intake; provider-manifest
approval still cannot replace proposal approval, and vice versa.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`envelope-production.md` owns `EP-PREVIEW`, `EP-APPROVE`, the two digest domains, proposal versus
manifest approval distinction, and preview's no-Run/no-ledger/no-dispatch boundary. `runtime.md`
keeps the Builder outside SYS-JIG authority; `data-and-identity.md` owns envelope/manifest binding;
`mechanism-and-provider-contracts.md` fixes manifest scope/posture. `PORT-CONSUMER`,
`CF-ENVELOPE`, `CF-CONSUMER`, and the negative-authority corpus provide the contract.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-014 evidence binding/redaction, GF-020 exact qualified file-source evidence,
GF-021 pure composition/bounds evidence, and GF-022 current manifest/positive-proof admission.
Revalidate all source, policy, profile, provider, proof, and capacity inputs on the selected
baseline before each preview/approval. A successful preview, a cached report, provider conformance,
or the other approval is not evidence for the missing approval.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: authenticated `PORT-CONSUMER` preview report; exact proposal digest and Arye approval
record; exact provider-manifest approval presentation/return; replay semantics and clear negative
authority. Exclude changing composition rules, provider proof acquisition, intake validation,
`LG-INTAKE`, `ID-RUN`, lifecycle/grant events, setup/workspace operation, and dispatch.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The external Builder receives `PORT-CONSUMER` invocations. Authentication binds the caller to
configured Arye `ID-PRINCIPAL`. Preview consumes resolved inputs and returns a read-only report of
composition, policy/bounds/capacity, qualification, and failures. `EP-APPROVE` writes an immutable
owner record binding only the exact proposal digest/scope. The separate manifest action binds only
exact canonical `SCH-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY` digest/scope. Pre-Run attempt evidence may be conditionally
created only as GF-022 permits; no action may mint `ID-RUN`, append `LG-INTAKE`, create a Transition,
Operation, event, grant, target authority, or dispatch. Consumer/UI cannot self-approve.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Preview success is a report bound to its exact input basis, explicitly non-entitling. Proposal
approval success is a durable exact owner binding; manifest approval success is a separate durable
exact owner binding. Wrong principal, stale/missing/mismatched scope/digest, changed inputs,
duplicate conflict, unknown identity, or invalid proof returns `Rejected`/`NotRun`. Exact replay
returns the same immutable approval/report where applicable. No outcome is Run, accepted intake,
effect, or landing.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Input/digest/scope faults are `FC-INPUT`/`FC-SUBJECT`; wrong actor is `FC-AUTHORITY`; stale/
unverifiable evidence is `FC-EVIDENCE`; trust conflict is `FC-TRUST`. Preview is pure except
GF-022's bounded proof-attempt evidence, which retains its immutable `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`/
`BND-RETRY` chain and reconciles same keys. A lost approval response re-reads the exact immutable
record; no blind recreation with changed bytes is allowed. Recovery recomposes/revalidates the
current basis, preserves approved records, and requires fresh approval for a changed proposal or
manifest. No effect allocation or cleanup exists.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Authenticate and bind Arye before either approval; canonicalize/digest all presented inputs; reject
unknown UI fields, crossed scopes, stale proof, self-authored authorization, oversized/hostile
reports, and hidden defaults. Resolve no provider/workspace credentials; redact prompt, source,
manifest, and proof diagnostics. Preview and proposal approval cannot elevate provider authority;
manifest approval cannot approve a proposal; neither can select an unqualified adapter. Guard tests
prove no preflight report becomes submission-time entitlement and no consumer path reaches Run or
dispatch.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver consumer request/report/approval schemas, exact digest presentation/binding, authenticated
principal guard, immutable approval store/lookup contract, negative-authority fixtures, and evidence
records. `DR-4` permits only a typed consumer transport below these semantics; evidence is
`CF-CONSUMER`, fallback reject/unavailable. `DR-5` permits consuming only GF-022-admitted providers;
evidence is their exact gate, fallback omit them. No UI/presentation algorithm may weaken identity,
approval, or authority.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test identical preview composition and validation path; report binding; Arye/non-Arye authentication;
proposal/manifest non-substitution; stale/mismatched/change/replay behavior; proof attempt recovery;
redaction/hostile input; no hidden defaults; and negative assertions for `ID-RUN`, `LG-INTAKE`,
events, grants, Operations, target effects, and dispatch. Record candidate/input/proposal/manifest/
approval/proof digests, principal/scope, suite/probe/build/version and findings for `CF-ENVELOPE`,
`CF-CONSUMER`, and negative-authority corpus. Provider E2E is consumed evidence, not rerun here.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer requires exact candidate/target digest, authenticated principal binding,
input/proposal/composition/manifest/approval digests and scopes, proof handles, suite metadata and
negative corpus result. Every finding must be resolved under an exact deterministic verification
posture. Acceptance neither lands the candidate nor accepts an envelope; authoritative merge/CI
landing proof is separate, and stale/self/partial verdicts fail.

## DR choices

- `DR-4` — Engineering/configuration; selected typed consumer transport; immutable principal,
  digest, and no-authority rules; `CF-CONSUMER` evidence; reject unavailable transport.
- `DR-5` — Configuration; only previously admitted exact providers may be displayed/bound; immutable
  manifest/gate rules; gate evidence; omission is fallback.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`M`, one consumer/approval oracle. Expected paths are preview, approval records, consumer guard,
fixtures, and evidence. Do not split proposal and manifest approval because their non-substitution
is the cohesive oracle; exclude intake and provider realization.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-014/020/021/022 exact evidence is merged and inputs can be independently revalidated.
- Principals, port, durable approval/attempt facts, failure/recovery fences, and `CF-*` proofs are named.
- `DR-4`/`DR-5` are constrained; no preview or approval path reaches Run authority.
- Independent review can reconstruct both action packages without history.

## Definition of Done

- Preview is effect-free apart from permitted durable pre-Run attempt evidence and never entitles intake.
- Approval records bind only exact authenticated subjects and remain non-substitutable.
- Required deterministic, replay, hostile, authority, and evidence tests pass.
- Exact evidence, diff/checks/CI, and independent review pass; no provider/preflight path mints runtime authority.
