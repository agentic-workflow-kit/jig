# GF-035 — Implementation assignment, candidates, and rework

## Outcome, value, and why now

Mint exact SCH-CANDIDATE/ID-CAND records after committed session/workspace facts and make every bounded rework turn a fresh logical assignment/session. It requires evidence GF-014, lifecycle GF-030, scheduling/bounds GF-031/032, workspace GF-033 and session GF-034. It enables Doorbell and later review, but does not accept or land a Candidate.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

data-and-identity.md owns candidate identity/binding; review-and-verification-execution.md owns exact candidate basis; D16 mandates a new logical ID-SESSION per admitted rework ordinal, binding Story, role, Candidate/verdict/failed-check basis, bounds, posture and authorizing transition. SCH-CANDIDATE and ID-CAND are immutable; BND-REWORK has no renewal. DR-3/DR-9 are bounded.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-014 exact evidence storage, GF-030 state/transition, GF-031 reserve, GF-032 bound, GF-033 workspace basis and GF-034 bound/active session evidence. Record all commit/tree/evidence digests, selected baseline, current generation, manifest/posture and setup receipt. An ambient working tree, prior candidate or provider process reuse is not start evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: implementation assignment basis, candidate minting/binding, fresh rework ordinal/session and failure containment. Exclude reviewer verdict/acceptance, publication, verification, finalization/delivery and changing rework limit. Reconnect may preserve one assignment but never replaces fresh rework logical identity.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

CP-TRANSITION consumes fenced Story state, workspace/session facts, evidence digest, reserve and BND-REWORK; witnessed ledger writes SCH-CANDIDATE with ID-CAND, content/tree/basis digest, session/workspace IDs, posture, ordinal and transition. Output is only candidate/rework intent over existing ports. Controller alone mints candidate and commits ordinal; provider/session cannot self-mint or re-authorize. Exact candidate/basis/generation fences stale attribution.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

A candidate binds one exact content/basis and attributable implementation session. Changes-required/failed-check rework commits next ordinal once, reserves capacity and creates fresh ID-SESSION; prior same-role assignment is terminal/reconciled/fenced first. Exhaustion blocks the Story and dependents derive NotRun dependency-blocked. No candidate is Accepted, published, landed or dependency-releasing in this phase.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Missing/mismatched basis/evidence is FC-EVIDENCE/FC-SUBJECT; stale session/generation FC-FENCE; invalid payload FC-INPUT; exhausted BND-REWORK FC-BOUND. Record candidate intent before session work. Uncertain session/workspace/evidence effects reconcile stable IDs and never duplicate candidate/ordinal; recovery rebuilds candidate/assignment facts. Preserve prior candidate and workspace; retirement is deferred.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical ID-CAND, content/tree/basis/evidence/session/workspace/posture/digest bindings and bounded ordinal. No credential resolves. Evidence trust roots are GF-014 and witnessed ledger; redact content secrets. Provider output, old candidate or reused process cannot widen authority or satisfy fresh rework requirement.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver candidate schema/identity, assignment/rework transition rules, fresh-session fixtures and crash/replay evidence. DR-3 chooses closed transition reducer; DR-9 chooses rework value within range. D16 rejects standing assignment authority. Gap is OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED; blast radius GF-036/040+.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test exact candidate binding, wrong/stale basis, duplicate mint, fresh ordinal/session, prior-session fence, BND-REWORK exhaustion, capacity wait, crash/replay/recovery and hostile evidence. Record candidate/session/workspace/evidence/bound/candidate-tree digests and witness/runner data. CF-BINDING, CF-RUN-CONTROL and crash/replay corpus required; acceptance/delivery E2E deferred.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer verifies candidate package binding and rework lineage only; that evidence verdict cannot transition Accepted. GF-040 requires a separate independent exact-candidate acceptance package and GF-044 landing proof. Stale/partial/self-authored basis fails closed.

## DR choices

DR-3/DR-9 only. New rework policy, ordinal reuse or standing session authority requires owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

Size M; single oracle is exact candidate plus fresh-rework lineage. Paths: schemas/transitions/fixtures/evidence; review/delivery excluded. Keep one PR unless session realization crosses a separate mechanism family.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-014/030–034 merged with exact basis, capacity, bounds and provider facts.
- Candidate/assignment writer, authority, failures, evidence and CF oracles explicit.
- DR-3/DR-9 and D16 fresh-session rule recorded.

## Definition of Done

- Candidates bind exact durable facts and every rework has fresh fenced logical session.
- Exhaustion blocks without acceptance/landing/release.
- Required evidence, diff check, checks, CI and independent review pass.
