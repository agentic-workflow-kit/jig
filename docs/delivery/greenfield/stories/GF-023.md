# GF-023 — Effect-free preview and distinct owner approvals

## Outcome, value, and why now

Provide exact effect-free preview and separately authenticated Arye proposal and provider-manifest
approvals. It releases a fully evidenced, owner-bound envelope candidate for GF-024; it cannot
create a Run, acknowledgement, authority, or dispatch.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`EP-PREVIEW` resolves the same candidate path read-only; `EP-APPROVE` binds only the proposal;
`PORT-CONSUMER` authenticates Arye; `SCH-ENVELOPE` carries the frozen candidate; `FC-INPUT` rejects
wrong bytes. `CF-ENVELOPE`, `CF-CONSUMER`, and the negative authority corpus prove the boundary.
`PC-README-3`, `PC-JIG-1`, `PC-JIG-2`, `PC-JIG-3`, `PC-JIG-7`, `PC-JIG-19`, `PC-JIG-23`, and
`PC-CONCEPTS-1` map envelope/configuration/consumer routes. `CFG-1`, `CFG-2`, `CFG-3`, `CFG-4`,
`CFG-5`, `CFG-6`, `CFG-7`, `CFG-8`, `CFG-9`, `CFG-10` retain the governance/profile/track/
computed/guidance/preset/open/prompt/freshness/posture owner commitments.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

GF-014 supplies bound evidence, GF-019 supplies the validated source contract, GF-021 supplies
composition, and GF-022 supplies provider-manifest/admission semantics. The GF-014, GF-019, and
GF-021 implementation predecessors must be contained in the externally recorded execution
base/tuple; the GF-022 owner/DR decision basis must be recorded and revalidated there.
The development posture may bind only a semantic-only manifest with `providerEnabled: false`,
`dispatchEnabled: false`, and fail-closed no-autonomous-restore recovery. Preview success or either
approval does not satisfy the other approval requirement. A real provider remains unavailable
until its GF-020/GF-025/GF-026 evidence closes separately.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is preview report, exact digest display, consumer authentication, immutable proposal approval,
and distinct immutable manifest approval lookup. Source/provider realization, proof production,
setup, intake, ledger mutation, Run creation, lifecycle/effects, and any self-approval are excluded.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The external Builder receives `PORT-CONSUMER` actions. Only authenticated Arye may bind the exact
proposal digest/scope; the distinct manifest action binds only exact manifest digest/scope. Preview
returns a report and may read existing pre-Run evidence but cannot write intake/runtime authority.
No consumer, provider, report, or approval can transition, dispatch, decide, publish, review,
finalize, create `SCH-ENVELOPE` acceptance, or create a Run.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is an exact input-bound report and, independently, immutable exact owner approval records.
Wrong identity/digest/scope, stale proof, or conflicting replay is typed consumer/input failure or
unavailable; it is not a Story outcome and not an intake acknowledgement. No `SCH-ENVELOPE` here
creates a Run, effect, grant, or dispatch.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

`FC-INPUT` covers invalid candidate/binding. Existing bounded proof facts are reconciled through
their exact records; a lost approval response reads its immutable record, never recreates changed
bytes. Recovery recomposes/revalidates current basis; changed proposal or manifest needs fresh
approval. There is no effect allocation or cleanup.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Authenticate Arye, canonicalize exact fields, reject unknown/oversize/cross-scope/stale data, and
redact source/prompt/proof diagnostics. Resolve no provider/workspace credential. Proposal approval
cannot substitute for manifest approval; manifest approval cannot approve proposal; preview cannot
entitle intake. The consumer guard and all four development dependencies prevent pre-Run authority
widening: the three implementation predecessors must be contained and the GF-022 decision record
must be current. The three real-provider qualification gates remain separately required before
provider activation.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver consumer request/report/approval schemas, authenticated guards, immutable lookup records,
negative-authority corpus, and evidence. `DR-4` owner **Engineering + configuration** selects only
consumer transport; `DR-5` owner **Configuration** consumes only previously admitted providers.
Unavailable is fallback; Arye retains widening and plan selection.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test identical preview path, exact bindings, Arye/non-Arye, proposal/manifest non-substitution,
stale/change/replay/lost-response, redaction, dependency gate absence, and no ledger/Run/event/
grant/dispatch. Record candidate/input/approval/proof/provider/suite/probe metadata for named
suites. Provider E2E is evidence consumption only.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires exact candidate/target, principal, input/proposal/manifest/approval
digests/scopes, the three implementation-predecessor records, the current GF-022 decision record,
corpus findings, and delivery metadata. Exact no-finding review is required; acceptance remains
distinct from landing and intake.

## DR choices

`DR-4`: **Engineering + configuration**, consumer transport under fixed principal/no-authority
rules, unavailable fallback. `DR-5`: **Configuration**, the development manifest binds no reachable
provider; any real provider still requires its exact qualification gate. Arye owns widening and
plan selection.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`M`: one consumer/approval non-substitution oracle. Do not split proposal from manifest approval;
their separation is the safety proof. Intake is excluded.

## Definition of Ready

- All four exact dependency edges and the semantic-only development posture are current.
- IDs/routes/imports, principal authority, durable approvals, failures, and oracles are named.
- Preview/approvals are structurally unable to create a Run or dispatch.

## Definition of Done

- Exact approvals remain separate and preview remains effect-free.
- Required replay, hostile, authority, and evidence proof passes.
- Exact review, checks/CI, and `git diff --check` pass; no pre-Run path mints runtime authority.
