# GF-004 — Exact-subject conformance evidence harness

## Outcome, value, and why now

Create the versioned deterministic harness, catalog, recorder, and pure gate evaluator through which all 39 fixed `CF-*` suites later prove an exact realization/provider/product subject. Its value is prevention of a false pass: missing, stale, substituted, self-authored, or differently bound evidence cannot authorize a suite, provider, or product gate. It is earliest after the substrate, codec, and topology, and enables GF-005 to record its first deterministic/replay evidence. It does not implement a suite's business behavior or qualify a provider.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

[`architecture-conformance.md`](../../../redesign/design/architecture-conformance.md) owns the 39-suite catalog and the `CF-GATE-REALIZATION`, `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, and `CF-GATE-PRODUCT` gate semantics. This story carries every `CF-*` catalog key and `CF-GATE-PRODUCT`, but does not mark any as passing. `DR-8` owns the deterministic private testkit/harness choice. `runtime.md`, GF-002's versioned framing, and GF-003's fixed topology constrain the evidence subject: build/provider/manifest/environment/suite/probe versions must bind the exact candidate and no underdeclared crossing can be hidden by a report.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

GF-001, GF-002, and GF-003 must be merged and independently accepted with exact graph, canonical-vector, and six-unit/eleven-port evidence. Start evidence includes the complete 39-name catalog and product gate input order from `architecture-conformance.md`, the selected baseline and normative manifest, and a proof that no real provider is registered. A test runner branch, a future suite assertion, or a generic CI green badge is not sufficient.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: immutable suite catalog/versioning; exact-subject record schema; deterministic IDs/clocks/seeds; fixture and fault-injection interfaces; evidence recorder; freshness/binding validation; pure gate evaluator; reports and negative test fixtures.

Out: implementing the semantic or provider algorithms that each suite tests; selecting a concrete provider; accepting a realization/product; changing the catalog; judging a reviewer finding; dispatching effects; and treating code coverage or a test exit status as a gate. The harness is not a policy engine and does not invent omitted inputs.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The harness is a private test/evidence unit outside the six runtime units. It consumes the exact candidate content digest, baseline/tree relation, toolchain/build digest, manifest/provider/environment identity where applicable, suite and adversarial-probe versions, fixture digest, deterministic clock/seed, and declared observation results. It emits an immutable evidence record and one of `pass`, `fail`, or `not-recordable`; it cannot emit an implicit `not-run = pass`.

Evidence records are durable test artifacts, not `RT-EVIDENCE` product facts and not a port. They carry no lifecycle authority: only later named gates consume validated records. The fence is total validation: a missing suite, duplicate/ambiguous key, unknown version, mismatched subject, self-attested result, stale environment, incomplete probe set, or absent evidence is non-passing. The pure evaluator has no dispatch or configuration API, so it cannot make a provider reachable.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a reproducible catalog capable of recording every fixed `CF-*` suite and evaluating realization/provider/product gates against a complete exact input set. Its output proves that a pass is tied to one subject and that any absent/stale/substituted result blocks the relevant gate. It releases evidence support for GF-005 and later provider qualification; it releases no provider configuration or product readiness claim.

Terminal outcomes are a recorded `pass`, recorded `fail`, or `not-recordable`/gate-closed. `Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, `NotRun`, `Stopped`, acceptance, landing, and lifecycle transitions are not representable. A gate-closed result must not be rendered as a green summary.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Typed harness failures distinguish invalid input/binding (`FC-INPUT`/`FC-SUBJECT` vocabulary where later semantic consumers need it), missing/invalid evidence (`FC-EVIDENCE`), and untrusted or inconsistent environment/provenance (`FC-TRUST`). They are evidence classifications only, not product events. Deterministic test timeouts/faults record a failed or incomplete probe with their bound; the harness never treats a timeout as success. A runner crash preserves append-only partial artifacts marked incomplete; resume reconstructs from immutable fixture/event logs and records a new attempt, never fills an uncertain observation. Cleanup may delete only unreferenced disposable local test outputs; required evidence is retained by content digest. No external effect is retried.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate schema versions, exact candidate/build/provider/manifest/environment bindings, suite/probe completeness, clock/seed origin, and artifact digests before gate evaluation. Test credentials are prohibited unless a later provider story supplies an approved, in-memory-only test context; evidence records/logs redact secrets and quarantine hostile payloads. A provider's own test result never mints its qualification: the harness requires the named independent evidence binding and gate inputs. There is no adapter registration path; an unqualified adapter remains unreachable even if it supplies a passing-looking report.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

- A versioned inventory of all 39 `CF-*` suites and their realization/provider/product gate membership.
- Strict evidence and candidate metadata schema plus append-only recorder/readback validation.
- Deterministic ID, clock, seed, fault, crash, and adversarial fixture interfaces.
- A pure complete-input evaluator for `CF-GATE-REALIZATION`, `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, and `CF-GATE-PRODUCT`, with explicit missing/stale/fail outcomes.
- Negative fixtures for absent suite, stale evidence, digest substitution, provider self-attestation, clock/environment drift, and a false-green summary attempt.

`DR-8`: Engineering selects a private deterministic, Vitest-compatible testkit/harness under the conformance contract. Evidence is deterministic rerun/fault/crash reports and the false-pass corpus; fallback is no gate claim. It does not dictate individual suite algorithms, provider protocols, or acceptance policy.

## Test and evidence obligations

Required: deterministic unit/schema tests for catalog/records; contract tests for every suite key and gate membership; adversarial missing/stale/substitution/self-attestation tests; replay/permutation tests for deterministic ordering; crash/fault tests before record, after record, and before gate evaluation; timeout/incomplete-observation tests; and pure gate tests proving no false pass. Provider and E2E product tests are N/A: this story has no provider/effect/product flow; prove it through no-adapter registration and fixture-only execution. Record exact candidate/baseline/tree, harness/build digest, suite/probe catalog versions, fixtures/seeds/clocks, environment/manifest/provider fields or explicit scripted status, observations, and evaluator result.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer verifies that all 39 fixed catalog entries are present exactly once; all three gates are total conjunctions over their declared inputs; any absent/stale/ambiguous/failing result closes rather than passes; and evidence binds exact content and delivery metadata. The acceptance package contains candidate digest, catalog digest, generated gate matrix, negative corpus results, deterministic rerun/crash evidence, findings state, and reviewer identity. Approval of the harness is not a realization/provider/product pass or a landing proof.

## DR choices

`DR-8`: question—what deterministic private harness records conformance? Owner—Engineering. Selected bounded option—versioned private testkit with deterministic clocks/IDs/fault fixtures and exact evidence records. Constraints—fixed 39-suite catalog, gate totality, no false pass, exact-subject binding. Deferred alternatives—hosted or provider-defined test reporting. Failure fallback—record failure/incomplete and close the gate. Catalog change or new gate input is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

**M.** One cohesive oracle: all declared gate inputs are exact and complete or no pass is possible. Expected paths are testkit/catalog/recorder/evaluator/fixtures. Do not combine real suite/provider implementation. Split runner mechanics from record format only if each validates the same immutable evidence fixture and neither opens a gate alone; otherwise retain one PR.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-001–003 are merged with exact graph, codec, and topology proof.
- The 39-suite catalog, gate memberships, metadata bindings, `DR-8` constraints, and false-pass fixtures are enumerated.
- No provider/adapter registration surface exists.
- An independent reviewer can reconstruct a gate result from one candidate package.

## Definition of Done

- Catalog/records are versioned; missing, stale, invalid, or substituted evidence fails closed.
- Deterministic, adversarial, replay, crash, timeout, and gate-totality proof passes on the exact candidate.
- No suite absent from execution can appear passed; no provider becomes configurable.
- Exact evidence, `git diff --check`, repository checks/CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
