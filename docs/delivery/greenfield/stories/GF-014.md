# GF-014 — Evidence admission and exact-subject binding

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver immutable, attributable evidence admission whose subject is exactly one existing Run, Story, Candidate, Operation, or target fact plus one claim. It gives later review/acceptance a trustworthy input but grants neither acceptance, disposal, target, provider, nor lifecycle authority. Oversize, secret-bearing, stale, wrong-subject, or unverifiable material is quarantined/rejected, never silently normalized into proof.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`guarantees.md` maps this to `SEC-1` (secrets absent from durable trail) and `MERGE-1` (independent evidence, never self-report alone). `evidence-handling.md` owns storage, attribution, integrity, redaction, quarantine and retention holds; `data-and-identity.md` fixes `ID-EVSUBJ`, `SCH-EVIDENCE`, `ID-MANIFEST`, structural secret absence and immutable schema responsibility; control-plane assigns `CP-EVIDENCE` binding and `CP-TRANSITION` adoption after matching artifact fact; runtime retains `RT-EVIDENCE`. `CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE` and `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE` are required oracles. `DR-1` affects encoding only; `DR-6` preserves artifact contract/routing from GF-013.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start after GF-002 merged canonical URI/schema vectors, GF-005 writer/adoption rules, and GF-013 exact protected/disposable artifact plus witness evidence. Verify baseline, selected evidence schema version, approved provider manifest identity when a producer is named, exact artifact-fact binding, quarantine context and retention-hold configuration. A raw artifact, provider assertion, filename, or digest without the exact subject is not evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: `SCH-EVIDENCE` schema/admission, exact subject/producer attribution, immutable manifests, bounded content classes, digest availability/integrity verification, redaction/quarantine, and retention holds. Exclude judgments of sufficiency, review/verdict/acceptance, export publication, final disposal, provider qualification, direct object routing mechanics, and all lifecycle/effect decisions.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`CP-EVIDENCE` consumes attributed material through `PORT-ARTIFACT`/`CP-MEDIATOR`, binds `ID-EVSUBJ`, `ID-MANIFEST` where applicable, producer identity, claim/completeness, size/oversize class, content digest and immutable artifact facts, then asks `CP-TRANSITION` to adopt only the matching durable fact. Persist immutable manifests, artifact digest references, producer and exact-subject/claim bindings, redaction/quarantine disposition, retention hold and admitted/rejected status. `RT-EVIDENCE` stores bytes; `CP-EVIDENCE` may bind but cannot accept a Candidate, create a verdict, grant authority, release/erase a protected address, or decide a lifecycle transition. Provider facts are assertions requiring validation, not authority.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a durable immutable evidence manifest whose exact URI resolves one existing subject and claim, whose producer/manifest/bounded artifact digest is validated and available, and whose adoption is tied to matching `EV-ARTIFACT-FACT`. Admitted evidence is a proof input only. Terminal non-delivery for an item is `Rejected` or quarantined evidence with typed reason and preserved audit metadata; no Run/Story `Blocked`, `Parked`, `NotRun`, `Stopped`, `Accepted`, or `Landed` is produced by this story. Unverifiable evidence cannot be upgraded by later metadata.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad URI/existing-identity/claim/schema/version/size is `FC-INPUT` or `FC-SUBJECT`; missing/wrong digest/producer/manifest/freshness/completeness/redaction is `FC-EVIDENCE`; stale operation binding is `FC-FENCE`; protected/disposable routing or witness discontinuity is inherited `FC-TRUST`. `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`/`BND-RETRY` govern artifact observations; `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` governs adopted artifact facts and exhaustion enters Recovery. Record intent/admission basis before effectful storage requests (GF-015 mediates real effects); a lost/read uncertainty re-reads immutable artifact/manifest facts. Same-effect retry needs confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization; otherwise quarantine/preserve and park/escalate the owning future scope. On restart rebuild evidence indexes from immutable facts and revalidate artifact integrity/holds; never infer admission from bytes alone. No cleanup/disposal is authorized; quarantine and retained evidence are preserved until later governed retirement.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Canonicalize and length-limit subjects/metadata before digesting; validate exactly one pre-existing subject path and one claim, producer identity, manifest, content type/size, and immutable digest. Credential names resolve in memory only; schemas have no secret-bearing field and admission scans/redacts/quarantines hostile payloads before durable evidence. Restrict evidence access by manifest/principal least privilege and retain redacted metadata/holds. Missing, stale, self-attested, ambiguous, or invalid proof fails closed; a provider, evidence result, quarantine release, or fallback never mints acceptance/lifecycle/provider authority. Artifact adapters remain unreachable unless GF-013 qualification stays current.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Add `packages/evidence-contract/`, `packages/evidence-admission/`, `packages/testkit/evidence/`, schema vectors, redaction/quarantine fixtures, and `evidence/conformance/{CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE,CF-SECRET-ABSENCE}/` results. **DR-1:** Engineering selects canonical wire encoding only while every field semantic, unknown rejection, version and exact binding remain unchanged; evidence is cross-runtime vectors; fallback rejects input. **DR-6:** GF-013's immutable/routing/witness constraints apply; evidence is its exact artifact evidence; fallback preserves/quarantines and disables admission. Any secret exception, subject grammar, retention/disposal policy, or authority effect needs owner decision.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test URI scope and nonexistence/wrong-claim vectors; manifest/producer mismatch; stale/replayed substitution; digest/content/type/size mismatch; immutable readback; admission-after-artifact-fact ordering; all oversize behaviors; hostile encodings; secret/credential/token absence in records/logs/exports; redaction/quarantine and retention hold persistence; crash/lost ack/restart reconstruction; negative authority that evidence cannot accept/release/dispose. `CF-EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE` oracle checks immutable exact attribution and hold/quarantine behavior; `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE` proves schemas and emitted evidence contain no secret. Record candidate and subject/artifact/manifest digests, producer identity, scan policy/version, fixture/probe version, redacted result, and check/CI metadata. Provider/E2E acceptance is excluded because no verdict/landing route exists.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer checks the exact candidate digest, schemas/fixtures, artifact binding evidence, hostile/redaction corpus, both conformance packages, and CI/check metadata. A self-authored or stale scan, another subject/manifest, partial evidence, unresolved finding, or secret-bearing output fails closed. Acceptance is a code/document candidate review only; it cannot mean Candidate acceptance or landing, which require later separate authority and landing proof.

## DR choices

DR-1 and DR-6 are bounded above. No algorithm may invent a sufficiency rule or turn quarantine/admission into acceptance; absent an owner decision the conservative fallback is reject/preserve.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

**M.** One oracle: exact-subject immutable evidence admission with secret absence. Paths are evidence schema/admission/testkit/fixtures/evidence. Do not split schema from admission unless schema remains closed/unusable and both halves retain the same exact-subject oracle; artifact realization, acceptance, export, and disposal are excluded.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-002/GF-005/GF-013 merge evidence and exact artifact/manifest/quarantine/hold bases are current.
- Subject, writer/adopter, durable facts, typed failures, bounds, recovery, security, and CF oracles are enumerated.
- DR-1/DR-6/fallback and unreachability are recorded.

## Definition of Done

- Immutable exact-subject admission, attribution, redaction/quarantine, retention hold, and no-secret structures are implemented/evidenced.
- All uncertainty rejects or preserves; evidence cannot widen any authority or authorize cleanup.
- Exact conformance, hostile/crash/recovery tests, `pnpm check`, `git diff --check`, CI, and independent review pass.
