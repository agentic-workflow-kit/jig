# GF-022 — Provider manifest and capability-proof admission

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver the owner-approved provider-manifest and bounded capability-proof admission layer. It lets
composition prove only an exact provider/build/environment/scope is eligible, while keeping all
unsupported modes unconfigurable. It intentionally does not define Work Source semantics or make a
provider configurable; GF-020 consumes this gate after GF-019.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`mechanism-and-provider-contracts.md` owns `MC-AUTHORITY`, provider scopes, capability exchange,
and no-middleman posture; `envelope-production.md` owns `EP-PROVIDERS` and distinct manifest
approval; D14 forbids privilege/credential widening. `data-and-identity.md` owns
`ID-MANIFEST`, `ID-PRINCIPAL`, `SCH-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY`, and `SCH-CAPABILITY-PROOF` variants;
`persistence-and-projections.md` owns `LG-PREFLIGHT-ATTEMPT` and says it is not authority.
`CF-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY` and `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE` are oracles. `DR-4`/`DR-5` remain below them.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only after GF-004's exact suite recorder, GF-013's protected artifact/preflight primitive and
witness evidence, and GF-021's immutable policy/bounds composition are merged on the selected
baseline. Require owner designation of Arye's configured `ID-PRINCIPAL`, initial provider list,
and the exact manifest candidate; require a current provider/build/environment basis. A proposal
approval, conformance assertion, or unapproved manifest is not admission evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: canonical manifest declaration, distinct Arye-only manifest approval, admission
evaluation, exact qualifying evidence references, bounded capability-proof start/result exchanges,
and unreachability registry. Exclude provider protocol/source semantics, actual provider adapters,
proposal approval, preview, setup execution, `SCH-ENVELOPE`, intake/Run creation, and any fallback
authority.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The Builder's `EP-PROVIDERS` consumes an authenticated `PORT-CONSUMER` manifest-approval action;
only configured Arye `ID-PRINCIPAL` may bind exact canonical `SCH-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY` digest/scope
to `ID-MANIFEST`. It conditionally records `SCH-CAPABILITY-PROOF` start before and result after one
mechanism exchange under `LG-PREFLIGHT-ATTEMPT`, keyed by provider/build/manifest/environment/
capability/basis/ordinal. Builder validates/adopts positive proof; provider supplies facts only.
Neither proof, manifest, preflight evidence, nor configuration may create a Run, event, Transition,
grant, dispatch, or approval of an envelope proposal. Guarded registry construction blocks provider
instantiation until exact approval, evidence, scope, posture, and proof all match.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a current, immutable Arye-bound manifest plus a positive exact-subject capability proof
and qualifying conformance evidence, all referenced by composition. It releases eligibility only.
Missing, changed, stale, negative, insufficient, policy-incompatible, wrong-principal/scope/
posture/build/environment, timeout, exhausted, or byte-mismatched records produce unavailable or
pre-Run `Rejected`/`NotRun`. No provider operation, Run, approval, ledger intake, or dispatch is
possible.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad identity/binding is `FC-SUBJECT`/`FC-AUTHORITY`; malformed proof is `FC-INPUT`; missing/stale
evidence is `FC-EVIDENCE`; exchange fault is `FC-MECHANISM`; corrupt proof/witness is `FC-TRUST`.
Immutable start/result variants carry start/deadline/predecessor and `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` 5s–2h plus
`BND-RETRY` 1–5 consumption. Loss/crash reads the same variant bytes; a next ordinal needs a proved
terminal/deadline predecessor. Same-key replay must be byte equivalent. Unknown outcome is
reconciled by exact readback, never blindly re-requested; exhaustion leaves no positive proof.
Protected attempt records are preserved, non-disposable, and resume only by validated chain.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical manifest/approval/proof bytes, strict scope, resource, native posture,
environment, capability, age, and evidence bindings. Credentials appear only as approved names and
are resolved in memory by the configured provider; manifest validation rejects forge/privileged
delivery credential classes and unauthorized filesystem/network/subprocess authority. Redact proof
payloads/logs and quarantine hostile provider facts. A changed byte or scope is a new unapproved
subject. Provider results, evidence, proposal approval, and fallback never widen authority; gates
make unqualified adapters structurally unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver manifest/approval/proof schemas, guarded admission evaluator, immutable attempt protocol,
registry/unreachability guard, failure/recovery tests, and exact evidence records. `DR-4` permits
typed mechanism transport only beneath fixed exchange semantics, evidenced by relevant `CF-MECH-*`;
fallback is unavailable. `DR-5` lets Configuration select concrete providers only after this exact
admission and their qualification; evidence is gate record, fallback omitted provider. No provider
semantics, privilege escalation, or secret transport is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test canonical approval identity/scope and proposal-non-substitution; manifest byte/scope/build/
posture/environment/age mismatch; secret absence/redaction; proof positive/negative/stale/
timeout/exhausted cases; variant/predecessor/ordinal replay and crash readback; witness/integrity
faults; registry unreachability; and negative authority for Run/event/grant/dispatch. Record
manifest/approval/proof handles/digests, provider/build/environment, suite/probe/version, bound
consumption, and candidate digest for `CF-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY`, `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE`, and relevant
provider gate. Real adapter tests are deferred to its story.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires exact candidate/baseline digest, Arye identity binding, manifest/scope,
proof chain/handles, evidence/gate metadata, adversarial results, and delivery metadata. All
findings must be resolved; self-attestation, stale evidence, or proposal approval fails. Acceptance
does not land or make any adapter configurable without its own exact provider gate and merge proof.

## DR choices

- `DR-4` — Engineering/configuration; typed exchange transport only; immutable attempt/authority
  rules; proof/conformance evidence; unavailable on uncertainty.
- `DR-5` — Configuration; initial provider selection is deferred to each provider story; immutable
  manifest/posture/gate constraints; gate evidence; omit rather than configure on failure.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

`L`, one admission oracle. Expected paths are manifest/approval/proof/admission/guard/evidence.
Split `GF-022a` manifest+unreachability from `GF-022b` proof machinery only if a remains green with
all provider construction disabled; do not combine a real provider.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-004/GF-013/GF-021 and owner principal/initial manifest evidence are exact and merged.
- Principals, ports, durable attempt facts, bounds, authority fence, failures, and `CF-*` proofs are named.
- `DR-4`/`DR-5` are recorded and every unqualified provider is unreachable.
- An independent reviewer can reproduce admission from the package.

## Definition of Done

- Exact owner manifest approval and current positive proof are both necessary and sufficient only
  for eligibility, not Run authority.
- Bounded readback/recovery preserves attempts and never blindly retries uncertainty.
- Required authority/secret/replay/crash/negative tests and evidence pass.
- Exact diff/checks/CI and independent review pass; no unsupported provider mode is configurable.
