# GF-041 — Pre-acceptance review publication

## Outcome, value, and why now

Publish an exact Candidate on a dedicated review ref and draft non-mergeable request with stable status/comment markers, enabling review before acceptance. GF-015 fences effects, GF-035 supplies Candidate identity, and GF-040 consumes the fixed publication observation. It grants no acceptance, finalization, target, merge, landing, dependency release, or real-provider configuration; GF-057 alone qualifies review publication.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

D15 authorizes the bounded review capability. Forge and landing defines `OPC-REV-PUBLISH`,
`OPC-REV-REQUEST`, `OPC-REV-STATUS`, `OPC-REV-COMMENT`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-REF`,
`OPC-REV-RETIRE-REQUEST`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-STATUS`, and `OPC-REV-RETIRE-COMMENT`; all are review
operations and forbid target lineage, target mutation, ID-AUTH, merge, and final-delivery
operations. Review execution binds the fixed observed tuple into RP-PACKAGE.
CF-REVIEW-PUBLICATION and the review subset of CF-MECH-DELIVERY are the proof oracle.

`RT-CONTROLLER` writes the authorized review transition; `PORT-DELIVERY` is the sole effect edge;
`EV-TARGET-FACT` is the redacted attested ref/request/marker fact; and `FC-EFFECT` identifies an
uncertain or contradictory publication effect. `PC-JIG-11` and `PC-CONCEPTS-6` require this
review-publication capability without landing power. `MERGE-1` requires independent
policy-aligned evidence; `MERGE-2` reserves push, PR creation, and merge to the runner; `MERGE-3`
makes done conditions policy-bound; `MERGE-4` separates done from merged; and `MERGE-5` requires
safe Blocked-work surfacing in the draft venue.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Freeze merged GF-015/035/040 evidence plus Candidate and target-basis digests, policy review mode, canonical ref/request/marker names, capability-manifest digest, and Transition, ID-OP, and fence per operation. The only mechanism is scripted; missing/stale capability or changed Candidate fails closed rather than blindly recreating a request.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: publish/update exact review ref, draft request, stable status/comment, readback/reconciliation, and preservation-safe review-venue retirement. Exclude reviewer verdict, acceptance, verification, finalizer/anchor, all final delivery, provider setup, and notice UI. A blocked Candidate may be surfaced but publication cannot change its outcome.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-CONTROLLER and CP-MEDIATOR record intent then invoke PORT-DELIVERY; provider only attests. Inputs are exact SCH-CANDIDATE, Transition, ID-OP, CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION, redacted correlation keys, and fixed observation tuple. Ledger writes ref, draft request, markers, effect certainty, and digest. Only controller plus D15-authorized Transition dispatches; provider/reviewer/worker/projection cannot mint verdict, ID-AUTH, target authority, or delivery authority.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is the exact digest on dedicated ref, an attested draft/non-mergeable request, and one stable marker context usable by GF-040; Story remains non-Accepted/non-Landed. Effect uncertainty reconciles and indeterminate publication parks; a required review path that cannot complete after its bounded attempts blocks. Only a direct Blocked/Rejected dependency makes Pending `NotRun`; only the exact Story-bound owner reject-story action makes `Rejected`; missing provider/start facts do neither. Run Stopped is an overlay. Retirement removes venue only after preservation and never releases dependents or manufactures outcome.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Wrong subject/capability yields FC-SUBJECT or FC-AUTHORITY; bad fact FC-MECHANISM; lost/contradictory effect FC-EFFECT; bound expiry is FC-BOUND. Record intent first. Reconcile publish/request/status/comment by exact ref-content digest or stable marker; same effect retries only after confirmed absence and recorded reauthorization, otherwise park/preserve. An indeterminate effect at BND-WAIT-MECHANISM parks; a required review that remains incomplete at its bounded end blocks. BND-RETIRE expiry retains the venue and creates a GF-046 obligation. Recovery rebuilds correlation facts.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical bounded Candidate/ref/target/marker/fence, draft posture, freshness, and response subject. Credential names resolve only in provider memory; ledger holds redacted non-secret correlation keys, never tokens or URLs. Bound/redact text and quarantine hostile output. Fixture has no credentials/configuration path; provider success cannot approve, finalize, land, or widen scope.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver D15 typed operation contracts, immutable observation tuple/digest, intent/reconciliation guards, scripted delivery fixture, redaction/marker rules, and CF catalog. DR-4 selects typed semantic port and edge transport with mechanism evidence/unavailable fallback. DR-5 defers GitHub review-publication selection to GF-057; no mergeability or final-delivery algorithm is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Unit/schema tests cover all eight operations, tuple, markers. Contract tests prove exact subject/fence. Negative-authority tests attempt merge, anchor, verdict, and release. Adversarial tests cover non-draft request, wrong digest, stale correlation, duplicate markers, secret output; replay/crash/recovery covers every effect and preserved retirement. Scripted E2E proves publication without Accepted/Landed; record exact build/manifest/environment/fixture/CER and run both CF suites.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review freezes candidate/tree/path set and publication tuple: Candidate/basis, ref/request/draft attestation, markers, ID-OP/fence/effect facts, redacted correlation, retirement evidence, DR and CF results. PASS proves no acceptance/finalization/landing/configuration authority; it is neither runtime review approval nor landing proof.

## DR choices

DR-4: Engineering plus configuration, typed PORT-DELIVERY contract, exact-subject/fence proof, unavailable fallback. DR-5: Configuration, real review-publication provider only at GF-057 with current gate; no provider selection here. No other DR applies.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L; one D15 review-only authority oracle across contract, guards, fixture, tests, catalog. Exclude acceptance, verify, finalizer, real forge, retirement orchestrator. GF-041a semantic fixture may precede GF-057 only while green and unconfigurable.

## Definition of Ready

- All named predecessor commits are contained in the externally recorded execution base/tuple with current exact subject evidence.
- Governing IDs, port, principal, fence, durable facts, typed failures, finite bounds, recovery route, oracle, DR record, and PR boundary have independent readiness review.
- Unqualified providers/effect paths are unreachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Versioned immutable inputs validate and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every effect; uncertain effect is reconciled by stable identity and only confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization permits same-effect retry.
- Required unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash, timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence is exact-subject-bound; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
