# GF-037 — Rule invalidation and Run control

## Outcome, value, and why now

Implement frozen-input/rule-surface revalidation and authenticated suspend, resume, and D16's two catalogued terminal-stop entries: an authenticated explicit terminal decision from `Suspended` after confirming that no resumable Transition remains, or a qualified `FC-TRUST` recovery observation from `Interrupted / Recovering` while a trustworthy witnessed append basis remains. Either terminal-stop Transition atomically opens D16's durable `SCH-SETTLEMENT` overlay with its complete deterministic duty inventory plus the first permitted next-duty intents and their exact bases, without advancing any duty. This story provides no Settlement-duty progress or completion, acceptance, delivery, or landing, and GF-050 later consumes and advances that already-open overlay.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

lifecycle-catalogs.md owns EV-RULE-SURFACE-TOUCHED and three Run decision events; lifecycle flow and D16 own Active/Parked→Suspended, Suspended→Active/Parked, the guarded `Suspended` → `Stopped` owner-decision edge, and the exceptional trustworthy-witnessed-basis `Interrupted / Recovering` → `Stopped` `FC-TRUST` edge to the same terminal overlay. Both catalogued terminal entries preserve Story states, fence dispatch/retained finalizer authority, and atomically create the durable `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened` carrier under `ID-SETTLEMENT` with its complete deterministic duty inventory and first permitted next-duty intents/bases. Opening authorizes but does not advance any duty. No direct `Active`, `Parked`, or `Settling` → `Stopped` edge exists, and resumable `Suspended` cannot stop. DR-3/DR-9 do not permit a new run state, timeout policy, or generic automatic stop.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: touched-rule detection, invalidation, frozen revalidation, dispatch suspension, resumed new generation, and either catalogued atomic terminal-stop Transition that opens the phase-preserving `SCH-SETTLEMENT` overlay with its complete deterministic duty inventory plus first permitted next-duty intents and exact bases. Exclude advancing or completing any Settlement duty, policy authoring, acceptance, delivery, retirement, and every automatic stop other than D16's witnessed-basis `FC-TRUST` recovery entry. A direct stop from `Active`, `Parked`, or `Settling`, or from `Suspended` while any resumable Transition remains, is explicitly out of scope and invalid.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-CONTROLLER/CP-TRANSITION validates rule-surface digest, affected subject and ID-GEN, writes touched/invalidation and decision events to witnessed ledger, and for terminal stop atomically commits the catalogued `Stopped` Transition plus `SCH-SETTLEMENT opened`/`ID-SETTLEMENT`, complete deterministic duty inventory, first permitted next-duty intents with their exact authorization bases, preserved Story snapshot, and dispatch/finalizer fences. That atomic commit advances no duty. `PORT-DECIDE` supplies the authenticated owner/current-grant decision from GF-036; it never directly changes state. The controller alone suspends/resumes/stops and opens the overlay, either from explicit terminal intent under that current exact grant while `Suspended` only after proving no resumable Transition remains, or from a verified `FC-TRUST` recovery observation while in `Interrupted / Recovering` with the required trustworthy witnessed append basis; provider/projection cannot. New generation and accepted-successor fence bind resume.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Touched frozen rule invalidates dependent evidence/authority and parks before unsafe dispatch. Suspend immediately fences dispatch, preserves Story state and retains finalization grant fenced until target-changing operations reconcile. Resume passes RC-RESUME-INTEGRITY under new generation or parks for exact reapproval. The non-resumable-`Suspended` explicit terminal decision or D16's qualified `Interrupted / Recovering` `FC-TRUST` recovery observation atomically creates one durable preserved-state Settlement overlay with every duty and its first permitted next-duty intent/basis, but no duty progress; it is not a Story outcome or settlement completion, and GF-050 may only consume/advance/seal that carrier. No direct stop from `Active`, `Parked`, or `Settling`, no stop while a `Suspended` Run remains resumable, and no acceptance/Landed/release occurs.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Invalid rule/input FC-RULES/FC-INPUT; stale generation FC-FENCE; untrusted decision FC-AUTHORITY; shared-truth `FC-TRUST` enters Recovery. Only the D16-selected recovery observation from `Interrupted / Recovering` with a trustworthy witnessed append basis may atomically stop/open the overlay. When that basis is absent or untrustworthy, fence and halt outside the ledger, preserve resources, and surface the trust-root loss for externally governed recovery; append no observation, event, `Stopped` claim, or overlay. An already-durable prior `FC-TRUST` fact may be retained, but absence of append authority cannot create one. Uncertain target effect retains fence and reconciles before authority release; no dispatch while suspended and no blind retry. BND-WAIT-DECISION/RECOVERY behavior is GF-032. Recovery reconstructs touched basis and decisions; cleanup/obligations deferred.
