# GF-060 — Qualified Codex role-session provider

## Outcome, value, and why now

Propose qualification for one exact Codex implementer/reviewer role-session provider build, protocol, and
native permission posture for the proposed local profile. It remains inactive until GF-022 admission and the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` bind the tuple, then may make the provider
configurable only after real evidence proves session attribution, Doorbell routing, and permission
boundary behavior. It follows envelope/provider authority and lifecycle/review/verification facts;
it neither infers a sandbox/egress guarantee nor grants Codex Jig lifecycle, acceptance, or delivery
authority.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`D14` freezes provider identity/build/native posture/filesystem/network semantics/approval mode and
states that provider-native permission enforcement stays provider-side. `mechanism-and-provider-
contracts.md` owns qualification and `PORT-SESSION`; `DR-4` and `DR-5` bound the proposed configuration/
provider mechanics pending external activation. No Codex capability, native reviewer, sandbox claim, or host fact changes
`CP-TRANSITION`, owner decision, verdict, or delivery authority.

Local mapping: `PORT-SESSION` is the only provider seam. `CB-SESSION` binds one role assignment,
its bounded inputs, one `ID-SESSION`, the admitted provider manifest/build/native posture, and the
exact principal/session lineage. `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes each session Operation and
`CP-MEDIATOR` validates role, principal, assignment, session, response subject, host/resource, and
manifest/posture before dispatch or adoption. The binding cannot cross principal, role,
assignment, session, host, provider posture, or response lineage, cannot be rebound after intent,
and cannot represent forge or privileged-delivery credentials or transfer session authority to
another port. `DR-4`/`DR-5` bound the configuration and provider realization;
`FC-MECHANISM`/`BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` contain confirmed pre-uncertainty launch/transport fault;
`FC-EFFECT`/`BND-RECOVERY` contain a lost or unverifiable post-dispatch session effect; `BND-RETRY`
bounds only confirmed-absent replacement; and
`CF-SECRET-ABSENCE`, `CF-PROVIDER-PERMISSION`, `CF-MECH-SESSION`, and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` prove
admission. `PC-JIG-8`, `PC-JIG-9`, `PC-JIG-15`, `PC-JIG-17`, `PC-JIG-21`, `PC-CONCEPTS-3`,
`PC-CONCEPTS-5`, `PC-USE-2`, and `PC-USE-5` are discharged only by qualified role sessions.
`FENCE-1`, `FENCE-2`, `FENCE-3`, `SEC-1`, `SEC-2`, `SEC-3`, `STACK-1`, `STACK-2`, `STACK-3`,
`STACK-4`, `STACK-5`, `STACK-6`, `STACK-7`, `DRIVE-1`, `DRIVE-2`, `DRIVE-3`, and `DRIVE-4` close
through exact posture, secrets, runtime boundary, and operator-session evidence, never inferred sandbox behavior.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-022 provider authority/envelope proof, GF-034 session lifecycle, GF-035 candidate
binding, GF-036 Doorbell/grant semantics, GF-040 review package, and GF-042 verification evidence,
each observed with exact commit/candidate and prerequisite suite output in this story's execution base/tuple. Require the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` to bind the proposed Codex build/protocol,
provider manifest digest, Execution Host identity/posture, configured role/principal, supported
filesystem/network/approval semantics, and credential reference names. If any is absent, stale,

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope is real-provider adapter qualification for bounded implementer/reviewer role sessions,
attribution, session fact/result/fault exchange, native human-needed Doorbell request, reconnect/loss
observation, and manifest gate. Excluded are asserting Codex universal sandbox/no-network behavior,
Jig reimplementing provider permission enforcement, automatic middleman approval, provider-native
action-as-Jig-Operation conversion, forge credentials, delivery, public provider support, and fallback
to another posture/provider.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The qualified adapter mediates `PORT-SESSION` to `RT-CONTROLLER`/`CP-MEDIATOR`; inputs bind the
complete `CB-SESSION`: provider manifest/build/posture, Execution Host, `ID-SESSION`,
`ID-PRINCIPAL`, role/assignment/attempt,
generation, exact subject, and producer dedup key. Outputs are validated session facts/results/faults
or an attributable human-needed request, committed through existing transitions. Ledger-backed
session/assignment/envelope/candidate facts are truth; provider process/correlation is
non-authoritative metadata. Codex performs only provider-native work under its frozen posture;
controller owns lifecycle, owner/grant path answers Doorbell requests, an independent reviewer owns
verdict, and finalizer/delivery remain elsewhere. The adapter rejects results from stale/replaced/
cross-role session lineage and any cross-principal, cross-assignment, cross-host, cross-posture, or
cross-manifest binding.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a current manifest-qualified session whose events bind exact role, subject, generation,
and provider posture; provider-native allowed/reviewed/rejected actions do not enter Jig. A human-
needed request becomes one durable `ID-PARK`, gets an exact authorized `PORT-DECIDE` answer, and only
the currently bound session may receive it. Loss, posture/build drift, unsupported request, malformed
result, or expired proof makes Codex unavailable/parked/blocked under existing rules, never a
weaker fallback. It cannot produce `Landed`, acceptance, or a new lifecycle outcome by itself.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Use `FC-INPUT` for malformed, unknown, noncanonical, oversized, or version-skew protocol/result;
`FC-SUBJECT` for wrong session/assignment/role subject; `FC-FENCE` for stale generation/session;
`FC-AUTHORITY` for manifest/principal/role/scope breach; `FC-EVIDENCE` for invalid qualification
proof; `FC-MECHANISM` for confirmed launch/transport failure or timeout before any session effect is
uncertain; `FC-EFFECT` for a lost, late, or unverifiable result after dispatch; and `FC-TRUST` for
witness failure. Record each exact session Operation intent before launch, cancel, or close.

`CP-MEDIATOR` starts `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` with the durable request and deadline. A confirmed
mechanism failure or timeout may consume the next `BND-RETRY` ordinal only after provider
observation proves the prior attempt had no effect and the controller records reauthorization. The
15-minute mechanism wait (range 5 seconds–2 hours) never renews; `BND-RETRY` has three attempts by
default (range 1–5), records every ordinal, and does not reset. Exhaustion with confirmed absence
and no effect uncertainty directly blocks the owning Story/Operation.

A lost or unverifiable post-dispatch session result is `FC-EFFECT`, retains the exact Operation and
session fence, and starts `BND-RECOVERY` under `CP-RECOVERY`. Its three observations by default
(range 1–5) never renew and permit no semantic effect retry. Reconcile with exact
session/assignment/attempt/marker and provider observation; exhaustion parks/escalates while the
fence and assignment/evidence remain preserved. Recover from ledger facts under controller
fencing; reconnect only where provider/contract supports the same bound session, and attested loss
requires the existing fenced replacement path. Cleanup closes/retires sessions through recorded
Operations and preserves uncertain resources.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate protocol/schema limits, canonical manifest, signed activation-bound provider proof freshness,
host/posture identity, exact role, fences, and dedup key. Resolve Codex/host credential names only in
memory, least privilege; prohibit forge/privileged-delivery credential classes in agent manifest or
session binding and secret-scan evidence/transcripts. Redact prompts, tokens, paths, and provider
metadata under policy. Do not claim unlisted sandbox/egress behavior: only the declared activation-bound
posture is an input, and missing/ambiguous proof fails closed. Unqualified Codex adapter cannot be
configured, and no session output widens a grant, policy, acceptance, or delivery power.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver private Codex `PORT-SESSION` adapter, pinned manifest/profile validator, launch/session fact
mapper, Doorbell bridge, effect reconciler, test host/protocol fixtures, qualification recorder, and
admission gate wiring. `DR-4` is implemented by Engineering + configuration and `DR-5` by
Configuration only after GF-022 admission and the exact external owner-ratification/activation record in `decisions.md` bind the profile; neither implementation owner may
widen it. Constraints D14 and existing port contracts are immutable; evidence is exact
real-provider corpus; fallback is unavailable provider. New permission classifier, posture claim,
or automatic responder is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`.

## Test and evidence obligations

Run real isolated-host provider tests for exact build/protocol/manifest/posture, implementer and
reviewer role attribution, stale/replaced/replayed result, Doorbell answer binding, provider-native
allowed/rejected/human-needed outcomes, disconnect/reconnect/loss/crash, cancellation/reconciliation,
credential rejection/secret absence, hostile protocol input, drift invalidation, and every
cross-principal/role/assignment/session/host/posture/manifest/response-lineage binding. Negative
tests reject forge/delivery credential classes and authority transfer to another port. Replay and
exhaustion tests prove durable intent, each `BND-RETRY` ordinal only after confirmed absence,
direct block when those attempts exhaust without effect uncertainty, `FC-EFFECT` entry after a lost
post-dispatch result, no semantic retry during every `BND-RECOVERY` observation, and retained fence
plus park/escalation on recovery exhaustion. Require
`CF-MECH-SESSION`, `CF-PROVIDER-PERMISSION`, `CF-SECRET-ABSENCE`, and `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, recording
candidate/build/manifest/host/posture/fixture/output digests. No test may infer a universal sandbox
or undeclared network posture.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer verifies exact candidate, Codex build/protocol, manifest, activation-bound posture,
host fingerprint, credentials class proof, real-suite metadata and raw redacted observations. Any
substitution/stale proof, inferred sandbox assertion, role ambiguity, or provider-enabled fallback
fails. Exact acceptance is separate from final merged/CI landing proof.

blast radius is the session mechanism. Any new build/posture requires renewed evidence, not inference.
