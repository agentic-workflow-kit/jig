# GF-043 — Serialized finalizer and target authority

## Outcome, value, and why now

Give exactly one comparator-least Accepted Story per target durable finalization grant, target-basis/anchor authority, finite refresh, and mandatory re-review after Candidate change. The authorizing `Waiting` to `Finalizing`, or retained-authority `Accepted` to `Finalizing`, Transition records the exact policy-selected verification intent. Under posture `deterministic`, a subject-matching passing observation for every policy-selected required check class must form a complete set inside `Finalizing` before any target-changing `OPC-DEL-*`; under posture `none`, the entry records the explicit no-op and dispatches no verification. It needs registry, recovery, capacity/bounds, acceptance, and GF-042 verify semantics; it never publishes, merges, lands, or releases dependencies, which sibling stories own.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

Concurrency/finalization owns one target authority, comparator, and the catalogued authority release/recovery prerequisites for rework, reconciled block, rejection, terminal stop, and Recovery transfer; only dependency release requires confirmed landing. Forge/landing owns lineage anchor and no effect before anchor. Listed IDs map finalizer, registry/target/grant, capacity/bounds/wakes, target facts, and CF-ORDER/CF-FENCE/CF-CAPACITY.

`CP-FINALIZER` inside `RT-CONTROLLER` uses `RT-REGISTRY` to bind `ID-AUTH` to `ID-TARGET` and
`RC-FINALIZER`; `SCH-CANDIDATE`/`ID-CAND`, `EV-WORKSPACE-FACT`, and `EV-TARGET-FACT` establish the
exact Candidate/basis. `OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE`, `EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION`, and `RP-VERIFY` bind the
finalization-entry intents and complete policy-selected required-check observation set consumed
inside `Finalizing`. `OPC-DEL-ANCHOR` is the fenced anchor operation, legal only after that complete
passing set or the explicit `none` no-op;
`FC-AUTHORITY` and
`FC-BOUND` are its authority/bound failures; `BND-REFRESH`, `BND-WAIT-CAPACITY`, and
`BND-WAIT-TARGET` are finite parked waits. `EV-WAKE-AUTHORITY`, `EV-WAKE-FINALIZATION`, `refresh`,
`target_stability`, and `finalizer_queue` are wake/progress facts only. `CF-ORDER`, `CF-FENCE`, and
`CF-CAPACITY` prove ordering, fencing, and reservation. No product route is carried. `MERGE-1`
requires independent policy-aligned evidence; `MERGE-2` reserves push, PR creation, and merge to
the runner; `MERGE-3` makes done conditions policy-bound; `MERGE-4` separates done from merged;
and `MERGE-5` requires safe Blocked-work surfacing.

Require merged current GF-012/015/031/032/040/042, proven in the separately observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment; keep approved delivery package `P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS` and the authoritative landing-equivalence record as distinct bound evidence. Changed package/basis/policy/rule/registry or an omitted/unknown posture invalidates start; no real delivery provider/merge exists.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is queue/admit, grant/rebind/release, the two legal entries into `Finalizing`, final-verification intent/no-op integration, pass/fail consumption inside `Finalizing`, target/anchor observation, fence, refresh and Candidate-changing re-review, and recovery. Exclude publication, acceptance judgment, check selection/semantics/provider realization, request/publish/merge/status/comment, content comparison/landing/dependency release, provider configuration, and retirement. Anchor is scripted authority contract only; real effect is GF-044/GF-061.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-REGISTRY is witnessed grant source; controller/CP-FINALIZER alone proposes verification-entry and target intents; scheduler supplies RC-FINALIZER; PORT-VERIFY returns GF-042 observations and PORT-DELIVERY returns scripted target facts. Inputs bind Accepted package, target/registry, comparator/reservation, Candidate/basis, explicit posture/selected check set, policy, ID-AUTH, generation/fence, and anchor. Ledger/registry write queue/grant/rebind/release/refresh/wake/invalidation before dispatch. The `Waiting` or retained-authority `Accepted` to `Finalizing` Transition records exactly one posture branch: every selected `OPC-VERIFY-EXECUTE` intent for `deterministic`, or the canonical `none` no-op with no verification Operation. A deterministic entry authorizes no `OPC-DEL-*`; only after subject-matching passing observations complete every policy-selected required check class may a self-loop authorize the next guarded delivery intent. The `none` entry may authorize the next guarded finalization intent in the same Transition because it has no check to await. Only the scoped current holder acts; no worker/reviewer/provider/projection/old holder crosses Story/target/basis/registry/generation fence.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is one witnessed current grant for the comparator-least eligible Story matching lineage and exact basis; others wait without power. Entry into `Finalizing` durably binds the exact accepted Candidate and posture. For `deterministic`, only a complete set of passing subject-matching `EV-CHECK-OBSERVATION` facts—one for every policy-selected required check class—recorded inside `Finalizing` may advance to anchor or any target-changing delivery intent; a required fail takes the cataloged rework edge and releases authority, and timeout/retry exhaustion from GF-042 directly blocks. For `none`, the entry records the explicit no-op, creates no verification Operation or observation, and may advance only when every remaining non-verification guard holds. A Candidate-changing refresh retains the Story's ownership and deterministic wait position, mints a new `ID-CAND`, requires renewed full GF-040 review, then uses retained-authority `Accepted` to `Finalizing` to record a fresh mode-bound intent/no-op before atomically rebinding the same finalization authority with no unowned interval or requeue. Ordinary implementation rework releases finalization authority. Refresh, capacity, and target wait exhaustion park; effect uncertainty reconciles and indeterminate facts park. Only exact Story-bound owner reject-story yields Rejected, only direct Blocked/Rejected dependency yields Pending NotRun, and missing start/provider facts do neither. Run Stopped is overlay-only. No Land/dependency release is possible here; only confirmed GF-044 landing releases dependents.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Wrong/stale grant/anchor/check/cross target is FC-AUTHORITY, FC-FENCE, or FC-SUBJECT; bad fact FC-EVIDENCE; capacity FC-CAPACITY; expiry FC-BOUND. Record grant and the finalization-entry verification intent/no-op first. A negative required check takes rework; a mechanism timeout/loss follows GF-042's new-ID-OP `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`/`BND-RETRY` path and blocks on exhaustion, never falling through to delivery. Reobserve an uncertain anchor only after the complete policy-selected required-check set has passed or the explicit `none` no-op was recorded: same registry succeeds, another parks, and only confirmed absence plus reauthorization retries. Recovery rebuilds registry, authority, mode-bound verification state, anchor, and target before resume. A refresh retains ownership/wait position until atomic rebind and a fresh verification intent/no-op; no release is dependency release, which only landing causes. GF-046 cleans later.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate IDs/digests/order/capacity/generation/anchor against witness; unknown/stale/cross-scope fails. Target/provider fact is evidence not grant. Credentials are named provider-memory refs; redact correlation/target metadata and quarantine hostile input. Fixture is non-configurable; no adapter gains observe/authority before qualification.
