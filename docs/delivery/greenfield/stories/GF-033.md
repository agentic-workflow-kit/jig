# GF-033 — Workspace semantic contract

## Outcome, value, and why now

Define PORT-WORKSPACE plus scripted fixture for stable host, workspace, basis, cleanliness, setup receipt and preservation facts. It needs GF-015 operation certainty, GF-021 frozen setup, GF-022 manifest/proof shape and GF-031 capacity. It enables GF-039 qualification, never a configured real host.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

mechanism-and-provider-contracts.md owns port direction/qualification fence; lifecycle-catalogs.md owns five OPC-WS operations and preserve-before-retire. DR-4 bounds the mediated operation contract, not provider authority or workspace algorithm.

### Track mapping

PORT-WORKSPACE is the mediated semantic port. `CB-WORKSPACE` binds one scripted workspace path and one repository at one declared basis; setup additionally binds the frozen recipe digest, freshness-input digest, and separately authorized setup effects. `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes the exact Operation and `CP-MEDIATOR` validates its repository/path/basis/recipe/resource binding before dispatch. The binding cannot cross repository, workspace, basis, recipe, host, or Operation, and it cannot turn observe/setup authority into retire authority. DR-4 is the Engineering-plus-configuration contract choice; OPC-WS-PROVISION, OPC-WS-SETUP, OPC-WS-OBSERVE, OPC-WS-PRESERVE and OPC-WS-RETIRE record provision, frozen setup, observation, preservation and later destructive retirement; EV-WORKSPACE-FACT and EV-SETUP-FACT are validated result events; CF-SETUP-FRESHNESS and CF-MECH-WORKSPACE prove receipts and contract. Product routes: PC-JIG-8 and PC-CONCEPTS-3 require binding/independence/provider-authority/secret/mechanism proof; PC-JIG-18 requires workspace mechanism plus B2 closure; PC-USE-1 requires bounds/capacity/workspace/observability. ISO-1 requires dependency-aware eligibility/isolation; ISO-2 policy-selected resolution; ISO-3 attributable blocks; ISO-4 isolated non-duplicate work.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-015 certainty, GF-021 recipe/receipt/policy digest, GF-022 scripted manifest/capability-proof shape, and GF-031 isolation reservation observed in this story's execution base/tuple. Record all predecessor commit/tree/evidence digests and fixture version. Real-host probe or unqualified provider claim is insufficient.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: schemas, identity bindings, freshness/no-op, destructive ambiguity containment and scripted faults. Exclude local Git host (GF-039), remote/Windows, sessions, candidates/delivery and real cleanup config. RETIRE semantics exist but no real operation enables.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

CP-MEDIATOR records SCH-OPERATION then calls PORT-WORKSPACE. Scripted output binds the workspace/host identity field in SCH-SETUP-RECEIPT and the CB-WORKSPACE resource/path binding, plus basis, cleanliness, recipe/input/host fingerprints, receipt and preservation; it uses catalogued ID-OP, ID-MANIFEST, ID-STORY and ID-CAND rather than an invented workspace ID. RT-CONTROLLER validates/writes workspace/setup/preserved events to witnessed ledger. Fenced transition alone dispatches; adapter fact cannot transition, authorize session or mint capability. Operation/manifest binding fences all actors.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Scripted provision/observe/setup returns stable exact facts; fresh exact receipt is setup no-op, stale receipt authorizes one setup intent; preservation precedes retire. Invalid, dirty, ambiguous, destructive or unverifiable fact parks/blocks and preserves. It cannot accept, land, release or silently retire; no concrete provider configures.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Bad identity/basis FC-INPUT/FC-SUBJECT; stale FC-FENCE; adapter failure FC-MECHANISM; uncertain operation FC-EFFECT with ID-OP. Intent precedes call; retry only confirmed absence plus reauthorization, otherwise reconcile/park/preserve. Recovery reads operation/fact identity. Cleanup uncertainty retains workspace; GF-046 owns actual retire.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical/limited values, digest/basis/host/build, cleanliness and receipt lineage. Credential names resolve only in provider memory; secrets/secret paths never ledger/evidence/export. Posture is exact provider claim, not inference. Unknown/stale/self-attested/ambiguous facts fail closed; scripted adapter is test-only/unconfigurable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver port/schema, five bindings, scripted adapter/fault fixtures, freshness/preservation oracle and evidence metadata. Engineering plus configuration own DR-4 mediation; operation identity, record-before-effect, exact manifest and preservation are immutable. Ambient filesystem authority/inferred freshness rejected; widening/change is OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED for Arye Kogan; blast radius GF-039/later workspace work.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test stable replay, wrong host/basis/cleanliness/receipt, stale/fresh setup, lost/uncertain/duplicate operation, preservation-before-retire, crash/recovery, and cross-repository/path/basis/recipe/host/Operation binding authority negatives. Record fixture/manifest/request/result/operation/candidate/tree digests, witness and runner. CF-MECH-WORKSPACE/CF-SETUP-FRESHNESS required; real provider gate absent/configuration off.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer verifies exact contract/fixture/evidence and absence of real config. Evidence verdict only: GF-040 acceptance and GF-044 landing unavailable. Partial fixture, stale manifest or self-review fails closed.
