# Wave 4b — decision log

Records `D-###` dispositions from framing and design-review for this wave (the four PROVIDER parts:
`w4-s5` agent, `w4-s6` execution-host, `w4-s7` forge, `w4-s8` work-source). D-001..D-006 are the
coordinator's frame-gate dispositions over the four per-part `InputResolution` reports; the Disposition
column names the resolved choice and the Rationale gives the source evidence. Later `D-###` entries
record `review-technical-design` (build-time QA) suggestion dispositions (`fix`/`reject`/`defer`) raised
over this wave's scaffold. D-### IDs are wave-scoped: a reference to another wave's decision names the
wave (e.g. "Wave 3's D-002", "Wave 4a's D-005"), never a bare `D-###`.

Wave 4b is framed **per part** (four frames in `frames/`), the second commit unit of Wave 4 after
`wave-4a-core/` (committed). Its frames consume Wave 4a's committed output as their frame-time
contract — especially `w4-s1`'s records/evidence surface and `w4-s2`'s evidence/attestation model —
which is why 4b could not be one unit with 4a.

## Design targets resolved (coordinator)

Following D-001 (deepen in place, shared file), each story's `design_targets` resolves to the same file,
one section each; a future `author-technical-design` session may relocate via its own `DocStructurePlan`
(the open split question, D-006), so these are proposed homes, not frozen paths:

- **`w4-s5-agent-provider`** → `docs/design/contracts/providers.md` (Agent section) — deepen: the
  stub-vs-real-driver posture, the structural no-privileged-method guarantee (`INV-002`, cited), the
  attestation-claim shape supplied to `w4-s2`/`w4-s3`.
- **`w4-s6-execution-host-provider`** → `docs/design/contracts/providers.md` (Execution host section) —
  deepen: the containment-proof discipline (SEC-2), isolation-strength categories, honest reporting
  (DRIVE-3), the host's ISO-4 contribution. `tactical-ddd` depth (D-002).
- **`w4-s7-forge-provider`** → `docs/design/contracts/providers.md` (Forge section) — deepen: the
  runner-exclusive push/PR/merge adapter, respect for the forge's own protection/queue rules, the
  mechanical block-surfacing act (MERGE-5).
- **`w4-s8-work-source-provider`** → `docs/design/contracts/providers.md` (Work source section) —
  deepen: the candidate origination/provenance surface and the never-bypasses-plan discipline (candidate
  invariant, flagged for U9 dedup with Wave 3).

never edited here.

## Safe assumptions confirmed

The frames' `safe assumption` items were confirmed by the coordinator and carry into the story briefs
unchanged:

- **ISO-4 ownership split** (s6, confirmed): `w4-s6` owns the execution host's structural
  isolated-workspace-per-run mechanism; Wave 2 (cited, unchanged) owns the scheduling/eligibility
  discipline around it.
- **MERGE-5 block-surfacing split** (s7, confirmed): `w4-s7` owns the mechanical act of surfacing a block
  through the forge (open/update PR, post status, write failure-reasons comment); Wave 2 (cited) owns the
  `blocked` state transition and its guard; the cannot-safely-push fallback is a Records/`w4-s1` concern.
- **Provenance/import-sync named at shape level only** (s8, confirmed): `w4-s8` names the seam's shape (a
  work source may supply candidate items and/or provenance, folded into the authored plan) without
  designing any concrete integration, format, or sync cadence — a further-deferred extension point.
- **Author-time contention, not dependency** (all four, confirmed): `depends_on: []`; the shared file is
  contention whose serialize-vs-parallel resolution is contingent on the open split question (D-006).
