# GF-045 — Block and held-integration surfacing

## Outcome, value, and why now

Surface a durable Blocked or held integration once through an authorized stable marker and durable notice/obligation basis, without release. GF-038 supplies obligations and GF-044 delivery semantics. It is not a notification UI, acceptance, landing, or real provider story.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

Failure/liveness owns bounded containment and preservation; operations/observability owns durable obligation/read-model basis; forge/landing distinguishes post-acceptance delivery markers from D15 blocked review markers and states surfacing never rewrites business outcome. CF-BLOCK-SURFACING is the oracle.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-038 obligation state and GF-044 operation/reconciliation evidence. Freeze exact Blocked/held fact, Candidate/request/ref where present, authorized operation/fence, stable marker context, accountable owner, completion criteria, and deadline. No safe authority/provider means record the same obligation, not an invented publication claim.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is idempotent one-marker status/comment surfacing and fact-to-obligation/notice source transition. Exclude changing Blocked, accept/finalize/merge/land/release, notice delivery channel, review publication creation, retirement, or provider admission.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

Controller authorizes exact status/comment through PORT-DELIVERY under existing review or final-delivery scope; ledger writes block root, marker fact, and ID-OBLIGATION. Inputs are exact Story outcome, authority/fence, request identity and stable marker. Provider can attest/update marker only; it cannot alter state, resolve obligation, or acquire target/acceptance power. Projections observe.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is one deduplicated visible marker tied to exact Blocked/held fact plus durable obligation and actionable source fact. Marker failure preserves Blocked and no dependent releases; it remains a finite residual duty. Repeated events update same marker, not append duplicates. Blocked, Rejected, NotRun, Landed, and retirement remain non-substitutable outcomes.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Intent precedes status/comment. Lost or uncertain marker reconciles by request identity plus stable context; retry only after confirmed absence/reauthorization. FC-EFFECT/MECHANISM/SUBJECT/AUTHORITY/BND fail closed; BND-WAIT-TARGET bounds held integration. Recovery rereads marker and obligation; exhaustion preserves outcome/resource and records overdue obligation. GF-046 cleans later.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate exact outcome/request/fence/marker, bounded redacted explanation, fresh provider fact, and no secret URL/token. Credentials stay named/in memory. Hostile provider text quarantines. Provider/marker cannot widen authority; scripted fixture stays unreachable.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver marker idempotency reducer, obligation/source-fact binding, authorized delivery contract, scripted fixture, read-model fields, and CF catalog. DR-4 selects typed edge port with contract proof/unavailable fallback; no channel/provider algorithm is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Unit/schema marker/obligation; contract exact scope and dedup; negative authority attempts release/state rewrite/merge; adversarial stale/wrong request, duplicate marker, secret text; replay/crash/recovery failures; scripted E2E Blocked/held preserves no release. Run CF-BLOCK-SURFACING and delivery subset with exact CER.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review freezes Blocked/held subject, authority, marker context, provider facts, obligation criteria/deadline, recovery and CF evidence. PASS confirms surfacing is idempotent and non-substitutive, not that a provider is qualified or work landed.

## DR choices

DR-4 Engineering plus configuration typed PORT-DELIVERY, exact scope/fence suite, unavailable fallback. No DR-5 provider selection; GF-061 owns it.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

M; one Blocked-to-marker-and-obligation oracle. Paths status/comment guards, obligation projection, fixture/tests/catalog. Exclude notice channel/GF-052 and retirement/GF-046; no split unless one half remains disabled.

## Definition of Ready

- Named predecessors are merged/current on selected baseline; exact subject evidence is frozen.
- IDs, port, principal/fence, durable facts, FC failures, finite BND bounds, recovery, oracle, DR, and PR boundary have independent readiness review.
- No unqualified provider/effect path is reachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Immutable inputs validate and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every effect; uncertain effects reconcile by stable identity and retry only after confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization.
- Exact unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash/timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence passes; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
