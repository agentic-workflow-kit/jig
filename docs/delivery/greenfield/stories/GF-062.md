# GF-062 — Supported local reference profile and closure

## Outcome, value, and why now

Evaluate two bounded closure results. First, `CF-GATE-PRODUCT` is exactly the deterministic
conjunction of all 39 fixed suite results plus every named suite, oracle, and cited governance record
in the 44 settled product proof routes. Second, the broader supported-profile claim requires a
separate current matrix-plus-suite disposition/coverage audit of all 56 imported IDs and selected
provider/profile evidence.
Neither audit category becomes another product-gate input. This is a closure story, not implementation
source or new behavior; it cannot waive, infer, substitute, or add semantics.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`architecture-conformance.md` owns `CF-GATE-REALIZATION`, `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, and
`CF-GATE-PRODUCT`; `product-readiness-gate-record.md` fixes the 44 route identities/settled route
composition and 56 imported commitments. The 39 suite names are the fixed inventory in delivery
verification. `DR-1/2/3/4/5/6/7/8/9/12` are consumed as already-recorded owner choices, not reopened;
the gate carries their manifest/evidence inputs and has no authority to choose a new option.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Before closure execution, bind exact pre-verdict candidate identity `Q`, full external review record
`R`, approved package tuple `P = Q + durable R identifier + PASS`, and the authoritative landing-
equivalence record. Separately resolve the execution-base ref/commit/tree and prove it contains all
47 listed predecessors; require independent exact-candidate implementation review, repository/CI
evidence, and current manifest bindings. Product-gate start evidence is the immutable 44-route
manifest, every named route element/cited governance record, and the full 39-suite catalog/version.
Separately collect the 56-ID import disposition matrix, realization
build/tree/config digest, selected host/profile/environment fingerprint, and current provider-gate
evidence for GF-020, GF-025, GF-026, GF-039, GF-047, GF-057 review publication, GF-060 Codex
sessions, and GF-061 final delivery, plus every DR qualification for the broader profile audit. A missing,
stale, duplicate, altered, or substituted required record makes the relevant result fail closed;
profile unsupported does not redefine the product gate's fixed inputs.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope is deterministic collection, validation, conjunction evaluation, immutable closure evidence,
and supported-profile admission/withdrawal. Excluded are new product semantics, route minimization
or re-derivation, suite changes/waivers, implementation repair, provider fallback, broader platform
support, automatic owner decision, or a claim that a passing old candidate covers a new candidate.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

The conformance harness from GF-004 reads evidence through existing private runtime/provider
boundaries and emits an exact Candidate Evidence Record (CER), route, suite, and separate import/
profile manifests. It deterministically emits a product-gate verdict from the 39 suite results plus
every named 44-route element/cited governance record, then emits a distinct broader profile-audit
result from the import disposition matrix and profile/provider evidence. It neither writes lifecycle
facts nor dispatches product effects. Durable evidence is authoritative for these evaluations; source
ledger/artifact/provider facts remain authoritative for their domains. Only the evaluator calculates
the conjunction; owner retains DR/product decisions and providers merely supply qualified evidence.
The admission fence disables a real provider/profile until its own required provider/profile proof
passes; that proof is not silently added to the product-gate input set.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is one fully bound `CF-GATE-PRODUCT` pass where all 39 fixed suite results pass and every
named element/governance record in all 44 settled routes passes or holds. The 56 import mappings and
provider/profile evidence are separately current for the broader supported-profile claim, not extra
gate operands. `CF-GATE-REALIZATION` and each `CF-GATE-PROVIDER` remain their own admission results,
not substitutes for a missing suite/route element. Any false/missing/stale/ambiguous required record
fails the result it belongs to, reports exact identities, and never infers `Landed`, acceptance, or
a product outcome. The product gate's terminal result is pass/fail for its exact CER; the profile
audit's result is supported/unsupported for its separately bound evidence.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Malformed, unknown, noncanonical, oversized, range, version, or duplicate route/suite/import/profile
manifest field is `FC-INPUT`; wrong route, suite, import, provider-qualification story, or profile
subject is `FC-SUBJECT`; stale candidate/configuration fence is `FC-FENCE`; invalid evidence
digest/signature is `FC-EVIDENCE`;
unverifiable witness provenance is `FC-TRUST`. An actually unavailable or timed-out harness,
storage dependency, provider dependency, or pre-Run mediated exchange remains `FC-MECHANISM`
under `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` in its owning story. Missing or stale evidence, including an elapsed
freshness requirement, is instead a fail-closed evaluator input for the exact product/profile
result; it is not `FC-BOUND`, does not create a wait, and cannot be retried into validity. GF-062
consumes the current results of all eight provider gates without choosing provider defaults,
resetting an owning bound, or synthesizing wake/exhaustion behavior. Evaluation is effect-free: no
same-effect retry exists; rerun is a new exact observation after the affected product-gate or
profile-audit input is repaired/requalified. On interruption rebuild the CER from immutable sources
and reject partial output; fence stale profile/config results. Cleanup may remove derived cache but
preserves CER/manifests/failures and cannot re-enable a profile. Resume recomputes the product
conjunction and profile audit separately against their current exact inputs.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical manifests, uniqueness/counts, signature/digest/freshness, the current observed
execution-base ref/commit/tree, host/profile configuration, credential-class attestations, and no secret fields. Harness
resolves named test/provider credentials only in memory; CER/log/export carries redacted identifiers,
digests, and safe environment fingerprint, never tokens/paths/prompts. No individual suite, provider,
route, fallback, or local profile flag can widen authority. A provider becomes reachable only under
its own current qualification/admission proof; a product gate passes only on its 39-suite-plus-44-route
conjunction and does not absorb unstated provider/profile inputs.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver immutable manifest schemas/validator, a deterministic 39-suite-plus-44-route product
conjunction evaluator, CER bundle format, a separate 56-ID matrix-plus-suite disposition/coverage
auditor, profile admission fence, reproducible local runner, failure report, and exact evidence recorder. The
applicable DR records remain selected by Arye in predecessor stories: their constraints/evidence/
fallbacks are profile-audit checks; a missing choice is `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`, not a default. No
algorithm may reinterpret routes, imports, suite outcomes, provider qualification, or product meaning.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test 39-suite manifest exactness; all 44-route named-element/governance-record completeness; separate
56-import uniqueness/count/disposition coverage; exact presence and current-subject binding of GF-020,
GF-025, GF-026, GF-039, GF-047, GF-057, GF-060, and GF-061 provider evidence; disjoint GF-057
review-publication and GF-061 final-delivery bindings; each missing/duplicate/stale/substituted/digest-
mismatch failure; profile/provider/realization mutation invalidation; fresh CER rebuild; signature/
redaction/secret absence; cache/crash/resume; and full supported local profile smoke through land/
reject/block/park/suspend/stop/resume/swap/export/cleanup and stated crash boundaries. Require all 39
named suite outputs and `CF-GATE-PRODUCT` for the product verdict; require `CF-GATE-REALIZATION`,
individual `CF-GATE-PROVIDER`, and profile evidence only for their separate admission/coverage result,
all with exact build/tree/manifest/config/host/provider/output digests. No provider category is
omitted from the profile claim; it is simply not an unstated product-gate operand.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer receives the complete exact CER: content/target/build/tree/config/profile/host
digests; 39 suite results; ordered 44 routes with every named element/governance record; ordered 56
import dispositions; DR/provider manifests; separate current GF-020/GF-025/GF-026/GF-039/GF-047/
GF-057/GF-060/GF-061 qualifications, including disjoint review-publication and final-delivery
subjects; raw redacted evidence references; and freshness timestamps.
They independently recompute the product conjunction and separately audit profile/import coverage,
requiring no missing/failing/stale/substituted element in either result. Review binds each verdict to
that CER; authoritative landing is separate merged commit/CI proof and any byte/input change requires
a new CER and review.

## DR choices

Carried implementation owners are Engineering for DR-1/2/3/6/7/8, Engineering + configuration for
DR-4/12, Configuration for DR-5, and Configuration/policy for DR-9. Arye approves recorded plans
and alone authorizes widening. Each selected option/constraint/evidence/fallback from its owning story
is a profile-admission input; alternatives are not reconsidered; missing/changed choice disables the
profile and requires owner decision. The product gate has no independent DR and no permission to
synthesize a default or add an unstated input.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L, one deterministic product-gate evaluator plus its separate supported-profile coverage audit.
Expected harness/manifest/profile/evidence/test paths; exclude new runtime/provider semantics. Do not
split the 39-suite-plus-44-route evaluator; partial product passes have no gate meaning. Independently
failing provider qualification remains in GF-020, GF-025, GF-026, GF-039, GF-047, GF-057, GF-060,
or GF-061 and supports profile admission only. GF-057 review publication and GF-061 final delivery
must remain separately qualified.

## Definition of Ready

- Full mutation, crash/rebuild, secret, all-eight-provider, real-profile smoke, product-gate, and
  separate coverage-audit evidence passes on one CER; GF-057 review publication cannot substitute
  for GF-061 final delivery or vice versa.
- Independent exact-CER review, `git diff --check`, repository checks, CI, and separate authoritative landing proof pass.
