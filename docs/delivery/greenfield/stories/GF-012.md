# GF-012 — Witnessed target-authority registry

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver a registry-first cross-Run arbiter for canonical targets under the same scripted, unapproved, logically separate control and fault-surface witness model as GF-010: deterministic waiter, withdrawal, grant, release, and atomic-rebind records. It lets later finalization safely serialize work but does not itself touch a target, accept a Candidate, or dispatch an effect.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`concurrency-and-finalization.md` fixes comparator and one target holder; persistence fixes registry-first recovery and variants. `RT-REGISTRY`, `RT-WITNESS`, and `PORT-LEDGER` carry passive witnessed authority; `ID-REGISTRY`, `ID-TARGET`, and `ID-AUTH` bind realization, target, and ordinal fence; `SCH-REGISTRY-RECORD` persists waiter/grant/rebind/release. `FC-AUTHORITY` and `FC-TRUST` fail closed, while `CF-ORDER`, `CF-TRUST-STOP`, and scripted `CF-MECH-LEDGER` are the exact oracles.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Start only after GF-005 exact comparator/authority-kernel evidence and GF-010 exact ledger/witness conformance pass are observed in this story's execution base/tuple. Record canonical target/comparator vectors, the registry realization descriptor, expected `ID-REGISTRY`, and an explicit scripted, unapproved registry-witness fixture binding that has a logically separate control and fault surface from the registry state. It is not an independently configured real witness/provider topology; that qualification is reserved for GF-025. A copied label, Run-ledger mirror, mutable queue, unreviewed registry branch, or configured provider is not evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: host-scoped registry records and witnessed conditional serialization. Exclude finalizer scheduling, Candidate acceptance/review, target observation/effects, capacity implementation, ordinary Run ledger authority, registry provider configurability, and policy changes. Registry has no global scheduler and is unreachable from target dispatch.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`RT-REGISTRY` is the one passive cross-Run authority record; `CP-FINALIZER` is its future proposer, while this story exposes no finalizer effect. `PORT-LEDGER` conditionally appends `SCH-REGISTRY-RECORD` with registry/target IDs, expected head/digest, position, predecessor digest, source Run/Story, and variant content digest. Persist waiter (exact Candidate/basis/eligibility/comparator and continuous `BND-WAIT-CAPACITY` start), withdrawal, grant/fence, release reconciliation proof, and atomic-rebind old/new bindings. Only registry commits allocate current `ID-AUTH`; Run ledgers mirror audit facts only. Registry/witness/adapters cannot decide acceptance, mutate targets, or mint a grant absent comparator-least current eligible waiter.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is a witness-covered registry head whose granted authority is exactly the least eligible unwithdrawn waiter and whose rebind has no observable unowned interval. Outputs are durable registry facts and audit mirror candidates; no `Landed`, `Blocked`, `Parked`, `Rejected`, `NotRun`, or target effect is produced. Conflict/stale eligibility remains ungranted/re-read; trust failure is `FC-TRUST` shared stop, not a winner; capacity exhaustion is future finalizer-owned `Parked` behavior.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Reject malformed/noncanonical IDs/descriptors/records as `FC-INPUT`/`FC-SUBJECT`, stale head/fence as `FC-FENCE`, invalid comparator/eligibility or duplicate authority as `FC-AUTHORITY`, and fork/rollback/missing/mismatched witness or registry/Run-mirror disagreement as `FC-TRUST`. `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` bounds append/read then enters Recovery; `BND-WAIT-CAPACITY` starts continuously at recorded waiter and is never reset by recomputation (future exhaustion parks, no grant). Conditional contention re-reads head and recomputes bytes, never blindly repeats uncertain target semantics. Recovery verifies registry/witness before a mirror; registry wins disagreements and mirror repairs only as audit. Preserve all registry/witness records; no release, target cleanup, or retry of a target effect is permitted. Resume/actual authority use is GF-043.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate exact target canonicalization, realization descriptor, immutable comparator/basis, record limits, and the scripted fixture binding. The fixture has no credentials or configurable provider path; redact locators/tokens if a later provider-qualified path supplies them. Its logically separate control and fault surface must not share registry state, but it cannot attest a separate device, deployment path, or remote service. Independently configured real witness/provider topology qualification is reserved for GF-025. An unqualified adapter cannot be configured, and a provider output or Run mirror cannot widen grant authority. Missing, stale, ambiguous, self-attested, or non-independent fixture proof rejects/fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver registry semantics, scripted concurrency/crash fixtures, and exact `CF-ORDER`/`CF-MECH-LEDGER` evidence at responsibility seams. Within D10/DR-2's delegation, Engineering may realize this Phase 1 story's bounded package/module layout, provided the six-unit/eleven-port single-authority graph and this story's bounded oracle remain proven; only an authority or topology change outside that delegation requires `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`. **DR-3:** owner Engineering; queue technique may vary only if comparator, records, and serial order stay fixed; fallback is no grant. **DR-6:** owner Engineering; storage technique remains semantic-contract bounded; fallback is provider unreachable/trust stop. Any realization requires an explicit current owner or named-delegate implementation request; Arye decides only constraint widening. GF-025 owns any real storage provider qualification.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test canonical descriptor/target vectors; concurrent conditional waiter/grant/withdrawal races; stale eligibility; same-target ordering permutations; duplicate/replayed grant; release structural/reconciliation proof; atomic rebind; crashes before/after flush/witness/ack; lost ack/readback; rollback/fork/witness mismatch; registry-first mirror repair; and the scripted, unapproved registry-witness fixture's logical control/fault-surface separation. `CF-ORDER` oracle checks comparator leastness/non-preemption; `CF-MECH-LEDGER` checks append/witness rules under that same GF-010 scripted fixture model. Record exact candidate, registry/target/basis IDs/digests, build/manifest/environment, fixture-isolation/fault-surface metadata, probes, fault points and results. Do not record real witness/provider-topology qualification here; provider and E2E delivery tests are inapplicable/unreachable.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review requires the complete candidate digest, registry contract/realization binding, race/crash corpus, exact two-suite evidence, scripted registry-witness fixture binding with logical-control/fault-surface separation, and CI/check metadata. The evidence must not claim an independently configured real witness/provider topology. Findings, self-review, partial candidates, stale registry identity, or evidence bound to another descriptor fail closed. Acceptance is not a target landing; merge/CI on the exact landing commit is separate proof.

## DR choices

`DR-2` is the applicable bounded D10 package/module-layout choice; its exact realization must be bound by an explicit current owner or named-delegate implementation request, and recording its applicability does not itself activate a provider/effect or authorize an authority-topology change. DR-3 and DR-6 are instantiated above; their fallback is no authority/recovery stop. No unrecorded registry algorithm or provider choice is authorized.
