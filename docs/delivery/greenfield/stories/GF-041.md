# GF-041 — Pre-acceptance review publication

## Outcome, value, and why now

Before package assembly or reviewer assignment, produce the one typed fixed review-publication observation selected by the frozen review mode. When that governing mode requires a forge venue, publish the exact Candidate on a dedicated review ref and draft non-mergeable request with stable status/comment markers; when it does not, record the canonical explicit-absence observation and dispatch nothing. GF-015 fences effects and GF-035 supplies Candidate identity. The result is a fixed input to GF-040, not acceptance, finalization, target, merge, landing, dependency release, or real-provider configuration; GF-057 alone qualifies real review publication.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

D15 authorizes the bounded review capability. Forge and landing defines `OPC-REV-PUBLISH`,
`OPC-REV-REQUEST`, `OPC-REV-STATUS`, `OPC-REV-COMMENT`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-REF`,
`OPC-REV-RETIRE-REQUEST`, `OPC-REV-RETIRE-STATUS`, and `OPC-REV-RETIRE-COMMENT`; all are review
operations and forbid target lineage, target mutation, ID-AUTH, merge, and final-delivery
operations. Review execution binds either the fixed observed venue tuple or its canonical
explicit-absence value into RP-PACKAGE before reviewer assignment.
CF-REVIEW-PUBLICATION and the review subset of CF-MECH-DELIVERY are the proof oracle.

Mechanism/provider contracts owns `CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`: one repository, exact Candidate
content/basis, dedicated review ref, draft non-mergeable request identity/marker, stable
status/comment marker, and redacted explanation digest. `RT-CONTROLLER` authorizes the exact D15
Operation and `CP-MEDIATOR` validates that complete subject/resource/capability binding before
dispatch and fact adoption. It cannot cross repository, Candidate, basis, ref, request, marker,
generation, or Operation; cannot contain `ID-AUTH`; and cannot admit anchor, target mutation,
merge, landing, any `OPC-DEL-*`, or another capability binding. Scheduling owns
`BND-WAIT-MECHANISM`, `BND-RETRY`, and `BND-RECOVERY`, separating confirmed mechanism absence from
uncertain review effects.

`RT-CONTROLLER` writes the authorized review transition; `PORT-DELIVERY` is the sole effect edge;
`EV-TARGET-FACT` is the redacted attested ref/request/marker fact; and `FC-EFFECT` identifies an
uncertain or contradictory publication effect. `PC-JIG-11` and `PC-CONCEPTS-6` require this
review-publication capability without landing power. `MERGE-1` requires independent
policy-aligned evidence; `MERGE-2` reserves push, PR creation, and merge to the runner; `MERGE-3`
makes done conditions policy-bound; `MERGE-4` separates done from merged; and `MERGE-5` requires
safe Blocked-work surfacing in the draft venue.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Freeze merged GF-015/035 evidence; explicitly authorized phase/story scope; current product/design/track provenance; the separately observed execution-base ref, commit, tree, and predecessor containment; Candidate and target-basis digests; and the frozen review mode. When that mode requires a forge venue, also freeze canonical ref/request/marker names, capability-manifest digest, and Transition, ID-OP, and fence per operation. The only publication mechanism is scripted; a mode that does not require a venue admits only the canonical explicit-absence observation and no dispatch. Missing/stale capability, an omitted or unknown review mode, or a changed Candidate fails closed rather than blindly creating or recreating a request.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: derive the mode-selected typed fixed observation; for a required forge venue, publish/update the exact review ref, draft request, stable status/comment, readback/reconciliation, and preservation-safe review-venue retirement; otherwise persist canonical explicit absence without dispatch. Exclude reviewer assignment and verdict, acceptance, verification, finalizer/anchor, all final delivery, provider setup, and notice UI. A blocked Candidate may be surfaced but publication cannot change its outcome.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

For a venue-requiring mode, RT-CONTROLLER and CP-MEDIATOR record intent, validate the complete `CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`, then invoke PORT-DELIVERY; provider only attests. Inputs are the exact SCH-CANDIDATE, frozen review mode, Transition, ID-OP, capability binding, redacted correlation keys, and fixed observation tuple. Ledger writes the mode, ref, draft request, markers, effect certainty, and observation digest. For a mode that does not require a venue, the controller writes the canonical explicit-absence value and digest with no Operation, port dispatch, provider fact, correlation key, venue identity, mechanism wait, retry, or recovery observation. Only the controller plus a D15-authorized Transition may dispatch in the required-venue branch; provider/reviewer/worker/projection cannot mint verdict, ID-AUTH, target authority, delivery authority, or reuse the binding for another subject/resource.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is exactly one mode-consistent durable observation before GF-040 package assembly: either the exact digest on a dedicated ref, an attested draft/non-mergeable request, and one stable marker context, or the canonical explicit-absence value and digest with proof that no review Operation dispatched and no venue exists. Story remains non-Accepted/non-Landed. Effect uncertainty in the required-venue branch reconciles and indeterminate publication parks; a required review path that cannot complete after its bounded attempts blocks. Only a direct Blocked/Rejected dependency makes Pending `NotRun`; only the exact Story-bound owner reject-story action makes `Rejected`; missing provider/start facts do neither. Run Stopped is an overlay. Retirement removes an existing venue only after preservation; the explicit-absence branch has no retirement effect. Neither branch releases dependents or manufactures outcome.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Wrong subject/capability or a review-mode/observation mismatch yields `FC-SUBJECT` or
`FC-AUTHORITY`; invalid mechanism fact is `FC-MECHANISM`; and a lost, late, contradictory, or
unverifiable result after dispatch is `FC-EFFECT`. In the required-venue branch, record exact
Operation intent and deadline first. `CP-MEDIATOR` starts `BND-WAIT-MECHANISM` with that durable
request; its 15-minute default (range 5 seconds–2 hours) never renews and wakes only on a validated
result, confirmed mechanism failure, or effect-certainty observation. A confirmed mechanism
failure/timeout may consume the next `BND-RETRY` ordinal only after exact ref/request/marker
observation proves absence and the controller records reauthorization. `BND-RETRY` has three
attempts by default (range 1–5), records every ordinal, never resets, and exhaustion with confirmed
absence and no effect uncertainty directly blocks the owning Story/Operation.

An `FC-EFFECT` result retains the exact Operation/fence and starts `BND-RECOVERY` under
`CP-RECOVERY`; it is not a mechanism retry. Reconcile publish/request/status/comment by exact
ref-content digest or stable marker. The recovery bound has three observations by default (range
1–5), never renews, wakes only on `EV-RECOVERY-OBSERVATION` or `EV-EFFECT-CERTAINTY`, and permits no
semantic-effect retry. Exhaustion parks/escalates while retaining the fence and review resource.
`BND-RETIRE` applies only to retirement; its expiry retains an existing venue and creates a GF-046
obligation. In the explicit-absence branch, any dispatch or returned venue/correlation field fails
closed and no mechanism/retry/recovery bound starts. Recovery rebuilds the same branch and fixed
observation from durable mode/correlation facts.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical bounded Candidate/ref/target/marker/fence, draft posture, freshness, and response subject. Credential names resolve only in provider memory; ledger holds redacted non-secret correlation keys, never tokens or URLs. Bound/redact text and quarantine hostile output. Fixture has no credentials/configuration path; provider success cannot approve, finalize, land, or widen scope.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver D15 typed operation contracts, immutable observation tuple/digest, intent/reconciliation guards, scripted delivery fixture, redaction/marker rules, and CF catalog. DR-4 selects typed semantic port and edge transport with mechanism evidence/unavailable fallback. DR-5 defers GitHub review-publication selection to GF-057; no mergeability or final-delivery algorithm is delegated.

## Test and evidence obligations

Unit/schema tests cover all eight operations, the required-venue tuple, canonical explicit absence, and markers. Contract tests prove exact subject/fence and fixed observation before package assembly or reviewer assignment. Negative-authority tests attempt merge, anchor, verdict, release, assignment before observation, every cross-repository/Candidate/basis/ref/request/marker/generation/Operation binding, every `OPC-DEL-*`, dispatch in the explicit-absence branch, and omission of required publication. Adversarial tests cover non-draft request, wrong digest, stale correlation, duplicate markers, secret output, unknown/omitted mode, and branch/tuple mismatch. Replay/crash/timeout tests prove durable intent, each `BND-RETRY` ordinal only after confirmed absence, direct block on retry exhaustion without uncertainty, `FC-EFFECT` entry for every lost post-dispatch result, no semantic retry through every `BND-RECOVERY` observation, retained fence/resource plus park/escalation on recovery exhaustion, no bound consumption in the explicit-absence branch, and retirement-only `BND-RETIRE`. Scripted E2E proves both required-venue publication and no-venue explicit absence without Accepted/Landed; record exact build/manifest/environment/fixture/CER and run both CF suites.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review freezes candidate/tree/path set, frozen review mode, and its exact fixed observation: for the required-venue branch, Candidate/basis, ref/request/draft attestation, markers, ID-OP/fence/effect facts, redacted correlation, and retirement evidence; for the no-venue branch, canonical explicit absence and no-dispatch proof. DR and CF results bind the selected branch. PASS proves no acceptance/finalization/landing/configuration authority; it is neither runtime review approval nor landing proof.

## DR choices

DR-4: Engineering plus configuration, typed PORT-DELIVERY contract, exact-subject/fence proof, unavailable fallback. DR-5: Configuration, real review-publication provider only at GF-057 with current gate; no provider selection here. No other DR applies.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L; one D15 review-only authority oracle across contract, guards, fixture, tests, catalog. Exclude acceptance, verify, finalizer, real forge, retirement orchestrator. An internal semantic-fixture slice may precede GF-057 only within GF-041's single worktree, branch, continuous pair, candidate review, and integration result while green and unconfigurable. It does not create a suffixed story ID, tracker edge, separate delivery subject, or PR.

## Definition of Ready

- GF-015/035 are separately proven in the observed execution-base ref/commit/tree and predecessor containment; explicitly authorized scope and current product/design/track provenance remain distinct bound evidence.
- Governing IDs, exact `CB-REVIEW-PUBLICATION`, port, principal, fence, durable facts, and the executable mechanism-wait/retry-versus-uncertain-effect/recovery split have independent readiness review, including the no-venue no-bound branch.
- Unqualified providers/effect paths are unreachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Versioned immutable inputs validate; required-venue and canonical explicit-absence branches are exhaustive and mode-consistent; and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every effect; confirmed mechanism absence alone consumes finite retry and exhausts to direct block, while uncertain effect uses finite recovery with no semantic retry and exhausts to park/escalation with its fence retained.
- Required unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash, timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence is exact-subject-bound; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
