# GF-037 — Rule invalidation and Run control

## Outcome, value, and why now

Implement frozen-input/rule-surface revalidation and authenticated suspend, resume and terminal-stop requests. It depends on lifecycle GF-030, candidate bases GF-035 and Doorbell principal decisions GF-036. It provides no Settlement duty completion, acceptance, delivery or landing; GF-050 later realizes terminal-stop overlay settlement.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

lifecycle-catalogs.md owns EV-RULE-SURFACE-TOUCHED and three Run decision events; lifecycle flow owns Active/Parked→Suspended, Suspended→Active/Parked/Stopped, dispatch fence, retained finalizer fence and terminal-stop preserved Story states. DR-3/DR-9 do not permit new run state or timeout policy.

### Track mapping

EV-RULE-SURFACE-TOUCHED invalidates frozen safety basis; EV-RUN-SUSPEND-DECISION, EV-RUN-RESUME-DECISION and EV-RUN-TERMINAL-STOP-DECISION are authenticated exact control events; RT-CONTROLLER is writer; PORT-DECIDE supplies but cannot directly transition; EV-OWNER-DECISION binds exact responder; SCH-RULE-SURFACE freezes the basis; FC-RULES parks/requires reapproval; CF-RULE-SURFACE, CF-RUN-CONTROL and CF-FENCE prove invalidation/control/fencing. Product routes PC-JIG-1 control, PC-JIG-2 envelope/rule/fence, PC-JIG-6 control, PC-JIG-12 policy/provider authority, PC-JIG-23 control/bounds/observability, PC-JIG-25 containment/control/release, PC-CONCEPTS-7 canonical control projection, PC-CONCEPTS-8 canonical control projection, PC-USE-4 fence/control and PC-USE-7 rule/operator all apply. RESUME-1 committed progress, RESUME-2 safe checkpoint, RESUME-3 no double effect, RESUME-4 diagnosable park and RESUME-5 changed-assumption reapproval are exact commitments.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged GF-030 legal Run phases, GF-035 frozen candidate/input basis and GF-036 authenticated owner/current-delegate decision records. Start evidence includes exact rule-surface/config/setup/candidate digests, current ID-GEN, decision/grant proofs, commits/tree/evidence/CI/review. Ambient changed file, provider request or unsigned operator intent is insufficient.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope: touched-rule detection, invalidation, frozen revalidation, dispatch suspension, resumed new generation, terminal-stop request and phase-preserving overlay-open intent. Exclude actual Settlement duties/obligations, policy authoring, acceptance, delivery, retirement and automatic stop.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

RT-CONTROLLER/CP-TRANSITION validates rule-surface digest, affected subject and ID-GEN, writes touched/invalidation and decision events to witnessed ledger, and outputs only catalogued fence/revalidation/overlay intent. PORT-DECIDE supplies authenticated decision from GF-036; it never directly changes state. Controller alone suspends/resumes/stops; owner/current exact grant authorizes decision; provider/projection cannot. New generation and accepted-successor fence bind resume.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Touched frozen rule invalidates dependent evidence/authority and parks before unsafe dispatch. Suspend immediately fences dispatch, preserves Story state and retains finalization grant fenced until target-changing operations reconcile. Resume passes RC-RESUME-INTEGRITY under new generation or parks for exact reapproval. Explicit terminal stop creates one preserved-state Settlement-overlay intent, not Story outcome or settlement completion. No acceptance/Landed/release occurs.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Invalid rule/input FC-RULES/FC-INPUT; stale generation FC-FENCE; untrusted decision FC-AUTHORITY; shared truth FC-TRUST enters Recovery. Uncertain target effect retains fence and reconciles before authority release; no dispatch while suspended and no blind retry. BND-WAIT-DECISION/RECOVERY behavior is GF-032. Recovery reconstructs touched basis and decisions; cleanup/obligations deferred.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate canonical rule surface/version/digest, affected bindings, principal/grant and fence. No credential resolves; redact sensitive policy values. Provider/config fallback cannot widen authority or append terminal state. Missing/stale/ambiguous evidence fails closed.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver rule-touch schema/selectors, Run-control transitions/fences, revalidation fixtures and CF evidence. DR-3 closed transition reducer and DR-9 existing bounds are immutable; automatic stop or mutable ambient basis rejected. Gap OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED; blast radius GF-040/GF-050.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test each touched surface, invalidation/reapproval, suspend from Active/Parked, unresolved target effect, resume success/park, stale/unauthorized decision, terminal-stop intent, crash/replay/recovery. Record rule/basis/decision/fence/candidate/tree digests, witness and runner. CF-RULE-SURFACE, CF-RUN-CONTROL, CF-FENCE required.

## Exact acceptance

Independent reviewer checks exact rule/decision package and findings. Verdict remains semantic evidence only: acceptance/landing unavailable. Partial, self-authored or stale decision/basis fails closed.

## DR choices

Engineering owns DR-3 and Configuration/policy owns DR-9. New stop semantics, rule policy or automatic action is a widening/change requiring Arye Kogan's owner decision.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

Size M; coherent oracle is frozen basis plus fenced Run control. Paths: controller/schema/fixtures/CF; Settlement/provider code excluded. Do not split terminal stop from suspend/resume unless shared fence/identity remains green.
