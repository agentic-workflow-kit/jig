# GF-046 — Preservation-safe retirement

## Outcome, value, and why now

Retire sessions, workspaces, review venues, authority, artifacts, and controller resources only after preservation, without changing business outcome or release. It needs durable artifact/evidence/operation/workspace/session/obligation/publication/delivery/block facts. Terminal Stopped settlement remains GF-050.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

I18 separates outcome from Retirement and I19 requires preservation before destruction. Failure/liveness defines finite Retirement; lifecycle catalog owns retiring transitions; operations/observability owns residual duties. Listed operations/events implement preserved session/workspace/review resources; CF-SEPARATION and CF-PRESERVATION are proof.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require merged exact evidence for all dependencies and freeze preserved resource inventory, owner, outcome, authority/fence, evidence/retention/pin state, review/request identity, destruction eligibility, and BND-RETIRE. Missing or unverifiable preservation/authority never permits cleanup.

## In-scope and non-goals

Scope is duty planning, preserve/readback, safe close/retire, reconciliation, residual obligation, and recovery. Exclude decision of business outcome, acceptance, landing/release, terminal settlement, evidence disposal policy, provider admission, and destructive cleanup outside authorized retirement.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

Controller/transition engine alone authorizes retirement operations through SESSION, WORKSPACE, ARTIFACT, DELIVERY and ledger ports; ledger writes preservation receipt, duty, outcome separation and obligation. Inputs bind resource, holder, exact outcome, fence, retention/pin, and deadline. Providers attest actions only; worker/reviewer/projection cannot retire authority or rewrite outcome. Preserved facts are prerequisite to destruction.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success is durable preservation evidence and completed retirement duties while Story outcome and dependency-release history are byte-for-byte unchanged. Failed/uncertain duty retains resource and opens obligation; no cleanup can create Landed, clear Blocked, accept, or release. Rejected/NotRun/Blocked/Landed retain their recorded outcomes; suspension/resume retains duty state.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

FC-TRUST for missing preservation/witness, FC-EFFECT for uncertain destructive action, FC-AUTHORITY/FENCE/SUBJECT for scope errors, FC-BOUND at BND-RETIRE. Intent precedes effect; reconcile by stable resource/operation identity; never retry destructive effect without confirmed absence plus reauthorization. Recovery rebuilds duties/pins/receipts; timeout creates residual obligation, preserves resource, and wakes only.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Validate resource identity, holder, retention/pin, evidence integrity, fence, destructive eligibility and bounded redacted facts. Credentials remain named/in-memory. Never log secret artifacts or provider URLs; quarantine hostile receipts. Unqualified adapter cannot clean; provider result never widens cleanup/outcome authority.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver retirement duty planner/state machine, preservation/readback receipts, port guards, scripted cleanup fixtures, obligation integration, recovery matrix, and CF catalog. DR-3 is immutable transition tables with replay proof. DR-6 is witnessed storage/protected-disposable separation with FC-TRUST stop; no disposal algorithm is invented.

## Test and evidence obligations

Unit/schema duties/receipts; contract preserve-before-retire/readback; negative authority attempts outcome/release mutation; adversarial missing pin/retention/fence, false receipt, secret payload; replay/crash at every preservation/destruction edge; timeout/recovery/resume; scripted E2E for all phase-4 outcomes proves cleanup invariant. Run CF-SEPARATION/CF-PRESERVATION and applicable evidence lifecycle.

## Exact acceptance

Independent review freezes inventory, outcome/release baseline, preservation receipts, pins/retention, retirement intents/fences, obligations, recovery and CF CER. PASS requires proof that cleanup cannot alter outcome/release; it is not authorization for real destructive provider configuration.

## DR choices

DR-3 Engineering immutable duty transitions/replay proof/reject gaps. DR-6 Engineering witnessed file artifacts and protected/disposable boundary, readback evidence, FC-TRUST deliberate stop. No DR-5 provider choice.

## PR boundary, relative size, and split

L; one preservation-to-retirement separation oracle over all mechanism families. Expected planner/guards/fixtures/tests; split GF-046a common duty/preservation core from GF-046b mechanism realizations only if core is green, disabled, and keeps authoritative duty model.

## Definition of Ready

- Named predecessors are merged/current on selected baseline; exact subject evidence is frozen.
- IDs, port, principal/fence, durable facts, FC failures, finite BND bounds, recovery, oracle, DR, and PR boundary have independent readiness review.
- No unqualified provider/effect path is reachable and configuration cannot widen authority.

## Definition of Done

- Immutable inputs validate and stale, malformed, ambiguous, oversized, cross-scope, or self-attested facts fail closed.
- Intent precedes every effect; uncertain effects reconcile by stable identity and retry only after confirmed absence plus recorded reauthorization.
- Exact unit, contract, negative-authority, adversarial, replay, crash/timeout/recovery, provider-or-scripted, and E2E evidence passes; git diff --check, repository checks, CI, and independent exact-candidate review pass.
