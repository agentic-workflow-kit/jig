# GF-011 — Fenced controller replay and recovery

## Outcome, value, and why now

Deliver deterministic recovery of authoritative Run facts: a new generation fences stale control, verifies ledger/witness, replays committed records in position order, and rebuilds disposable projections. This is the earliest safe way for later Operations to restart after interruption. It enables no external effect, target authority, artifact mutation, intake, or lifecycle invention beyond GF-005's closed kernel.

## Governing paths and stable IDs

`PC-JIG-2` and `PC-USE-4` require the imported `FENCE-1`, `FENCE-2`, `FENCE-3`, `RESUME-1`, `RESUME-2`, `RESUME-3`, `RESUME-4`, and `RESUME-5` commitments: exact owner-selected posture, no worker widening/privileged credentials, durable progress, safe checkpoint resume, recognition of already-taken effects, named fail-closed stop, and changed-assumption reapproval. `state-and-recovery.md` and persistence own this story's mapping: `RT-CONTROLLER` executes fenced replay; `ID-GEN` fences stale control; `EV-RECOVERY-OBSERVATION` carries validated recovery facts; `BND-RECOVERY` bounds reconciliation; `FC-FENCE` and `FC-TRUST` stop invalid control/trust; `CF-ORDERING`, `CF-FENCE`, `CF-TRUST-STOP`, and `CF-RUN-CONTROL` are the exact suite oracles.

## Dependencies plus start evidence

Require GF-005's exact merged reducer/catalog evidence and GF-010's merged `CF-MECH-LEDGER` evidence for this configured Run ledger/witness. Verify the current baseline, a readable witnessed chain, canonical record schema/upcasters, and that no effect adapter is configured. An unreviewed projection, snapshot, or controller instance is not start evidence.

## In-scope and non-goals

In scope: conditional generation claim, stale-controller fencing, verified reconstruction, deterministic replay, optional verified snapshots/projections, and recovery observation interfaces. Exclude dispatch/retry of Operations, artifact context/pin recovery (GF-013/015), registry arbitration, suspension/resume UX, backup/disaster procedures, new transitions, and provider mechanisms.

## Runtime/product unit, ports, inputs, outputs, identities, persisted facts, authority

`CP-RECOVERY` obtains `ID-GEN` by GF-010 conditional append; `CP-TRANSITION` remains sole lifecycle writer. It consumes verified `PORT-LEDGER` records/witness head and emits reconstructed canonical state plus a replaceable `CP-PROJECTION` view; no output is authority. Persisted facts are generation claim/token, immutable ledger records, verified snapshot position/digest, and recovery observations later adopted only by the transition engine. A stale controller, snapshot, projection, or timer has no transition/dispatch power; controller generation is the fence.

## Observable success and terminal outcomes

Success reconstructs identical canonical state and decision inputs from a verified chain regardless of process/replay ordering, with a current generation and discarded/rebuilt stale projection. It does not itself deliver, block, park, reject, land, or stop a Story. Trust failure produces the deliberate shared stop (`FC-TRUST`) and no autonomous resume; indeterminate control commit remains Recovery. A verified future `Stopped` record is only outside this story's authority.

## Failure, uncertainty, timeout, retry, reconciliation, recovery, cleanup, and resume

Reject malformed/upcast-invalid records as `FC-INPUT`; stale or duplicate generation/dispatch basis as `FC-FENCE`; broken chain, fork, rollback, witness disagreement, absent required witness, or same-generation different-digest as `FC-TRUST`. `BND-WAIT-LEDGER` bounds ledger reads; its exhaustion enters Recovery. `BND-RECOVERY` (default 3, range 1–5) is reserved for each later uncertain effect: replay never treats it as permission to reissue an effect. On lost commit ack use GF-010's five-way result; confirmed absence may retry only that control commit, indeterminate halts. Restart acquires a new generation, verifies chain/witness, rebuilds projection/snapshot only after validation, then exposes pending/uncertain operations for GF-015 reconciliation. Preserve ledger/snapshots and evidence; discard only failed projections, never durable records. Resume is fenced until all required verification completes; no blind effect retry or cleanup occurs here.

## Security and trust: validation, credentials, redaction, and authority widening

Strictly validate identity/path/position/digest/schema/version sizes before replay; upcast on read without rewriting history. Recovery credentials are named and in-memory only; logs expose opaque IDs/digests, never secrets or raw provider data. Trust roots are the independent witness and canonical chain, not snapshots/backups/projections. Recovery observations are non-authoritative until validated/recorded. No adapter is reachable: all observation and effect ports remain scripted and configuration-rejected until their qualification stories pass.

## Deliverables without delegated algorithm invention

Deliver recovery/replay and replaceable-projection responsibilities, schema-upcaster and crash fixtures, and exact suite evidence; GF-001 decides package layout. **DR-3:** owner Engineering; deterministic replay/index technique may vary only while exact state/order stays fixed, with permutation evidence and reject/park fallback. **DR-6:** owner Engineering; storage access remains GF-010's semantic contract, with unavailable/trust-stop fallback. Arye decides only constraint widening or approved plan selection.

## Test and evidence obligations

Test generation races/stale process dispatch, replay permutations, duplicate/lost ack, verified and corrupt snapshots, full-chain fallback, crashes before/after claim/replay/projection, witness rollback/fork/mismatch, unknown schema/upcast rejection, and restart from every committed prefix. Oracles: `CF-FENCE` current-generation only; `CF-RUN-CONTROL` exact reconstructed control state; `CF-ORDERING` no adoption before verified commit. Record candidate digest, ledger/witness fixture identities, schema versions, exact expected state/decision digests, fault point, suite/probe versions, environment and results. Provider/E2E tests do not apply because ports are unreachable.

## Exact acceptance

An independent reviewer evaluates one candidate with source/content digest, generation/replay contract, crash fixtures, exact three-suite evidence, and check/CI metadata. Any stale, self-authored, partial, or different-ledger/witness binding fails closed. Acceptance approves the candidate only; landing requires the authoritative merged commit and CI proof separately.
